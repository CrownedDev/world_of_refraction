### B-517: the skills you pick live on what is worn (skills track S1, part 1)

### Asset overhaul plan, revision 6 (docs only)

- **Weapon average, elemental side only (§4.1):** the weapon-and-crystal average drives only the elemental part of a hit and the element bar's build-up. The physical part and the physical bar stay on the weapon's own tier. A hit with both is split: the infusion's added share reads the average.
- **Found skills follow the equipment rules (§5.3):** only slotted skills are kept, the rest go back. Out of wipes or on a quit, the slotted skills take the tier drop as worn gear does. Kept skills are for the hub and its presets, not the next run's start. Questions 10-12 are marked answered.

Plan default (Crown may change): the power reads the copy's tier too, so the drop costs about half the skill's damage (C to F).

### B-519: the built tier, rings and weapons (asset overhaul stage 2b)

- **What changed:** a piece's skills now read its **built tier**: its own tier and its socket's averaged, a half rounded down (an F ring with a B gem is D). A ring's spell slots, its spells' damage and their wear read it; the ring's stat lines stay on its own tier. A weapon's elemental side reads it (an infused hit's extra share, an infused hit's build-up, its crystal's spells); a plain hit stays on the weapon's own tier. Combat Tier counts the built tiers. A skill's power reads your own copy's tier. The Equipment detail shows "Skill tier D (F + B)".
- **Why:** D-224 and the plan's stage 2b (`Design/AssetOverhaulPlan.md` §4, §4.1).
- **Defaults taken (Q-519):** there is one status bar per target, so an infused hit's whole build-up reads the built tier and a plain hit's the weapon's (the plan spoke of two bars); a weapon crystal's wear and slot counts keep their old tiers (the plan's "keep" rows); an evolution in a ring now gives its spells a channel.
- **How it was checked:** build and the full suite (2247 passed, 0 failed), new spec `WoR.Loadout.AdapterTier`. wor-reviewer (Sonnet): added a debug readout (`wor.Combat.PrintTiers` now lists each piece's built tier), the AI's status estimate reads the copy's tier, named the average's constant, and recorded the weapon-wear choice.
