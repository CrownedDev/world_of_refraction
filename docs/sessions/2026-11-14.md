### B-435: same-element infusion sources stack (D-174)

- **What changed:** when an ability or attack is infused, every one of the character's sources with the same element joins in. Each pays its own cost: a stacked innate or evolution adds its EP, a stacked gem takes its wear, the level-2 HP backlashes add up. Status buildup gets +25% per extra source, up to +50% (`TODO: tune`). `GetSameElementSourceCount` gives the battle UI its xN. The AI's "would this kill me" check counts every source that pays HP.
- **Decided by default (Q-435, Crown may change it):** spells don't stack; a spell keeps its one source, so a free ring spell doesn't start costing innate EP. This narrows D-174 to abilities and attacks.
- **Also changed, outside D-174:** an evolution in the character's own slot used as the infusion source now pays its level-2 HP and, on abilities, its wear; it paid neither before (the cost read the weapon's attachment, not the seat).
- **Verified:** build OK; `WoR.Infusion`, `WoR.AI` and `WoR.Combat` specs green. Reviewer: nothing to fix.
- **Crown checks:** `docs/PIE/B-435.md`.
- **Not done:** the battle UI's xN; a ritual works the stack out again when it fires (`TODO`); the specs don't cover a real three-source loadout, the summed HP of two HP-paying sources, or the AI clamp; the AI prices a skill before it picks the source, so a stacked innate's EP can make its pick unaffordable (the pick is then refused at validation and the AI falls back to another action).
