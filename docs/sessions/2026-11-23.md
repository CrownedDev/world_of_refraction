## B-587: the Intel close-up (`feature/B-587-intel-closeup`, for the brain to play)
- **Why:** Crown, D-262: Intel should show the character large, with the panel on one side, as Final Fantasy XIII's Libra screen and Expedition 33's target close-up.
- **What:** opening Intel, and every tab change, cuts the fight camera to a front close-up of that tab's character (party and enemies): the target view's front shot, a quarter nearer, with the character in the middle of the left two-thirds. The Intel panel now sits on the right and is narrower (the right third of the screen); long names shrink to fit their tabs. Closing Intel, or the Status button replacing it, cuts back to the turn view (and to the picker's target view if it was open). With Camera Movement Off the camera never moves; the panel still sits on the right.
- **Decided defaults (Crown may change):** 0.75 of the target view's distance, never under 2 m; the same close-up in every turn-view style; only on your own turn view (an enemy's turn, an action's shots and cinematics hold the camera); a new turn view while Intel is open shows the close-up again.
- **How it was checked:** build; `WoR.Combat.Camera` has two new cases (the rule frames enemies and allies from the front, nearer than the target view, left of the panel, for a person and a 4 m golem; the HUD's open, tab change, close and Status button drive the camera as cuts, a repeat push doesn't cut, Movement Off keeps it still, Movement back On and a new turn show the close-up again); the full suite; wor-reviewer (its point about a turn change while Intel is open is fixed and specced).
- **Not checked:** nothing on screen: the framing in a real arena (another fighter in the way, real capsule heights), the panel's look at 720p and 1080p (`docs/PIE/B-587.md`, 10 checks, for the agent).
- **Agent QA 1 fixes (Crown: fix before merge):** an ally's close-up was a far shot because a recruit's talk sphere counted as its body: the body now leaves trigger shapes out (this also fixes a heal's target view on that ally), and the close-up picks a clear angle when another fighter stands in front. Long tab names stay full size and wrap to a second row. While Intel is open the turn order, enemy cards and corner hints hide (chosen over a smaller panel: Intel shows the enemies and its own keys, and no menu key works while it is open). A long stat name wraps instead of running into its value. Checked by build, three new spec cases, the full suite and wor-reviewer; the look needs the agent's replay at 720p and 1080p.
- **Crown's changes after QA (D-262 additions):** the tab row is gone: Intel shows one name with `◀ Q` and `E ▶` arrows either side (LB / RB on a pad) and a small Party / Enemy / You label. V / R3 now cycles: a tap opens on your party, the next jumps to the first enemy, the next closes; holding it closes. Settings > Gameplay > Intel key lets you pick Open and close instead (Cycle is the default). While Intel is open the command menu, the corner hints and the enemy cards hide; the turn order stays and the panel sits under it (Expedition 33's way). Checked by build, new spec cases (the hiding, the title, the cycle, the setting), the full suite and wor-reviewer. **For Crown to check:** the hold time (0.5 s) and that a tap acting on release feels quick enough.
- **Agent QA 2 fixes:** Intel stayed open over the enemy's "DEFEND! Guard / Dodge" prompt (the keys were blocked, so no guard) and over the Defeat screen. Now it closes by itself when a guard prompt opens for one of your party and when the fight ends (win, lose, flee). The Status button no longer opens the old plain-text list: it opens Intel on your party, and a second click closes it (one place for status info, as Expedition 33 and FFXIII; the old list's code stays until Crown's play). Checked by build, two new spec cases, the full suite and wor-reviewer.
