## B-435b: same-element stacking for spells
- **Branch:** `feature/B-435b-spell-stacking-cloud` (from `origin/dev` at `f34247a11`).
- **What changed:** an **infused** spell (L1 / L2) now uses every source of the character's that shares its cast element (Crown's Q-435 B): the origin first, the others joining. Each source pays the spell's cost as cast from it: a ring or weapon-crystal source is free, a stacked Innate or Evolution pays the spell's EP (so an infused ring Fire spell cast by a Fire Caster costs the innate's EP), and the L2 HP adds up per HP-paying source. Status buildup ×1.25 per extra source, up to ×1.5. A non-elemental spell never stacks. A Reality or Broken Darkness caster may pick Innate on any spell (was evolution spells only): the stack keys on their own element and the hit casts it. An uninfused spell is unchanged (Q-435b).
- **Files:** `Combat/Actions/ActionExecutor.cpp`, `ActionExecutorCosts.cpp`, `Public/Combat/Actions/ActionExecutor.h`, `AI/AIDecisionManager.h/.cpp` (comments), `Tests/InfusionSameElementStackSpec.cpp`; docs: Backlog, DesignDecisions (Q-435b), Rules ED-51 status, `Architecture/InfusionSystem.md`, `PIE/B-435b.md`, `PIE/README.md`.
