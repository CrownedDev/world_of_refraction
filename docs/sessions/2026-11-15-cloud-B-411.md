**What changed, in plain words**
- The `wor.Loadout.WeaponSpells` switch (the old path, for B-098's comparison) is gone, with the code it brought back: a weapon's spell list, slots, caps, the Resonate Weapon menu filling, the broken weapon crystal spell greying, adding a spell to a weapon.
- The loadout rules used to check a weapon's spells (learned, element, cap) though nothing reads them. Now any spell an old loadout still has on a weapon is simply dropped, with the usual "Removed: …" message. That is the one change a player could see, and only with an old save.
- Form pieces (character forms) no longer count spells left on a weapon.
- The console stand-ins for the weapon crystal's spell list (`wor.Loadout.PrintSpellSlots` / `ReplaceSpell` / `RestoreSpell`) are deleted; the adapter's list is edited on the gear screen.
