## B-600i: the skill camera styles fitted to Expedition 33 (Code Writer, `feature/B-600i-e33-skill-shots`)

- **What changed:** melee skills now cut to a close three-quarter shot of the enemy as the blow lands; a combo like Over Under cuts to a new angle on each hit. Area skills are filmed lower and from the side. A new Support camera style shows the caster from the front, then the party, for buffs and heals. Thrown skills can keep the camera behind the thrower through the hit (a per-skill tick box, off for now).
- **Why:** the E33 skill study (camera study section 7) matched each skill type to our styles.
- **Verified:** the build, the camera specs (113/0) and the full suite plus validation on the final tree (dev 769b56d89 merged in): Tests: 2601 passed, 0 failed; Data validation: 12 error(s), 0 new.
- **Decided by default (Q-600i):** the re-cut is on for every Melee Rush; the shoulder hold is off until a skill turns it on; the distances in Q-600i.
- **Crown checks:** `docs/PIE/B-600i.md`.

## B-610: jump from a weapon to its skills (Code Writer, `feature/B-610-equipment-skills-jump`)

- **What changed:** on the loadout's Equipment tab, K (R3 on a pad) opens the focused weapon's or adapter's skill slots; Back returns to the same line. Y Options has a Skills line too, and the hint bar shows the key.
- **Why:** to change a weapon's skills without leaving it and finding it again on the Skills tab.
- **Key:** K / R3, free on that tab (X is the view, 6 / L3 set active, Y Options); the mockup's "X again" gave way since X is the view now.
- **Verified:** the build, the loadout v2 and key-hint specs (40/0) and the full suite plus validation on the final tree: Tests: 2602 passed, 0 failed; Data validation: 12 error(s), 0 new. The jump itself (slots open, Back to the same line) needs a party, so PIE checks it.
- **Crown checks:** `docs/PIE/B-610.md`.
