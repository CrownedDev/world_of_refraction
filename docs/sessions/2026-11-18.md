## D-242: weapons attach to the grip sockets (`feature/D-242-grip-sockets`)
- **What changed:** a weapon attaches to one hold per hand (`hand_r_grip`, `hand_l_grip`) and a shield to its own point (`hand_l_shield`, `hand_r_shield`), in place of one socket per weapon type. A body without the socket uses the older socket and warns once. `wor.Weapons.UseGrip 0 / 1` flips between the two in PIE.
- **Why:** D-242: one hold per body, every weapon made to fit it. The female body's same-named older sockets are turned differently, so the sword sat sideways.
- **Not changed:** the weapon's own Mesh Rotation is still applied; weapons that sit wrong on the grip need their fixed copy (`wor.Weapons.PrintSockets` lists what each character uses).
- **How it was checked:** build, the full suite, spec cases in `WoR.Weapons.MeshFollowsLoadout` (each socket choice, the fallback, the switch), wor-reviewer. PIE sheet `docs/PIE/D-242-grip.md`.
- **Crown checks:** the sheet; the three sockets still to add on the skeleton are listed there.
