## B-472 — Loadout costs: the gear is free, only what is in it costs (D-206)

**Branch:** `feature/B-472-loadout-costs-cloud` (from `origin/dev` 90e38b09d).

**What changed, in plain words**
- A weapon or adapter costs nothing in the loadout now. A crystal costs 1, a stone 1, an evolution 2, and an evolution on its own (the character's own slot) 2. Stones are charged for the first time.
- Budgets: Generic 5 (up to three items: two weapons and one adapter, no "one evolved weapon" limit), Resonator 5 (one weapon, at most 5 adapters), Refractor 2 (one item).
- A Refractor that is over 2 (evolved gear plus a stone, e.g. from an old save) loses the stone at the next loadout check: it goes back to the inventory, not destroyed (as Q-159 = A does for crystals). A Resonator's sixth adapter is dropped like an over-budget one.
- The pouch (inventory capacity) still uses the old costs (bare weapon 1, crystal weapon 2, evolved weapon 3, adapter 1, evolved adapter 2), as D-206's notes ask.
- The loadout screen's cost line now names only what is inside: "Free", "Crystal 1", "Evolution 2 + Stone 1 = 3". The new game screen says the Resonator takes "up to five adapters".

**Files:** `LoadoutConstants.h`, `LoadoutRules.h/.cpp` (`SlotCost`, new `AttachmentCost`, class table, `ELoadoutSlotType::PrimaryStone`), `LoadoutComponent.cpp` (RingOverflow and PrimaryStone clears), `LoadoutScreenModel.cpp`, `NewGameScreenModel.cpp`; specs `ClassLimitsSpec`, `LoadoutBudgetSpec`, `LoadoutScreenSpec`; rules CL-03, CL-04, CL-10 to CL-12; `LoadoutSystem.md` changelog; `PIE/B-472.md`.

**Desktop must:** build; run `WoR.Loadout.ClassLimits`, `WoR.Loadout.Budget`, `WoR.UI.LoadoutScreen`, `WoR.UI.NewGameScreen`, then the full suite (other loadout specs may lean on the old costs; the reviewer pass looked for them).

**Notes for Crown**
- A Generic's third item (an adapter beside two weapons) is allowed by the numbers, but the loadout can't hold it yet: that is B-477's weapon-set shape.
- An old save with a Refractor holding evolved gear and a stone will see the stone come off (returned to the inventory) with a "Removed:" message.

**Check in PIE:** `docs/PIE/B-472.md` (11 checks).

## B-477 — Generic weapon sets, first part (D-209 to D-211)

**Branch:** `feature/B-477-weapon-sets-cloud`, **branched from `feature/B-472-loadout-costs-cloud`** (B-477 depends on B-472); merge B-472 first or with it.

**What changed, in plain words**
- A weapon now says how it may be held: single only (the default for every asset until the editor step B-478), dual only, or either. It also gets a second stance slot, the dual-wield stance.
- A Generic's weapon entry can hold a second weapon, the off hand. Only two weapons of the same type that may pair are allowed. The entry's sockets are the main hand's and serve both.
- A pair has one skill list: its slots are the average of the two tiers' (rounded down, so a B and a C give 4) plus the crystal's; both weapons' built-in abilities fill it, the main hand's first. It may hold dual-blade abilities.
- In a fight a pair adds the off hand's stat and resistance lines, keeps the main hand's Power, and stands in the main hand's dual-wield stance. Switch Weapon already swaps the whole entry.
- The loadout check removes a bad pair's off hand (different types, a single-only weapon, a pair on a Resonator or Refractor) and drops a dual-only weapon held alone.
- Saved: the save is now version 39 (older saves are wiped, as every bump in dev).

**Decided by the reference-backed default (Q-477, Crown may change):** new weapons are single-only until B-478 marks them; a pair's Power is the main hand's (Monster Hunter's Dual Blades have one attack value); an off hand's own sockets are ignored; a bad pair goes back to its main hand alone.

**Split off:** B-477b (the off hand's mesh in the left hand, the AI building pairs). The Generic's adapter beside two weapons is B-480's entry shape.

**Files:** `EWeaponHandling.h` (new), `WeaponData.h`, `WeaponSetRules.h/.cpp` (new), `FWeaponLoadoutEntry.h/.cpp`, `FSavedLoadout.h`, `FCombatLoadout.cpp`, `WoRSaveTypes.h`, `SaveSubsystem.cpp`, `WoRSaveGame.h`, `LoadoutRules.h/.cpp`, `LoadoutComponent.h/.cpp`, `InventoryComponent.cpp`, `WeaponSetRulesDebug.h/.cpp` (new), spec `WeaponSetsSpec.cpp` (new); rules CL-23b; Q-477; `LoadoutSystem.md`; `PIE/B-477.md`.

## B-480 — Loadout entries, two augment sockets, evolution placement (D-210)

**Branch:** `feature/B-480-entries-sockets-cloud`, **branched from `feature/B-477-weapon-sets-cloud`** (so it carries B-472 and B-477).

**What changed, in plain words**
- A Generic can now carry an adapter beside its two weapons. It sits in the same adapter list a Resonator uses, so saves need nothing new; with no adapter as its primary, that adapter is the one in use. A second adapter is removed at the next check.
- An entry's two sockets can hold two stones. A stone goes in the stone socket first, then in the empty crystal socket; two of the same stat stack. The loadout screen offers a stone in the crystal socket once the stone socket is full. A third stone, or a gem over a stone, is refused.
- Evolutions: a Resonator can't have one on its weapon, a Generic can't have one on an adapter; a Refractor can do either. A misplaced evolution is taken off at the next check and is free again in the inventory.
- Class limits were already in place from B-472 (Generic 2 weapons + 1 adapter, Resonator 1 weapon + up to 5 adapters, Refractor 1 entry); the character's own evolution costs 2 (B-472). The "asks first" before it attaches belongs to the Add gear window (B-476).

**Decided by the reference-backed default (Q-480, Crown may change):** the Generic's adapter uses the existing adapter list; broken rules are fixed at the next check with a "Removed:" message; two stones fill the stone socket first (Path of Exile's stacked supports as the reference).

**Files:** `FCombatLoadout.cpp`, `FSavedLoadout.h/.cpp`, `LoadoutRules.h/.cpp`, `LoadoutComponent.cpp`, `InventoryComponent.cpp`, `LoadoutScreenModel.cpp`; specs `LoadoutEntriesSpec.cpp` (new), `TwoSocketsSpec.cpp`; rules CL-23c; Q-480; `LoadoutSystem.md`; `PIE/B-480.md`.

**Desktop must:** build; run `WoR.Loadout.Entries`, `WoR.Inventory.TwoSockets`, `WoR.Loadout.*`, `WoR.UI.LoadoutScreen`, then the full suite. **Watch for:** an authored Generic loadout that had hidden adapter entries (the field was hidden for a Generic): they now count; the check drops more than one.

**Not in this part:** the loadout screen offers no free "add an adapter" place for a Generic yet (a Resonator's only); an authored or saved adapter entry shows as a row. The Add gear window (B-476) adds it.

**Review pass:** the Sonnet reviewer found two specs the change would have broken (`LoadoutBudgetSpec`, `LoadoutRulesSpec`), a double count of the Generic's adapter in `GetEquippedCrystals`, and the authored inventory emptying a Generic's adapters; all fixed.

## B-475 — Apparel's rolled bonus line (D-208)

**Branch:** `feature/B-475-apparel-bonus-line-cloud`, **branched from `feature/B-474-weapon-follows-skill-cloud`** (carries the four loadout jobs before it; B-475 itself only needs B-380, already in dev).

**What changed, in plain words**
- Every apparel piece now rolls one extra line the first time it is owned: either a small sub-stat (+2, to tune later; never Turn Speed) or +5% to one pillar's value. The pillar line raises the pillar's value but adds no pillar points, so it never helps meet a skill's requirement.
- Two copies of the same apparel can roll different lines. A piece keeps its line forever (it is saved, and a loaded piece is never re-rolled).
