## B-435: same-element infusion stacking (D-174)
- **Branch:** `feature/B-435-same-element-stacking`
- **What changed:** when an ability or attack is infused, every one of the character's sources with the same element joins in. Each source pays its own cost: a stacked innate or evolution adds its EP, a stacked crystal adds its wear, and the L2 HP backlashes add up. Status buildup gets +25% per extra source, up to +50% (named constants, `TODO: tune`). The stack is worked out once, at commit, so a gem broken by that commit's own wear still counts for that action. `GetSameElementSourceCount` (Blueprint) gives the battle UI its ×N. The AI's "would this kill me" check now counts every source that pays HP.
- **Also fixed on the way:** the Evolution source's commit cost used to read the weapon's attachment, not the character seat. A seated evolution therefore paid no L2 HP and, on abilities, no wear. Both now apply.
- **Files:** `InfusionConstants.h`, `ActionExecutor.h/.cpp`, `ActionExecutorCosts.cpp`, `ActionStructs.h`, `AIDecisionManager.cpp`, new `Tests/InfusionSameElementStackSpec.cpp`; docs: Backlog, DesignDecisions (Q-435), Rules ED-51, `Architecture/InfusionSystem.md`.
- **Check:** build; `WoR.Infusion.SameElementStack`, `WoR.Infusion.Cost`, `WoR.Infusion.SourceResolver`, the AI infusion specs. PIE: a Fire Caster with a Fire gem uses an innate-infused ability (the log line `same-element (Fire) infusion sources stack`; buildup is higher than with one source). A picked evolution now costs HP at L2.
- **Q-435 (default, Crown may change it):** spells don't stack. A spell keeps its one origin source (D-110), so a free ring spell doesn't start costing innate EP.
- **Left open:** a deferred (ritual) fire works the stack out again at fire time (`TODO`). Specs don't yet cover a real 3-source loadout or the AI clamp.

## B-425: a co-op guest's saved wallet was wiped
- **Branch:** `fix/B-425-guest-wallet`
- **What changed:** a guest sends its saved balances and run Gold to the host once when it joins (new `ServerReportWallet`). The host applies them once per player, so a re-report after travel can't refill spent money. A guest never saves the host's copy of its wallet over its own: not at leave (`EndPlay`), not at a save (`CaptureInto`).
- **Files:** `Player/WoRPlayerState.h/.cpp`, `Save/SaveSubsystem.cpp`, `Tests/PlayerWalletSpec.cpp`; docs: Backlog, `CurrencySystem.md`.
- **Check:** build; `WoR.Economy.PlayerWallet`. **2-player PIE:** the guest's balances show on the host after joining, and are still right after leaving and rejoining.
- **Left open (follow-up row needed):** what a guest earns or spends in co-op is not written back to the guest's own save.

## B-428: host hand-over records, and a leaver's dropped Prisms (Q-428 = A)
- **Branch:** `fix/B-428-handover-rekey`
- **What changed:** (a) on a hand-over, the old host's opened chests stay the old host's, and the new host's own become plain host records. Only chests are per-player; shared records (fragments, spots, lore) are left alone. No more duplicate chest loot, and the new host isn't blocked from its own chests. (b) A friend who leaves a run mid-way has their dropped Prisms split equally among the players still there; the host gets the leftover and the drop is removed.
- **Files:** `Save/SaveSubsystem.h/.cpp`, `Online/HostSuccession.cpp`, `Trial/DefeatRules.h/.cpp`, `Player/RunStateComponent.cpp`, `Tests/HostSuccessionSpec.cpp`, `Tests/DefeatSpec.cpp`; docs: Backlog, Rules PA-37i, `SaveSystem.md`.
- **Check:** build; `WoR.Coop.HostSuccession`, `WoR.Trial.Defeat`. 3-player PIE: hand over at a rest spot, then open chests as each player. Have a friend drop Prisms and leave, and check the others' Gold.
- **Left open (follow-up):** the old host's *own* drop during a hand-over isn't split yet. At adoption the other players' wallets don't exist yet. A record counts as a chest's when its feature path's object name contains "Chest" (the chests aren't loaded at adoption), so a chest Blueprint must keep "Chest" in its name (`TODO`). One review-fix commit touched 4 files and was already pushed, so it was left as is rather than rewriting history.
