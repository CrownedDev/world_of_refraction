# Hub and Story

**Status:** decided by Crown, 2026-10-26 (`DesignDecisions.md` D-079). Companion to `RunStructure.md` and `Enemies.md`.
**Spoiler rule:** §2 (who V is) is **never stated to players**. They work it out from lore, dialogue and clues.

## 1. The premise

- A group of characters went to a **bar**, drank **void-spiked drinks**, passed out, and woke up in **the Void Realm** with **no memories**.
- **V** is their guide.
- The playable characters are **not lords**. "Elemental Lord" was a working label; each will have a **real name and lore** (to come). Each is tied to an element.

## 2. Who V is (hidden from players)

- **V is the Void**: the Void Realm itself, the one true Void Lord.
- **The Void is any and all concepts.** That's why V can take any form:
  - **the guide**
  - **every normal enemy** (`Enemies.md`)
  - **every hub NPC** (§3)
- Players should slowly notice that everyone looks like V and wonder why. That's the intended "figure it out".
- V's look (concept: `CharacterDesigns/V_turnaround.png`; void material and clothing on Manny/Quinn, B-143/B-144) carries all of this, so it's **imperative**.
- **The Void Realm wears V's skin** (Crown, 2026-10-28): the hub's stone, crystal faces and sky use the same galaxy material as V's head and hands (`VisualDirection.md` §2f).

## 3. The hub: the Void Realm

- **One fixed hub**, Nightreign's Roundtable model: solo prep between runs, with co-op matchmaking at trial launch (`Resources_Design.md`). The hub itself doesn't change as you progress.
- **Service NPCs are V in different forms** (outfit or prop per role): Blacksmith (weapons), Jeweler (rings), Stone Smith, Spiritualist (evolutions, fusion), ability and spell shops, the loadout vendor (D-048). Every service also works in runs (D-047). Lost-soul NPCs may come later.
- **Gates** to the trials, each showing its variation and difficulty (`RunStructure.md` §3).

## 4. How the story unfolds

- **The opening:** you wake in the Void Realm, no memories, V there. The bar night isn't shown; it's **one of the first memories recovered**.
- **Phased by theme:** the story advances theme by theme, following the gate variations. First **lost Present memories**, next **memories of the Past**, and so on; each phase is about its theme. *Open:* how one phase unlocks the next.
- **Recovered memories show up in three places:**
  - a **memory journal**: every memory, lore object and logged dialogue, rereadable in the hub
  - **V's lines**: new things to say between runs, and the place for clues about V
  - **character conversations**, the heart of it: talks between characters unlock as memories return (Expedition 33 camp style), home of the cross-linked lore
- All three unlock from the **same data** as memories and reaction triggers (`RunStructure.md` §5).
