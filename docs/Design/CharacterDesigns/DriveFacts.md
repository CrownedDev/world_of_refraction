## 2. World system facts (Masters of refraction, 2020)

- **Magic = "refraction"**, "based on light refraction and 8 elements (red = fire, blue = water, yellow = earth, green = wind, orange = lightning, purple = void, white = light, black = darkness)". Eight, not nine; no ninth element named anywhere.
- **Strengthening:** "refracting energy externally… strengthened through incantation, emotion and intent". Later: "emotion, intention and passion are all factors in potent refractions"; refractions are more potent in tense situations than in training.
- **Evolution:** "extreme passion/emotion, can manifest a unique ability… a blessing or curse"; "uses intents emotion and passion to create an ability that draws its power from nature"; never two exact copies. Gifted to the world by the primordial Light. Some cards also list a **"Breakthrough"** (e.g. "Ricochet Aura", "Conjuration breakthrough: path of asura", "Cowling breakthrough: mobility").
- **Dispersion:** every person has a "dispersion container"; a percentage can "refract dispersion energy". A **dispersion element test** (age 7) reads a person's dispersion element and tests "refraction proficiency with destruction, enchantment and conjuration". Historica had "emerald dispersion energy… proficient in conjugations"; Shadow "didn't have one".
- **Refraction categories named:** Destruction, Enchantment / Enhancement (aura, "cowling" = elemental aura), Conjuration. Incantation form: "<Crystal> <element> refract: <category>: <effect>" (e.g. "Opal light refract: enchantment: empower my legs").
- **Crystal ↔ element names** (from cards): Onyx = darkness, Opal = light, Amethyst = void, Emerald = wind, Citrine = lightning, Garnet = fire; Amber = earth and Sapphire = water are implied (Amber ↔ "Seismic sense"; Sapphire ↔ "Circulation"/"Liquid") but not stated outright.
- **Crystals as items:** "Crystal Weapon" (e.g. Emerald(wind) short-sword: "Refracts darkness through the emerald gem creating dark winds"), "Crystal garments" (Opal gauntlet "Resists all elements by 25%", Void Cloak, Citrine ring, Sapphire bracelet), "Evolution crystal" (Historica's necklace, 2024 sheet). **Spectrum transplant:** a procedure that adds a crystal "as a secondary refraction container" (Shadow: amethyst/void).
- **Darkness kit (Shadow):** "Absorbs 25% of external energy (evolution can increase this)", can re-release it keeping traits; "Refraction proficiency: Enhancement enchantment/aura". Void: "Weapon/item Conjuration", "shadow tendrils/sculpting"; "dark voids" cost more than normal voids.
- **Light/dark dispersions** are new to the world (awakened by Light's visits); the world is on its "7th repetition".
- **Titles:** "Elemental Lord" (gods: Mwari, Wukong, Ra, Indra, Odin, Zeus, Vortrix), "Elemental Champion: <crystal/element>" with an "Attunement: <concept>" (e.g. Emerald/protection, lightning/magnetism, Earth/soft spot), "Imperial Blade" > "Imperial Blade Guard" > "Imperial Guard"; also "Imperial Blade Edge", "Peacekeeper / Jr peacekeeper", "Darcist", "Amethyst Queen", "T GUARD", "Twilight guard", "Ronin".
- **Factions / kingdoms** (location cards): "The only timeline where all 5 main kingdoms exist and 6 gods".
  - **The Imperial Kingdom Prismata** (centre) — ruler Orthos; military "Imperial blades / Imperial blade guards / Imperial guards". Seven Imperial Blades per Orthos docx.
  - **The Holy Province Zandaria / Sanctum** (north) — ruler Cosmica (Sanctum) Crown, queen Zandark Sanctum; military "Zandark's Warriors, Enforcers, Wind riders".
  - **The Futuristic Workshop Technologia** (south) — ruler Artemis Citrine; "Technologian Firewall" is its guard rank (Umbra, Elizabeth); "Advancement War" vs the Twilight Empire; "Technologial Kingdom".
  - **The Twilight Haven / Twilight Empire / "Madman's Empire"** (east) — emperor Maximus Madman, queen Peridot; military "Madman's Legion (Amethyst), Berserkers, Army". Contains "the great tree" with sealed "Elemental lords" (Peridot sheet 2024).
  - **The Void Dominion Conceptula** (west) — "Ruler: Victor?"; Vortrix/Violet listed under it.
  - Minor: "blade village" ("Known for soul sword evolutions"), Tiny Kingdom (NW), Sky kingdom (SE), Entertaina City ("gambling city"), Dominion of Ra, Hidden Village (Shadow/Historica's home, NE).
  - "Prismatic Peace Treaty between the 4 kingdoms" (50 years before the main story).
- **Ricochet** (Riley family): "Wind/lightning: Ricochet twins, ones more powerful with a longer duration while the other is more accurate with shorter duration".
