# Hidden Quests and Lore (Shangri-La Frontier model)

**Status:** decided (`DesignDecisions.md` D-100, pillar 10, Crown 2026-10-27). The research that informs it is in the appendix (web summaries, fan-wiki details are what fans report, not canon).

## 1. Decided (D-100)

- **Two separate systems:**
  - **Boss triggers:** each boss / mini-boss has its own "if these conditions, then this" rules (weaken, strengthen, skip / add a phase, a line). Not memory-based.
  - **Memories:** unlock when **all** their conditions are met (who, gear and its level, where, what you beat, how you played, earlier memories, hidden). Per character, can chain, one-time, never shown.
- Hidden conditions are a pillar (10): reward curiosity and experimenting.
- **No hints at all** (Crown, 2026-10-27, pure Shangri-La): hidden memories and scenarios give no rumours, cues or "?" slots; "they activate when they activate".
- **Never missed for good** (Crown, 2026-10-27): conditions are checked again every run; failing one only means "not this run".
- **Co-op** (Crown, 2026-10-27): every player who meets the conditions gets the memory on their own save. Character and gear conditions are checked per player's own character; shared ones (what was beaten, where) count for everyone present. No first-come lock.
- **Rewards beyond story** (Crown, 2026-10-27, SLF / Elden Ring): a hidden unlock can also give rewards, set per unlock: a unique item, a skill, a title, or a curse (a penalty that is also a key to other secrets). Rewards are **different, not stronger** (pillars 2, 3).
- **More than one way in** (Crown, 2026-10-27, SLF's Akane / Rabituza): an unlock can list **alternative condition sets**; meeting any one set opens it.

## 2. What the research suggests (to decide)

1. ~~Hints~~ **decided: none.** SLF has none (it relies on 30 million players trading tips). Small games that did well give **soft hints that never show the conditions**: NPC rumours (V's lines), a small cue when you're close, journal "?" slots (Outer Wilds' ship log, Hades II's prophecy list).
2. **Decided: never missable.** check conditions every run, so one-time means the unlock, not the chance (fixes the Elden Ring / Dragon's Dogma 2 complaints).
3. **Decided: co-op per player.** each player who meets the conditions gets the memory on their own save; nothing is first-come (SLF's Lycagon EX, Destiny 2's Whisper).
4. **Decided: rewards allowed (different, not stronger).** SLF's Unique Scenarios also give gear, skills, jobs and curses; whether this game's hidden content can grant more than story.
5. **Decided: alternative routes.** different routes can lead to the same unlock (SLF: Akane reached Rabituza through a different Colossus).

## 1. Shangri-La Frontier (SLF): how its secret systems work

### Unique Scenarios (US)
- These are hidden side stories that give top-tier gear, skills and magic. The game deliberately
  keeps their trigger conditions secret. Players form clans to find them and trade or sell tips.
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Shangri-La_Frontier_(world)
  - https://www.thereviewgeek.com/shangrila-frontier-s1e3review/
- **Trigger is a state plus a moment, not a quest giver.** Sunraku's start:
  1. He fights the Colossus Lycagon and loses well. Lycagon curses him ("Lycagon's Mark").
     Fans read the curse as being marked a worthy opponent.
  2. Vysache's messenger Emul (Ellym in some translations) seeks him out and becomes his companion.
  3. That opens "Invitation from Rabituza" (recommended level 80). At first only he can see it.
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Emul
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Invitation_from_Rabituza
  - https://plotspoiler.com/title/4057172/tvepisode-2025-venturing-wolves-then-from-the-seas-to-the-skyscrapers
- **Different routes can lead into the same scenario.** Akane was cursed by a different Colossus,
  Siegwurm, and became the second player to unlock the same Rabituza scenario. The underlying
  condition seems to be "marked by a Colossus" rather than one exact fight.
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Akane_Oki
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Siegwurm_the_Sky_Ruler
- **Exclusivity is soft.** A scenario feels personal because almost nobody meets its conditions.
  It is not hard-locked to one person: a second player who meets the conditions gets it too.
- **The game never tells you.** No hint UI exists. Players find these scenarios by accident or
  through information brokers. Sunraku keeps quiet so he doesn't spoil Rabituza, and he trades
  robot information for a lead on Ctarnidd. Information is an in-world currency.
  - https://www.sportskeeda.com/anime/shangri-la-frontier-season-2-episode-3-the-ruler-rabituza-revealed-pencilgon-proposes-alliance

### Unique Monsters / Seven Colossi and Unique Scenario EX
- The Seven Colossi are near-unbeatable world bosses. They can only really be beaten through their
  own **Unique Scenario EX**. Each kill advances the world story, which points toward Bahamut.
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Colossi
  - https://epicstream.com/article/shangri-la-frontier-seven-colossi
- EX scenarios **chain** off earlier scenarios or fights:
  - **Vorpal Epic** is offered only to players who finished "Invitation from Rabituza". It is a
    fetch-and-forge task for Vysache.
    https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Vorpal_Epic
  - **Lycagon:** you must first beat her alter-ego (shadow). That opens the EX scenario
    "Light of Courage, Banishing the Dark" to fight the real Lycagon. The scenario reportedly goes
    to everyone who took part, not only the player who landed the last hit.
    https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Lycagon_the_Nightslayer
  - **Ctarnidd of the Abyss:** Sunraku learns about its EX scenario from other players (Rust and
    Mold). This is discovery through people.
    https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Ctarnidd_of_the_Abyss
- **Wezaemon / Setsuna, "From the Living World, With Love"** is a good template for this game:
  - Location plus time condition: on a full-moon night you inspect moss in the Prismatic Forest
    Grotto, and a secret path opens to the Hidden Garden.
  - Setsuna, the ghost of Wezaemon's lover, sits there. Talking to her starts the scenario. She
    tells the backstory: a misunderstanding, her death, and him guarding her grave.
  - A second condition opens the fight: come back on a **new-moon** night and the area becomes the
    Reverse Grave, where Wezaemon waits. Afterwards you speak to Setsuna again to close the story.
  - Afterwards Setsuna tells the party to uncover "the truth of the world", which hooks into the
    main mystery.
  - One CBR article reverses the moon phases. The fan wiki and the AniFlixy guide agree on the
    version above.
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/From_the_Living_World,_With_Love
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Garden
  - https://aniflixy.com/setsuna-of-the-distant-days-lore-quest-guide-and-epic-rewards-in-shangri-la-frontier/
  - https://www.thereviewgeek.com/shangrila-frontier-s1e13review/
- **Ways to weaken the boss that players discover themselves** (Crown's boss triggers):
  - Holy water cancels Wezaemon's third-phase area attack once Pencilgon realises he is undead.
  - Stealing his sword stops his sword skills.
  - Drawing him to Setsuna's grave distracts him.
  - The Lycagon fight is won with a 100-critical-hit approach.
  - The pattern: the boss's lore tells you its weakness, and players who read the story win.
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_17
  - https://kvasir369.wordpress.com/2024/02/04/shangri-la-frontier-episode-17-review/
  - https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Literature/ShangriLaFrontier

### Curses, hidden jobs, titles
- **Curses as story keys.** Lycagon's Mark stops armour being worn on the cursed body parts. It
  can only be removed by a Saint or by killing Lycagon, and it **evolves** into "Contusion of
  Lycagon" after the shadow fight. Some sources say weak monsters flee from cursed players.
  A curse is both a drawback and a flag that marks you as eligible for scenarios.
  - https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/ShangriLaFrontier
- **Vorpal Soul** goes to players who stand up to huge odds and hit hard. It works as a hidden
  "how you played" condition. The Vorpal Soul Collar trades EXP gain for more stat points.
  - https://www.primevideo.com/detail/0LA5USYMUJWOHIADWEXZSMJU6R
- **Hidden jobs.** Akane's Ninja job unlocks when three things are true: she uses the Thief job,
  she masters some magic, and a **hidden stat** ("Ninja Quality") passes a threshold. Then a
  Thieves' Guild NPC approaches her and gives a three-fight test. The pattern is hidden counters
  tracked silently, an NPC who appears when you qualify, and a trial.
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Akane_Oki

### Lore books ("World Book of Truth" / Tomes of Truth)
- Beating a Colossus gives a **Tome of Truth** covering its battle phases and backstory. Lore comes
  as a reward for doing the hard thing, and it explains what you just survived.
  - https://shangrila-frontier.fandom.com/wiki/Tome_of_Truth
- Lore is pieced together across books, NPC talk (Setsuna, Vysache) and the world's history
  (Alice Frontier's experiments, the "Plans"). Lore-hunting clans buy and sell the books. Fans
  report a lore clan ("Library", led by "the Professor") that was overcharged for a Wezaemon book.
  This detail is not checked.
  - https://www.cbr.com/shangri-la-frontier-key-lore-details/

### SLF summary of patterns
1. Conditions are a **state** (cursed, holding a companion, a hidden stat, earlier scenarios done)
   plus a **moment** (place, time or moon, talking to an NPC).
2. A scenario is personal because its conditions are rare, not because it is locked to one player.
3. Scenarios chain: a US opens an EX scenario, and an EX scenario moves the world story on.
4. The game gives no hints. The **community and information trading** are the hint system.
   That works in a 30-million-player fictional MMO. It does not work for a small player base.
5. Boss lore gives away boss weaknesses.

## 2. Real games: what players praised and what they criticised

### FromSoftware (Dark Souls / Elden Ring)
- **Praise:** lore lives in item descriptions, NPC lines and the world. Players have to put it
  together, and the community theorising (VaatiVidya) is part of the appeal. Short item texts hint
  instead of explaining.
  - https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/narrative-design-in-dark-souls
  - https://gamedeveloper.com/design/story-where-there-is-none-lessons-from-demon-s-souls
- **Criticism:** NPC questlines are easy to miss or break. Entering a new region (for example the
  Forbidden Lands) can quietly close quests, and players say they need a wiki. Even VaatiVidya
  says he ignores lore on a first playthrough.
  - https://steamcommunity.com/app/1245620/discussions/0/5706632550006340901
  - https://pcgamer.com/all-elden-ring-quests-side-best
  - https://gb.readly.com/magazines/edge/2024-05-16/66434f107e4e23ea7bc1fb3f
- **Fix FromSoftware shipped:** patch 1.03 marks NPCs on the map once you've met them, because
  reviewers had told players to keep paper notes. This is an admission that some tracking is needed.
  - https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2022/03/17/elden-ring-update-1-03-patch-notes-adds-npc-log-map-icons-quest-phases-and-more/

### Outer Wilds: progress through knowledge
- What you know is the only progress. The **Ship Log** records every clue across time loops.
  - **Rumor Mode** draws a web linking the clues and where they might lead.
  - An **asterisk** marks a place you haven't fully explored.
  - There are no objectives. Widely acclaimed: BAFTA Best Game and Best Game Design, and many
    Game of the Year awards.
  - https://www.pressreader.com/australia/edge/20190620/282278141843961
  - https://www.tomsguide.com/reviews/outer-wilds
  - https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/little-big-universe-outer-wilds-review
- Lesson: a journal that records what you've **seen**, without telling you what to do, keeps
  the mystery and stops players getting lost.

### Tunic
- Manual pages hidden in the world teach you things you could already do. Marginal notes give
  hints. Critics loved it; the developer favours "risky secrets" that change how you think.
  - https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-content-for-no-one-an-interview-with-the-team-behind-tunic
  - https://www.redbull.com/int-en/tunic-video-game-developer-interview
- Lesson: hints can be collectable in-world objects instead of UI.

### Animal Well and Fez: layered secrets and community secrets
- Animal Well has three layers:
  1. Anyone can finish the base game.
  2. Optional secrets sit on top for those who look.
  3. A last layer is designed for groups. One puzzle gave each player a unique tile, and about 50
     Discord players combined theirs.
  - https://thinkygames.com/features/interview-how-animal-well-is-using-secrets-and-mysteries-to-be-a-different-kind-of-metroidvania/
- Fez's black monolith was only solved by brute force from the community, and it was called
  practically unsolvable. Fans enjoyed the shared effort, but solo players could not reach it.
  - https://www.engadget.com/2012-04-20-how-the-community-solved-fezs-hardest-puzzle.html
  - https://kotaku.com/a-look-back-at-fezs-unsolvable-black-monolith-puzzle-1794358854
- Lesson: keep the community-only layer small and optional. It works for very large audiences.

### NieR: Automata weapon stories
- Each weapon upgrade unlocks the next part of that weapon's (often tragic) story. Level 4 needs
  a special smith. The secret Ending Y needs every weapon fully upgraded, but the main ending
  needs none of it.
  - https://twinfinite.net/guides/nier-automata-unlock-view-weapon-stories-how-to/
  - https://twinfinite.net/guides/nier-automata-how-to-upgrade-your-weapons-to-max-level-4/
- Lesson: this maps directly onto Crown's "gear equipped and its upgrade level" condition. Lore
  that rewards investment, and upgrades you would do anyway, feels fair.

### Hades / Hades II: story in a roguelite
- Every death returns you to the hub, where characters have new lines that react to your last run:
  the boss that killed you, the secret you found. This is widely praised as how to tell a story
  in a roguelike.
  - https://www.godisageek.com/2020/12/goty-2020-best-new-idea-hades-how-to-tell-a-story-in-a-roguelike/
  - https://gdconf.com/news/roguelikes-and-narrative-design-hades-creative-director-greg-kasavin-gdc-podcast-ep-16
- The **Fated List of Minor Prophecies** is a visible checklist of side goals. Hades II tracks
  progress on them **even before you meet the prerequisites**. Later patches made key events
  easier to see and added narrated endings.
  - https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Fated_List_of_Minor_Prophecies/Hades_II
  - https://www.siliconera.com/?p=1114705
- Lesson: in a run-based game, check conditions at the end of each run and pay out in the hub.
  Nothing has to be permanently missable.

### Monster Hunter World
- Special Arena quests unlock silently. Most unlock when you capture that monster; others unlock
  through gathering, research levels or a count of tempered hunts. Players complain they cannot
  tell which one they are missing.
  - https://steamcommunity.com/app/582010/discussions/0/1735462352477330430
  - https://monsterhunterworld.wiki.fextralife.com/The+White+Winds+of+the+New+World
- Lesson: a hidden counter is fine, but give the player a way to see "X of Y found".

### FFXIV (Hildibrand) and Persona
- FFXIV's Hildibrand chain is gated by level, main-story progress and earlier chains. It is hidden
  only in that it is not on the main path, and it is a beloved comedy side story.
  - https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Hildibrand_Sidequests
- Persona 5's requests stop at a story date (December 23) and some need eavesdropping or a
  Confidant rank. Content missed this way is a common source of player regret.
  - https://megamitensei.fandom.com/wiki/File:Youji_Isshiki_(P5).png
  - https://www.thegamer.com/persona-5-royal-mementos-requests-shadows-walkthrough-guide/

### Dragon's Dogma 2: what not to do
- Quests fail silently over time, quests disappear when you change region, and a point of no
  return wipes all side quests. Players criticised this as punishing, and the warning text was
  called misleading.
  - https://www.gfinityesports.com/news/dragons-dogma-2-side-quests-warning
  - https://primagames.com/gaming/are-there-missable-quests-in-dragons-dogma-2-answered
  - https://www.powerpyx.com/dragons-dogma-2-all-side-quests/

### Destiny 2 "The Whisper": a co-op secret done well
- Bungie shipped a secret mission without announcing it. A hidden Taken portal on Io led to a timed
  fireteam mission with an exotic reward. Players loved finding it together, and it is remembered
  as some of Destiny 2's best content.
  - https://primagames.com/featured/destiny-2-whisper-of-the-worm-guide
  - https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2018/07/21/destiny-2-secret-mission-gives-exotic-weapon-vog-reference-new-lore/amp/

### Common criticism and the fixes good designs use
| Problem | Fix that worked |
|---|---|
| Content missed forever | Hades: re-checks every run, nothing lost. NieR: upgrades are permanent. |
| "Needed a wiki" | Outer Wilds Ship Log. Elden Ring 1.03 NPC map markers. |
| Can't tell what's left | Monster Hunter-style counts or a Hades-style list showing "?" slots. |
| Hint text too cryptic or none at all | Tunic's in-world hint pages. NPC rumours (SLF's information trading, made into an in-game NPC). |
| Solo players locked out | Animal Well: only the top layer is communal, the rest is solo-solvable. |

## 3. Practical takeaways for World of Refraction

These are suggestions to judge against `DesignPillars.md`. None of this is a ruling.

### Condition categories (one shape for memories and boss triggers)
- **Who:** character id; class (Generic / Caster / Resonator); party makeup.
- **Gear:** item equipped; minimum upgrade or tier; crystal or element seated. (NieR-style.)
- **Where and when:** trial map, zone, hub spot; time or phase (SLF's moon), weather (WoR already
  has a WeatherSystem).
- **What you beat:** enemy or boss id, optionally *with* a condition (killed by element X).
- **How you played:** no damage taken, turns under N, never healed, only one element used, crits
  of at least N (SLF's 100-crit Lycagon).
- **Story state:** memories already unlocked (this is what makes chains), flags, curses carried.
- **Hidden counters:** silent running totals (SLF's "Ninja Quality", Monster Hunter's tempered
  hunts), for example "parried 50 times as character X".
- **Bosses:** the same condition list, but the result is "boss weakened or phase skipped" instead
  of "memory unlocked". Make the boss's lore hint at its own weakness (SLF's holy water and
  Setsuna's grave).

### Hint tiers (keep conditions hidden but avoid needing a wiki)
0. **Nothing shown:** for the rarest secrets, a small share of the content (Animal Well's layer 3).
1. **Rumour:** hub NPCs or item text vaguely point at an *unmet* memory. Each run, pick one
   rumour from memories that are close to unlocking. (This is SLF's information trading done by NPCs.)
2. **Partial echo:** when at least half the conditions are met at once, play a short sensory cue
   (a whisper, a crystal flicker). Never list the conditions. Outer Wilds' asterisk works the same way.
3. **Journal slot:** a memory archive per character shows "?" slots, with the count per character
   or zone. Later, a fragment line appears once one condition has ever been met (Hades II style).
- All tiers keep Crown's "conditions never shown" rule. They hint at *where* to look, not *what*
  the conditions are.

### Anti-frustration in a 30–45 minute run game
- Check at run end and at key events, every run. A failed attempt is just "try next run". Avoid
  Dragon's Dogma-style lockouts.
- Make "one-time" mean the unlock itself. Its conditions can be retried forever.
- Keep "how you played" conditions inside a single fight or a single run, not a whole save.
- Hidden counters persist across runs (on the save), so progress builds slowly.
- A chain should never need a memory from a character the player can't get any more.

### Co-op (solo and co-op equally)
- Precedent: SLF's EX scenario goes to every participant, and Destiny's Whisper is a group find.
- Suggestion: per-character conditions (who, gear) are checked **for each player's own
  character**. Shared conditions (place, what was beaten, party-wide how-you-played) count for
  everyone present.
- Each player whose character qualifies gets the memory **on their own save**. There is no
  first-come exclusivity: SLF's exclusivity comes from rarity, not locks.
- Optional "co-op only" memories (for example two specific characters both present) as a small
  extra layer. Never gate a main chain on co-op.
- When one player unlocks a memory, the others could see "[ally] remembered something", which
  shares the find without spoiling it. Crown would decide this.
- Note: CLAUDE.md D-064 says per-fight state goes on actors or GameState, not a subsystem.

### Data structure sketch (fits the CLAUDE.md architecture rules)
- `UMemoryDefinition : UPrimaryDataAsset` holds:
  - Id
  - OwningCharacter
  - `TArray<FMemoryCondition>`, all of which must pass
  - PrerequisiteMemories
  - HintTier and RumourText (`FText`)
  - Reward, and lore text as `FText`
- `FMemoryCondition` (`USTRUCT`) holds a type enum (Character / Equipped / UpgradeLevel /
  Location / Defeated / PlayStyle / Memory / Counter / Flag), an id parameter, a number parameter
  and a comparison.
- Fight events (enemy defeated, damage taken, crit, turn ended, run ended) go through delegates
  into a per-run tracker, which lives on GameState or PlayerState under D-064. The tracker records
  facts. At run end, or on a trigger event, the checker evaluates candidate memories, indexed by
  the event types they care about so it doesn't loop over every memory.
- Unlocked memories and hidden counters go in an explicit save struct (D-059), per character.
- Boss triggers reuse `FMemoryCondition` and are checked at combat start or on phase change, with
  the result applied as a modifier.
- Debug tools: a `MemoryDebug` that prints, for any memory, which conditions currently pass. Per
  the repo's debug rule, a designer must be able to see why a memory did not fire without
  replaying the run.
