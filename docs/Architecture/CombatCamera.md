# Combat Camera

## Overview

The combat camera decides what the player sees during a fight (design: `docs/Design/CombatCamera.md`, D-074). Each combatant owns one camera, and every camera follows the same mode rules. A director on the combat orchestrator picks which camera the viewer sees and in which mode:
- **Turn:** the viewer's own character is choosing an action.
- **Not your turn:** anyone else's turn, panned out wide.
- **Action:** a skill's Level Sequence is playing.
- **Static:** camera movement is turned off.
- **Defence** (B-370, B-402): an enemy attack on the viewer's character: a low side-on shot of the defender and the attacker from the attacker's weapon side (several targeted: a wide side-on shot of them all), held still: no shake and no FOV punch while the viewer defends.

Built in B-156 (code only). The template sequences and the rig tuning are B-157.

**Frame:** the camera is always framed from the viewer's side: `TowardEnemies` runs from the local party's centre to the opposing party's. So enemy turns never flip the view across the arena.

**Sequence binding:** the tags `Caster` and `Targets` are bound with `ALevelSequenceActor::SetBindingByTag`. A sequence's camera-cut track owns the view while it plays.

## Integration Points

- `ACombatOrchestrator::StartCombat` calls `CameraDirector->BeginCombat(LocalParty, OpposingParty, ActionExecutorRef)` **before** `TurnManager->InitializeCombat`, because turn 1 is dispatched inside that call.
- `ACombatOrchestrator::ReleaseCombatResources` calls `CameraDirector->EndCombat()` on a win, a loss and a forced end. The director's own `EndPlay` also calls it.
- Delegates the director binds (and removes at the end of the fight):
  - `ACombatOrchestrator::OnActorTurnStarted`: show that actor's camera in Turn, NotYourTurn or Static.
  - `UActionExecutor::OnActionStarted`: frame the executor (Execution turns, where a ritual fires, have no turn-start broadcast), then resolve and play the sequence. With no sequence, the turn view stays.
  - The sequence's `OnFinished`: go back to the turn view. The sequence actor is destroyed next tick, not inside its own broadcast.
  - The sequence is also stopped by the next turn start. Not by `OnActionCompleted`: that broadcast arrives after the orchestrator may already have started the next turn and its sequence.
- B-406 (Q-406): boss cinematics, opt-in per boss (`UBossData::OpeningSequence`, `FBossPhase::CinematicSequence`, soft, empty = none; tags `Boss` / `Party`). `StartCombat` asks `CameraDirector->PlayBossOpening()` just before `InitializeCombat`; when it plays, turn 1 waits (`bWaitingForAsyncAction` + `bHoldingForBossOpening`) for `OnBossCinematicFinished`. The director binds each opposing `UBossPhaseComponent::OnBossPhaseReached` and plays that phase's sequence; `RequestActionFromActor` holds while one plays. `OnCinematicHudHidden(bool)` hides the native `UCombatHUD` (`SetHiddenForCinematic`). While one plays the director leaves the view alone (no shots, no Defence hand-off). No local viewer, cinematics off (D-078) or an empty field: nothing plays. Cap `BossCinematicMaxSeconds` (20 s). An enemy skill with `FSkillCameraTuning::bNamedAttack` opens on the `NamedAttack` front shot. Debug: `wor.Camera.BossCinematic opening|phase N|stop`, `BossCinematicPreviewSeconds`.
- B-405: on a win, `ACombatOrchestrator::OnActionCompleted` calls `CameraDirector->PlayKillShot(LastEnemyDown, GetEnemyRank(...))` and holds the fight open for the seconds it returns before `FinishCombat`. No viewer returns 0 (no hold).
  - B-399: `UCombatCommandMenuSubsystem::OnMenuDepthChanged(Actor, MenuDepth)` (found with `WoRFight::Get`, held weakly for the fight): the viewed character's menu went to the main menu (0) or a sub-menu (1); a target picker keeps the depth it came from (`CommandMenuRules::CameraDepthFor`, Q-399). On the viewer's own turn view the director blends the turn camera to that depth's view (0.3 s on the shot timer), then holds still; closing the menu sends nothing. Both the old HUD and the new one (B-391) drive the subsystem, so neither widget is involved.
  - B-400: `UCombatCommandMenuSubsystem::OnTargetHighlighted(Actor, Target, TargetCount)`: the single-target picker's highlight (the first target on open, then a target button's hover / focus via `UCombatCommandButtonWidget`, or `CycleHighlightedTarget`; null on close). With 2+ targets on the viewer's own turn view the director shows `ComputeTargetView`; null while it shows: back to the turn view; `OnActionStarted` cuts back to the turn view first, so the action's shots start as before.
- Viewer: `GetWorld()->GetFirstPlayerController()`, the same one `UCombatHUD` uses. The view target from before the fight is restored at the end.

## Known Limitations / TODOs

- Solo only: the whole local side counts as the viewer's characters. Co-op and PvP come with D-065.
- No template sequences exist yet (B-157), so every action stays on the turn view.
- Offsets and the 0.6 s blend are first guesses (`TODO: tune`, B-157). So are B-399's menu step (0.25 in, 0.3 down, 0.3 s).
- B-400: the target view's numbers are first guesses (`TODO: tune`); nothing checks that another enemy stands between the camera and the target (`CameraFraming::IsLineClear` could, later); no world marker on the highlighted target yet (`OnTargetHighlighted` is ready for one). The Blueprint pie-menu buttons, if they don't derive from `UCombatCommandButtonWidget`, need `HighlightTarget` wired to hover / focus (editor step, PIE B-400).
- B-399: in a turn with several actions (B-170c), a skill's held shot stays on screen while the menu reopens; the menu step waits for the turn view (B-401).
- Settings have no menu yet: set them in the Details panel or with the console commands (D-024). The turn view is saved (B-398, `FSavedSettings`); the others are not yet.
- Sequences load synchronously at action start (`TODO: B-140`).
- A sequence runs until it ends or the next turn starts. Re-check this once real templates exist.
- Choices made for the basic version, for Crown to confirm in PIE (D-078):
  - camera movement off also skips sequences
  - enemy actions play sequences too
  - a death at turn start leaves the view where it was
- B-404: `OnTurnHandover` has no HUD listener yet (the turn strip and card highlight bind it with the new HUD, B-105), and the action menu still opens at turn start, during the wide beat (Q-404).
- B-406: no skip key for a boss cinematic, no hero shot on every boss turn, an Execution (ritual fire) turn is not held by a phase cinematic, and an action already playing finishes under it (B-406b).
- The Defence view (B-402) holds one angle per enemy action; cutting between side angles inside a long combo is not built (Q-402). Its side assumes a right-handed weapon (`WeaponSideSign`).
- B-407: a player or team skill's own `CameraSequence` is cut back at `SignatureCameraCap::MaxSignatureSeconds` (2 s, `TODO: tune`); enemy casters' sequences run to their end. The validator counts only skills a `UBossData` phase adds as a boss's (Q-407).
- Arming a delayed skill also broadcasts `OnActionStarted`. Once templates exist, a ritual's cinematic may play at arm time and again when it fires. Check this in B-157.
