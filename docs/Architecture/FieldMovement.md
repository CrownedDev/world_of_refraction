# Field movement (field skills)

## Overview
B-214 (D-099, `Design/FieldAbilities.md`): movement on the map beyond the free sprint. Everyone has the normal dash; each character has one **active field skill** (a source, then a movement type) that a double tap of sprint uses. The source's element styles the skill and sets its strength. B-214a builds the core: the dash, the per-character choice (saved), and **Enhanced Sprint** (a faster sprint by element, paid in EP per second). Unlocks (B-214b), the other moves (B-214c..e), the field attack (B-214f), the input assets (B-214g) and evolutions (B-214h) follow.

## Architecture
- `FieldSkillTypes.h` (`Field/`): `EFieldMovementType` (11 types, append only: saved as a byte), `EFieldSkillSource` (Innate / Weapon / Ring), `EFieldSkillRefusal`, `FFieldSkillChoice` (default Innate + Dash).
- `FieldSkillRules` (pure, `Private/Field/FieldSkillRules.cpp`): `CheckPick` (Dash for anyone; every other type needs an elemental source, i.e. not Generic / None; unbuilt types refused), `CheckEnhancedSprintStart` (the pick rule again, then one second's EP), `GetEnhancedSprintMultiplier` (Lightning ×1.5, Wind ×1.4, other elements ×1.25, non-element ×1), constants (dash 1 s cooldown, 1800 cm/s; 1 EP per second). All numbers `// TODO: tune` (Q-214a).
- `UFieldMovementComponent` (`Field/FieldMovementComponent.h`): on the map pawn, replicated.
  - `Dash()`: `LaunchCharacter` along the ground velocity (else facing); free; refused on cooldown or on a non-character pawn.
  - `SetActiveFieldSkill(Source, Type)` / `GetActiveFieldSkill()`: the choice's home is the party record (`FPartyMember::FieldSkill`, written by `UPartySessionSubsystem::SetMemberFieldSkill`; only a pawn stamped with its member id, B-348b's rule); a pawn with no record keeps it on the component. `ActiveChoice` is the replicated mirror for the owning client (`RefreshFromRecord` after possess). Changing the choice stops Enhanced Sprint.
  - `GetSourceElement(Source)`: Innate = `UCharacterData::GetElement` (a Caster's element, Generic otherwise); Weapon = the crystal in the weapon in hand (None when empty or broken); Ring = a Resonator's active adapter, another class's primary ring (as the parry colour reads it, B-393).
  - `ActivateFieldSkill()`: Dash dashes; Enhanced Sprint toggles; other types refused (not built yet).
  - Enhanced Sprint: on start the first second is paid (`UCharacterDataComponent::ServerSpendEnergy`), then a looping 1 s timer (`DrainEnhancedSprint`) charges again; below one charge it turns off. A step charges only while the pawn moves at sprint speed (`IsMovingAtSprintSpeed`: the CharacterMovement's real ground speed above 600 cm/s; B-214a2, Crown's Q-214a answer), so standing or walking costs nothing and it stays on. `bEnhancedSprintActive` and its element replicate; `OnRep_EnhancedSprintActive` re-reads the sprint speed when the pawn is sprinting and broadcasts `OnEnhancedSprintChanged`.
  - Client calls (`Dash`, `ActivateFieldSkill`, `SetActiveFieldSkill`) go to the server by Server RPC; the server spends the EP and owns the record.
- **Ground and air moves (B-214c, Q-214c, `FieldMovementMoves.cpp`):** `ToggleGlide` (in the air, paid once; gravity ×0.15, air control 0.8, the fall capped at 250 cm/s each step), `HighJump` (ground: 1200 up; air: one 900 double jump), `AirDash` (in the air, once, along the controller's aim at 2000 cm/s; Lightning: 3 swept 250 cm blinks, then a hover). Wall-run: while Enhanced Sprint is on, `StepMoves` traces 80 cm left and right; `ApplyWallRunStep` holds the height (gravity 0) along the wall for up to 1.5 s, once per airtime. Each move re-checks the pick (`CheckActiveChoiceStillPickable`) and refuses `NotActiveSkill` unless it is the active field skill; a glide also ends when the pawn stops falling without a landing event. The owner's `LandedDelegate` → `NotifyLanded` gives the air uses back and ends glide / wall-run. `bGliding` / `bWallRunning` replicate; `ApplyAirMoveState` sets gravity and air control (restored to the values read at BeginPlay). A 0.05 s step timer runs only while gliding or in Enhanced Sprint.
- **Flight (B-214d, Q-214d, `FieldMovementFlight.cpp`):** `ToggleFlight`: refused in a no-fly zone (`IsInNoFlyZone`: any `AVolume` with the actor tag `Zone.NoFly` encompassing the pawn) or without a second's EP; on: `MOVE_Flying` at 900 cm/s with 4000 braking (it hovers with no input), the first second paid, then `DrainFlight` 2 EP a second; off (`MOVE_Falling`) at 0 EP, on a second tap, on a new pick, or when the source stops allowing it. `ApplyFlightStep` (on the move step timer) brings it down in a no-fly zone and clamps it under the ceiling (1500 cm above the ground under the take-off). `FFieldFlightLimits` (`SetFlightLimits`, not saved) lifts the EP cost, the ceiling (or raises it) and the no-fly zones. `bFlying` replicates; `ApplyFlightMode` sets the mode, speed and braking on the server and the owning client.
- **Advanced moves (B-214e, Q-214e, `FieldMovementAdvanced.cpp`):** `UseAdvancedMove(Type)`: the pick re-check, then `FieldSkillRules::CheckAdvancedMove` (Regroup refused while `UPartySessionSubsystem::IsFightRunning`, the per-move cooldown in `LastAdvancedUseTime`, the EP), paid only once the move happened. Teleport: swept along the aim up to 1500 cm (Void only by the unlock rule). Phase step: `TeleportTo` 400 cm ahead (only the destination is checked). Mark and recall: `bHasRecallMark` / `RecallMark`; marking is free, recalling paid. Super launch: 2500 up and `bSuperLaunchGlidePending`, which `StepMoves` turns into a free glide at the top. Regroup: `TeleportTo` behind the farthest other `UFieldMovementComponent` pawn. Every movement type is built.
- **Field attack (B-214f, Q-214f, `FieldMovementAttack.cpp`):** `FieldAttack(bHeld)` sweeps a sphere 2 m ahead; the first `UEncounterComponent` whose `StrikeFromField` accepts engages (the same path as walking in, `HandleOverlap`), with `EFirstStrike::Party` and the strike (`FFieldStrike`: element of `FieldAttackSource`, the weapon's `WeaponAttack` physical type, Ice when held with Water, 25 buildup) sent through `UTrialRunSubsystem::SetPendingFieldStrike`. `ABattleGameMode::ApplyFieldStrike` gives every enemy that buildup (`AddStatusBuildupForHit`) after `StartCombat`. `SwitchFieldAttackSource` steps Innate → Weapon → Ring (weapon by default, replicated to the owner).
- **Evolutions (B-214h, Q-214h):** `GetSeatedEvolution` (the character slot's, unless broken). Its `FieldSprintBonusPercent` multiplies `GetSprintSpeedMultiplier` always (+15% default); its `FieldSkillEPDiscountPercent` lowers every field EP cost through `GetFieldEPCost` (`SpendFieldEP`, Enhanced Sprint's drain; at least 1); its `FieldFlightLimits` combine with the effects' in `GetEffectiveFlightLimits`.
- `UHubMovementLibrary::ComputeHubSprintSpeed` (the pawn Blueprint's sprint press) multiplies by `GetSprintSpeedMultiplier`; `GetSprintSpeedMin` tells the component whether the pawn is sprinting.
- Save v36: `FSavedPartyMember::FieldSkillSource` / `FieldSkillType`.
- **Unlocks (B-214b, Q-214b):** `EFieldAura` (Elemental / Enhancement / Manipulation) marks an aura spell (`USpellData::FieldAura`; its element is the variant). `FieldSkillRules`: `GetUnlockAuras` (FieldAbilities' movement → aura table), `GetUnlockRank` (3 = D tier for Dash, Enhanced Sprint, Glide, High jump, Air dash; 7 = S tier for the rest), `CanElementUnlock` (a real element; Teleport Void only), `IsUnlockedByRanks`, `TierToRank`. `CheckPick` / `CheckEnhancedSprintStart` take `bUnlocked` and refuse `NotUnlocked`; the dash never needs one.
- `UFieldSkillUnlockSubsystem` (GameInstance, account-wide): the best rank per aura and element (ranks only rise). Fed by `UEconomyService::LevelUpSpell`, the store's `OnPoolSkillsChanged`, and `RecordFromPool` (on load, on a pick, when the picker opens). `IsUnlocked`, `GetUnlockedTypes`, `OnFieldSkillUnlocked`. Saved as `FWoRSaveData::FieldUnlocks` (`FSavedFieldUnlocks`, no version bump: an older 38 loads none).
- `UFieldSkillPickerWidget` (placeholder, `UI/Field/`): the player's character's source, then the next unlocked type; from the party screen's "Field skill..." or `wor.UI.FieldSkill`.

## Integration Points
- `AWoRHubController::OnPossess` (server) adds the component after `EnsureLocalParty` and calls `RefreshFromRecord`; fights open their own level, so the pawn (and the drain timer) ends there.
- Input: `IA_Field_MovementSkill` through the config soft pointer `DefaultFieldMovementSkillAction` (as `IA_Interact`), bound to `Triggered` (the asset's own trigger does the double tap). B-214g binds `IA_Field_Attack` (Started stamps the press, Completed calls `FieldAttack(held)`, held at 0.4 s), `IA_Field_SwitchSource` (Started) and `IA_Field_FlyVertical` (1D axis → `AddFlightVerticalInput`) the same way (`DefaultField*Action`). The assets are B-214g's editor step; the ini lines are commented until then.
- Debug (`Debug/Field/FieldMovementDebug`): B-214b adds `wor.Field.Unlocks` (aura ranks and unlocked types) and `wor.Field.Unlock <Type> <Element>`; `wor.Field.Print` (every field pawn: choice, source element, pick result, sprint on / off and multiplier, EP, dash cooldown, record), `wor.Field.Activate` (the double tap's stand-in), `wor.Field.Dash`, `wor.Field.Set <Innate|Weapon|Ring> <Type>` (placeholder until B-214b's picker).
- Spec `WoR.Field.Skills`; `WoR.Save` checks the save round trip.

## Known Limitations / TODOs
- A crystal source doesn't wear yet (B-214 row: crystal sources wear).
- Unlocks are per machine: in co-op the host checks a guest's pick and Enhanced Sprint against the host's unlocks (`TODO` for co-op accounts). The aura spells aren't marked yet (Crown's table, editor step), so only `wor.Field.Unlock` unlocks anything until then.
- B-214c's moves are server-side (no client prediction): a guest's jump or dash arrives a round trip late and may correct. The air dash has no aim pause ("hover, aim") and no VFX; the wall-run checks the actor's sides only (no wall-climb, no jump off the wall).
- Flight scans every volume for the `Zone.NoFly` tag each 0.05 s step; fine for a few volumes, cache them if a map has many. Flight's looks per element (Earth magnetism, Fire Phoenix Rising) aren't built.
- B-214h: a co-op guest's loadout isn't replicated, so its machine computes the sprint without the evolution's bonus while the server applies it (corrections). The new speed applies from the next sprint press.
- The picker edits the map pawn only (the leader); a member picks when it leads.
- The drain samples the speed once a second, so a second is charged or free by the speed at that moment. 
- Co-op: a guest's dash launches on its own machine and on the server (not a predicted saved move), so a small correction is possible. `MaxWalkSpeed` doesn't replicate: the server and the guest each re-apply the sprint speed when Enhanced Sprint toggles (`OnRep`), so for a moment they can differ; the pawn Blueprint's sprint press must also run on the server for the two to agree (PIE check, review 2026-11-12).
- Enhanced Sprint stops when its source loses the element it started with (gear change, broken crystal), checked each drain second.
- No HUD cue for Enhanced Sprint (binds `OnEnhancedSprintChanged` later).
