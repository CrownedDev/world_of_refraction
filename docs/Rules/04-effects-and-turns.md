# Effects and turns

This page covers how a fight starts, who acts when (the action-pips rotation, bonus and Execution turns, the turn-order strip), what you may do on a turn and what it costs (EP, HP, overflow), and every rule for effects: how long they last, when they act and expire, re-applying and stacking, charges, Last Stand, absorb and reflect, cleanse, gear effects on a mid-fight swap, auras and effect text. It ends with how a fight is won or lost. Status bars and what a full bar does are in 03-status-bars.md; damage and elements in 02-elements-and-damage.md; classes, loadouts and infusion sources in 01-classes-and-loadouts.md; rewards and runs in 05-economy-and-runs.md; party control in 06-party.md. Code paths are relative to `Source/world_of_refraction/`.

## Conflicts
- **C4-1 — Turn ratio cap.** `MAX_TURN_RATIO` 2 and `TURN_SPEED_THRESHOLD_DOUBLE` 15 say speed is "capped at 2:1" (`Public/Combat/CombatConstants.h`), and the comment on `TURN_SPEED_GEAR_CEILING` relies on it. The scheduler never reads either constant, and the ratio is uncapped (`Private/Combat/TurnManager.cpp` `CalculateSpeedRatios`); a strong Turn Speed Debuff can push a rival toward speed 1. The deckbuilder has no speed scheduler. **Ruled (D-049): A**, 2 turns per turn of the slowest; built by B-297.
- **C4-2 — Defend wore off before anyone attacked. Settled (D-050), built in B-225.** `ExecuteDefend` gives +50% Defense for 1 turn. The old end-of-turn tick removed it on the defender's own turn, so it did nothing. With D-051's counting the defend turn counts as turn 1 and the buff ends as the defender's next turn starts, as the deckbuilder's Defend covers the enemy's turn (ET-20).
- **C4-3 — How durations count, for every timed effect. Settled (D-051), built in B-225.** The code used to end an effect at the end of the holder's Nth turn, so an effect applied on your own turn lost a turn and one applied by an enemy ended before the enemies acted again. Now every timed effect uses D-030's counting: the landing turn counts as turn 1 and the effect ends as the holder's next turn after those starts (`Private/Skills/Effects/SkillEffectManager.cpp` `ApplyTimingRule`; ET-34).
- **C4-4 — DoT tick count. Settled (D-051, Q-044c), built in B-225.** A new DoT used to tick when it landed and again at each of the holder's turn ends, so a 2-turn DoT from an enemy ticked 3 times. Now every payout pays at the holder's turn start, exactly N times, as the deckbuilder says ("a damage over time ticks exactly N times", `wor-deckbuilder/docs/reference/04-effects.md` "Lasts"; ET-33).
- **C4-5 — An Emerald "bonus" turn is borrowed.** A pinned turn counts as taken with no credit, so it costs a later normal turn (`Private/Combat/TurnManager.cpp` `AdvanceSimState`). Extra Action, by contrast, adds a real turn (`RequestExtraTurn`). The deckbuilder's `AddTurn` adds a turn (`wor-deckbuilder/docs/reference/04-effects.md` verb 7). **Ruled (D-052): A**, a real extra turn; not built yet, so ET-14 stays a conflict.
- **C4-6 — What Silenced blocks.** The code blocks only refractions (`Private/Combat/Actions/ActionExecutor.cpp:176-180`). The enum says "Silenced (No EP Spend)" (`Public/Skills/Effects/ESkillEffectType.h:129`), `docs/archive/Mechanics/Status/StatusEffects.md:25, 42` says no spells, abilities or infusion, and D-023's context says infusion is "the EP-spend layer that Silenced already gates". Question, options and recommendation: **D-038** (same question as 03-status-bars.md C3-2). **Ruled (D-038):** a seal of 50% of max EP on every EP spend. **Built in B-148** (1 holder turn since D-090).
- **C4-7 — Same effect from two gear pieces.** Here one definition referenced by two items merges into one effect (`docs/archive/Mechanics/Status/SkillEffects.md:85, 92`; `Private/Skills/Effects/SkillEffectManager.cpp:319-330`). The deckbuilder adds boosts from different cards, even two copies of one face, and refreshes only a repeat from the same card (`wor-deckbuilder/docs/reference/04-effects.md` `Stat` duration row). D-012 uses the same "different source adds" idea for caps. Question, options and recommendation: **D-053**. **Ruled (D-053):** the item is the source; built in B-062 (ET-42).
- **C4-8 — "Turn Speed Up" counts as a debuff.** `ModifyTurnSpeed` is displayed "Turn Speed Up" (`Public/Skills/Effects/ESkillEffectType.h`) and adds speed (`Private/Combat/TurnManager.cpp` `BuildTurnSpeedLayers`). Yet `IsDebuff` lists it (`ESkillEffectType.h`), so cleansing an ally strips it, and Iolite can't strip it from an enemy. **Ruled (D-054), built in B-267** with the Energy Cost modifier (Q-267): the side sets the sign at apply (`SkillEffectManager::ApplyEffect`), on an ally a buff, on an enemy a debuff; card text follows the payload's target (`FSkillEffectPayload::GetClassifyingMagnitude`).
- **C4-9 — Speed Buff/Debuff (generic) don't move turns.** `docs/archive/Mechanics/Combat/TurnOrder.md` says Slow (`SpeedDebuff`) pushes turns back. The scheduler reads only Turn Speed Up/Buff/Debuff (`Private/Combat/TurnManager.cpp` `BuildTurnSpeedLayers`). `SpeedBuff`/`SpeedDebuff` trigger a recalculation (`Private/Skills/Effects/SkillEffectManager.cpp` `IsSpeedEffect`) that changes nothing. **Ruled (D-056): C**, retire them (kept in the enum, refused by data validation); not built yet.
- **C4-10 — TurnManager.md is out of date.** It says pinned turns don't count down other delays and that dead combatants are owed turns (`docs/Architecture/TurnManager.md:62-63, 144-147`). The code says every turn counts down every delay, and the dead are owed nothing (`Private/Combat/TurnManager.cpp:577-589, 605-618`). Doc fix, no ruling: **fixed in B-117**.

## Gaps
- Flee: no rule for whether you can flee, from which fights, or what it costs (ET-21). **Ruled: D-091** (flee normal fights, not mini-bosses or bosses; at a cost).
- Draw: D-002 covers win and lose but not a draw (both sides down at once, e.g. by Reflect or a DoT). **Ruled (Crown, 2026-10-26, rules review): A, a draw counts as a win.** Open: if the **whole** party is down after such a win, proposed that the leader gets back up at 1 HP so the run can continue to a rest spot (confirm with Crown when building).
- Do bonus and Execution turns count as the holder's own turns for durations? D-030 didn't say. **Ruled: D-055**, every turn the holder takes counts. Bonus turns always ran the end-of-turn tick; since B-225 an Execution turn also runs the turn-start step (Q-044d; ET-16, ET-34).
- The turn-order strip isn't rebuilt when speed changes mid-turn (`Private/Combat/TurnManager.cpp` `OnActorSpeedChanged` recalculates the ratios but doesn't call `RebuildBelt`), only at the next turn start.
- HP shields and element wards (D-092, B-042j / B-042j2): Barrier (any damage), Shield (physical) and Ward (one element, every element, or everything) negate damage after Guard and Defense, on every damage instance including a damage-over-time tick; what they stop counts as a guard for On Guard effects; a hit they stop entirely is a hit, not damage. A skill or item gives one through the Negate … effect types (Value = damage negated, Charges = uses, Duration = turns). The deckbuilder has the same family (`wor-deckbuilder/docs/reference/04-effects.md` "the absorbing family"); the order against Guard and Defense is ours (Q-044e). `GAS/WoRAbsorb.h`, `Tests/AbsorbSpec.cpp`.
- Defend's 50% and 1 turn are inline numbers, with no named constant to tune them by.
- No turn or round limit on a fight. **Ruled (Crown, 2026-10-26, rules review): later, as a setting.** A soft limit (after N rounds both sides take growing damage, "sudden death") as an option where **players set the max rounds** (or off); **not built for now**.
