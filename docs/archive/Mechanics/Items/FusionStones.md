# Fusion Stones

**Status:** Live. Player-facing reference.

## What they are

A **fusion stone** is **player-created**. You don't find or loot one — you **make** it by **fusing
two attachables you already own** into a single new stone. The fused stone then attaches like any
other.

## What can fuse

The rule of thumb: a valid fusion is **one stat stone plus one other contributor**.

**Allowed:**

- a **stat stone + an elemental crystal** (you get the stat bonus *and* the crystal's element/spells)
- a **stat stone + another stat stone** (both stat bonuses)
- a **stat stone + an ability stone** (the stat bonus *and* the extra ability slots)

**Not allowed:**

- **two crystals** (element + element — nothing to anchor the fusion)
- **two ability stones** (two slot-grants, nothing to combine)
- **an ability stone + a crystal** (slots + element, no stat stone)
- **anything + an evolution crystal** — an **evolution crystal can never be fused**. Evolution is a
  transformation, not an ingredient.

## What you get

The fused stone carries **both halves' effects** — you keep everything each ingredient gave —
**plus a fusion bonus** to a stat you choose at fusion time.

The fusion bonus **scales with the tiers of the two halves**: two low-tier halves give **little or
no bonus**, while two top-tier halves give the **biggest** bonus. (A bottom-tier pairing is
effects-only — the value is in keeping both halves' effects on one slot.)

A fusion stone is **attach-only** — there's no consumable form. Identical fusions **stack** as the
same item (the order you fused the two halves doesn't matter).

## Fusion stones on rings

A ring accepts an **elemental fusion** (a stat stone fused with a crystal): the crystal supplies the
ring's element and spells (D-009, CL-28). A **bare stat stone**, an **ability stone** or a **two-stone
fusion** stays **weapon-only** (a ring has no weapon damage and no use for ability slots).
