# PIE — B-400: target cut with several enemies

**Branch:** `feature/B-400-target-cut` (in dev, built on the desktop 2026-11-12 from the cloud's `feature/B-400-target-cut-cloud`; going back to the turn view keeps B-405's low height against a boss or mini-boss). No save change.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.Camera` "target cut with several enemies (B-400, study R3 / §9, Q-400)": every target's feet, head and HP-bar point are on screen in all three styles (three enemies, an ally, two acting positions, 180 and 400 cm tall); one target gives the turn view itself; Over the Shoulder is a front shot, centred, larger than in the turn view, a cut; Centred Wide keeps its place and re-aims with a short blend; cycling wraps both ways. `WoR.Combat.Camera.Director`: with two enemies Attack's picker highlights the first and the camera follows; each cycle is one move and wraps; cancel goes back to the turn view; movement off holds still.
**This is a feel check:** does picking among enemies feel like Expedition 33's field fight (each press cuts to a clear front shot of that enemy, its HP bar readable; confirm cuts to the skill)? Numbers are first guesses (`TODO: tune`, `CombatCameraTargetView` in `CombatCameraTypes.cpp`: `MediumShotFill` 0.6, `HealthBarHeadroom` 0.25, `MediumShotSide` 0.35, `MediumShotRise` 0.15, `MediumShotMinDistance` 250, `ReaimFraction` 0.35, `ReaimBlendSeconds` 0.15; `CombatCameraTargetShape::DefaultTargetHeight` 180 in `CombatCameraDirector.cpp`).
**Setup:** a fight against **two or three** enemies, then one against **one** enemy. Start in Over the Shoulder (`wor.Camera.Style 1`), then Centred Wide (`wor.Camera.Style 0`). Output Log filtered on `[CombatCamera]`.
