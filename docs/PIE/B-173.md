# PIE — B-173: Energy refills at each turn start (D-089)

**Branch:** `feature/B-173-turn-start-energy-cloud` (cloud pre-write, UNBUILT, from `dev`).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Energy.TurnStart` (a share of Max EP; at least the minimum; never past Max EP; Broken Darkness and a dead holder gain none).

**Setup:** pull the branch (built), reopen the editor, start a trial fight with a Caster, a Resonator with a weapon, and (if you have one) a Broken Darkness character.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At the start of the fight, `wor.Energy.Print` | Each combatant: `EP x/y; turn start offers +N (10% of Max EP, at least 1)`; Broken Darkness shows `refused: Broken Darkness (absorption only)` | [ ] |
| 2 | Spend some EP with the Caster, end the turn, wait for its next turn | At its next turn start its EP rises by the offer (capped at Max EP) | [ ] |
| 3 | Basic-attack an enemy and Perfect Guard a hit (no gear that grants EP) | No EP gained from either | [ ] |
| 4 | Equip gear with an effect that restores EP on attack (if any is authored), repeat 3 | EP gained from the gear effect only | [ ] |
| 5 | Watch the Broken Darkness character's turn starts | No turn-start EP; absorption still works | [ ] |
| 6 | Over a few rounds: does EP feel too fast or too slow? | Note it for tuning `TURN_START_EP_SHARE_OF_MAX` | [ ] |
