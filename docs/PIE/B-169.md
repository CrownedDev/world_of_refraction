# PIE — B-169: Luck dodge (D-085)

**Branch:** `feature/B-169-luck-dodge` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Defense.LuckDodge` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - the chance is capped at 15%, scales with Luck, and reaches the cap at full Luck
  - gear never takes it above 15%, and no or cursed Luck never dodges
  - on a fixed battle seed the dodge rate over 20000 rolls matches the chance
  - the same seed replays the same dodges

**What changed (D-085):**
- A hit that would land (you pressed nothing, or missed the timing) can still be dodged by luck: Luck x 15%, at most **15%**.
- A lucky dodge is a full Dodge: no damage and no status.
- It shows "Lucky dodge!" as a yellow on-screen message (a placeholder until the HUD has floating text).
- Open details (curve, triggers, enemies): **Q-169**.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a fight against an enemy that attacks. A character with high Luck makes it easier to see (at full Luck about 1 hit in 7).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Let several enemy hits land without pressing anything | Now and then (more often with high Luck) a hit does no damage; "Lucky dodge! (<name>)" shows in yellow and the Output Log reads "[LuckDodge] Lucky dodge! ... (chance x%)" with x at most 15 | [ ] |
| 2 | Guard or Dodge a hit with good timing | No lucky dodge message: luck only rolls for hits that would land | [ ] |
| 3 | Note the `[BattleRandom]` seed logged at combat start, set `wor.Battle.Seed <that seed>` and replay the same fight with the same presses | The same hits are lucky-dodged | [ ] |
| 4 | Optional: a character with no Luck | Never lucky-dodges | [ ] |
