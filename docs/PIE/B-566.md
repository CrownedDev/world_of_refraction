# PIE — B-566: the trial pool, stage 2 (D-225, TrialPoolDesign §2 and §9)

**Branch:** `feature/B-566-trial-pool-items` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). Nothing new on screen; the check is a console print. Not seen in PIE yet.

**What it is:**
- A trial names its pool: `Pool` on the trial data asset (new tag `Source.Trial.Fire`). A zone's own Loot Pool still overrides it inside that zone.
- Evolutions are in the pool (read, named, and given by a chest). Apparel is listed; handing it out comes with drops (stage 5).
- Crystals: every trial holds every element's crystal (Garnet, Sapphire, Citrine, Emerald, Amber, Opal, Onyx, Amethyst, Iolite; B-566b), leaning to the trial's element: 3 times as often, and likelier at a higher tier. Not tagged: the pool holds them all.
- Tiers by zone: zone 0 F to E, 1 F to D, 2 F to C, 3 F to B, 4 F to A.
- Chests: a chest now skips anything it can't give (apparel, until stage 5), so it never sticks on "No room". An evolution tagged into a chest's pool can now come out of it; adding items to a pool changes which item a replayed seed gives.
- Nothing rolls from it yet (stage 4 the enemies, stage 5 the drops); chests roll from their zone's pool as before.

**Defaults chosen (Crown may change, Q-566):** every element's crystal, Quartz out (B-566b); the lean numbers 3 and +1 per tier.

**Editor steps:**
1. Open the Fire trial's data asset; under **Pool** set `Source.Trial.Fire`. Save.
2. Add `Source.Trial.Fire` to **Source Tags** on the clean set (TrialPoolDesign example 5): Arming Sword, Round Shield, Hand Axe, Bearded Axe; the Adapter Ring; the Phoenix Fire Ring prebuilt find; the skills Lunge, Heavy Strike, Thrust, Quick Strike, Wrath Cut, Whirlwind, Cross Cut, Smash, Bolt, Missile, Lance, Wave, Storm, Might, Warding. Do **not** tag Gale Force (the merchant's own). Save all.
3. Leave each zone's **Loot Pool** empty unless a zone should give something else (an empty one takes the trial's pool).
