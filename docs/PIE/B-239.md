# PIE — B-239: buy item slots at the hub

**Branch:** `feature/B-239-item-slot-upgrades` (in dev, built 2026-11-12; cloud pre-write `-cloud`).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Inventory.ItemSlotUpgrade`: the price rises with every slot (200, 300, …) up to the cap of 20; a bought slot lets a pouch carry 11 item types with none sent to storage; buying is refused when too poor, at the cap or during a run, and nothing is spent then.
**Setup:** in the hub, with some hub Gold (`wor.Wallet.Add` or a finished run; hub Gold is `ECurrencyType::Prisms`, shown as Gold). Placeholder screen: open the console and type **`wor.UI.ItemSlots`** (Q-239, Crown: the Jeweler's service; the screen's title reads "Jeweler: item slots"; the real vendor screen replaces it, D-024). `wor.Inventory.PrintSlots` lists each pouch's slots.
