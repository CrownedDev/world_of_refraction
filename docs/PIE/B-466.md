# PIE — B-466: the Gate screen

**Branch:** `feature/B-466-gate-screen` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.GateScreen` (5 cases): a trial row shows its name and line and never its boss; a locked trial shows "???" with how it unlocks and opens once its quest is finished; the three difficulty cards (Hard pays more); Enter refuses a locked or missing trial and a gate with no level; an AI seat shows nothing for who plays it; the keys (first open trial picked, a locked one refused, the difficulty steps and wraps, RB wraps the gates).
**What changed:** the hub's trial door opens **The Gate** in place of the plain-button panel (still used if the screen can't open). Top: one tab per gate (every trial asset with a map). Left: the gate's trials in a list (a locked one is "???" with its unlock line), then Easy / Medium / Hard with enemy damage and payout. Right: who's going in, the wipes allowed, and **Enter the trial**. Each trial in the editor has two new fields: **Unlock Quest Id** (locked until that quest is finished) and **Unlock Hint** (the line shown while locked). Decisions: **Q-466** (reference-backed default).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** the look against the mockup; the tabs when there are many gates; that Back from the party / loadout screen returns to the Gate.
**Left out (noted for later):** the hub gate's glow taking the last gate's colour (a visual, editor step); the Party Combat Tier line (the hub has no fighters to read it from yet); "{friend} is ready" in co-op (no ready step exists); removing extra trial doors from the hub map if there are several (editor step: each door opens the same Gate anyway).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, play in the hub.
