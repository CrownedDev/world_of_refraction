# PIE — B-170f: action pips 6, the AI spends or banks

**Branch:** `feature/B-170f-ai-actions-cloud` (cloud, UNBUILT, on `feature/B-170d-action-effects-cloud`).
**Proven by tests once built:** `WoR.AI.ActionPips` (the spend-or-bank rule branch by branch, affordability, the worth of the action effects, an AI with 2 actions striking twice with the switch on and once with it off, an AI with nothing worth doing ending its turn and keeping its action). Re-run unchanged: `WoR.AI.OneSkillPath`, `WoR.AI.DamageEstimate`, `WoR.AI.StatusQueries`, `WoR.Effects.VoidRandomAction`, `WoR.Turns.MultiAction`, `WoR.Combat.ActionEffects`, `WoR.Balance.Sim.OneFight`.

**With the switch off (default) nothing changes:** the AI acts once a turn, as before. With it on, the AI is asked again while it has actions and ends its turn itself. There are no pips on screen yet (B-170e): use `wor.Actions.Print`.

**Setup:** type `wor.Turns.ActionPips 1` in the console **before** touching an enemy team (the switch is read when the fight starts). For the AI's reasons, show Verbose AI lines: `log LogTemp Verbose`, then filter the Output Log on `[AIDecisionManager]`.

**Known:** the AI's spend / bank reason is in the Verbose log and `UAIDecisionManager::GetLastSpendOrBankString()`, not on screen. The AI gives an Emerald only to itself or the foe, not to an ally (as before). The break's action loss is valued from the bar's pending trigger, as the status score reads it.
