# PIE — B-382: Amp lines (Q-164b A)

**Branch:** `feature/B-382-amp-lines-cloud` (on `docs/damage-defence-sources-cloud`; written in the cloud, **not built yet**).
**Proven by tests once built:** `WoR.Damage.Amp` (AmpSpec), plus the existing `WoR.Damage`, `WoR.Weapon.RequirementFactor`, `WoR.Loadout.GearVisitor` and AI damage-estimate specs (their numbers must not change: no gear has Amp yet).
**Setup:** build, open the editor. Pick one weapon asset your party uses (e.g. the starter sword) and, in its Details panel, set **Bonuses → Amp → Fire = 3** and **All = 2** (BaseStatBonus). A weapon copies its asset's lines when it is **acquired**, so use a **new save** (the save version moves to 30, which wipes old saves anyway) or pick up a fresh copy. Start a trial and equip that weapon on a Generic or Caster who can cast a Fire spell and one other element.
