# PIE — B-568: the trial pool, stage 4: the roll (D-225, TrialPoolDesign §5)

**Branch:** `feature/B-568-enemy-kit-roll` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). **Built unseen:** not played yet.

**What it is:** in a trial, each enemy now fights with a kit rolled from the trial's pool, inside its kit (its always-has part and limits, B-567), instead of its authored inventory.
- The kit is the same each time you meet that fight in this run (a retry or a resume gives the same kits). Two enemies of one type in a fight get different open parts; their always-has part is the same.
- Order: the always-has pieces, then more from the pool up to its limits (unset: its class's), a crystal in each adapter (weapons stay empty), then skills its gear can use (always-has first, at most 3 unless its kit says otherwise, never past its slots).
- A fight with no pool (outside a trial) is as before: the authored inventory.
- `wor.Trial.PrintEnemyKits` prints every encounter on the map with each enemy's kit for this run (each encounter as if fought alone; a fight that catches two teams rolls with both, so it can differ).

**Defaults chosen (Crown may change, Q-568):** the seed; the open part fills every place its limits leave; crystals in adapters only; where the pieces are worn. Shortcuts: evolutions and apparel are not rolled yet; a co-op guest's view of an enemy is not rolled yet (B-230).

**Editor steps:** none. (The test trial's pool, `Source.Trial.Fire`, and the Generic enemy's kit were set in 5d354692d.)
