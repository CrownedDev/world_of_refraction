# PIE — B-581: fight card text and inspect

**Branch:** `feature/B-581-card-text-inspect`.
**What changed (D-254, D-259, Q-581):**
- No card shows "Spirit of ..." or "Body of ..." any more: the fight's cards, the hub's and the trial's cards, and the game menu's party list. The Character sheet still shows both.
- The fight card's actions line is pips with no numbers (D-259): "Act: ●●○", a filled pip for each action held and an empty one up to Max. Each action above Max is a ◆: "Act: ●●●◆". Once a skill is used this turn it adds "· Skills 1/3".
- A left click on a party card in a fight inspects that character: the Status panel shows it alone, with its Spirit / Body lines, the full actions line (pips, Max, the action-point progress, the bonus warning) and its statuses.
- The same card again closes inspect. Another party card switches to it. The Status button (top centre) shows everyone.
- D-257's status bar is unchanged.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.BattleHUD`, `WoR.Combat.ActionPipsText`, `WoR.UI.HubHUD`, `WoR.UI.PauseMenu` and the full suite, headless. Not proven there: that a real click reaches the card, what the panel looks like, the screens themselves.
**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`, "How an agent plays"). Play on the QA save folder only.
**Setup:** a party of two or more, the played character one made on the new-game screen (so it has Spirit / Body lines). `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`. `RunConsoleCommand(Command="DisableAllScreenMessages")` before each screenshot.
**To know:** inspect is mouse only for now (a pad route comes with B-436h). The panel takes no clicks, so clicking empty ground does not close it.
