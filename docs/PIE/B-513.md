**Branches:** `feature/B-513-wheel-piece-names`, `feature/B-513b-joined-piece-names` (checks 6-7; both in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** from Crown's PIE of the wheel (which weapon was picked couldn't be told):
- The wheel's **weapon slices show the weapon's own name** ("Arming Sword"). Adapters, evolutions and "Innate" already showed their names.
- The **centre and the strip keep the type and result**, now as held: a sword and shield reads "Sword and Shield" ("Fire Sword and Shield"), a dual or same-type pair reads "Dual Axes".
