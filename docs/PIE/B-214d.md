# PIE — B-214d: field skills part 4 (flight)

**Branch:** `feature/B-214d-flight-cloud` (cloud, **unbuilt**: build first; it carries B-214b and B-214c).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Field.Flight` (start refusals, flying mode, EP up front and each second, landing at 0 EP, unlimited flight free, the ceiling held unless lifted, off on a second use or a new pick). Not provable headless: a real `Zone.NoFly` volume, the hover feel, co-op.
**Setup:** pull the branch, build. **Editor step for check 6:** in a trial map, add any volume (e.g. a Trigger Volume) over an area and give it the actor tag `Zone.NoFly`. Play with a leader that has an element; `wor.Field.Unlock Flight <element>`, `wor.Field.Set Innate Flight`, and make sure it has 10+ EP.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `wor.Field.Activate` | The character lifts into flying; 2 EP at once; `wor.Field.Print` shows `flight ON` | [ ] |
| 2 | Let go of all keys | It slows to a stop and hovers in place | [ ] |
| 3 | Watch EP for a few seconds | 2 EP a second, hovering or moving | [ ] |
| 4 | Fly straight up | It stops about 15 m above where it took off and can't go higher | [ ] |
| 5 | Let EP run out | It falls when EP can't pay another second | [ ] |
| 6 | Fly into the `Zone.NoFly` volume; then stand inside it and `wor.Field.Activate` | Entering: it falls (`entered a no-fly zone`); inside: `NoFlyZone` refused | [ ] |
| 7 | `wor.Field.FlightLimits All`, then repeat 3, 4 and 6 | No EP spent, no ceiling, the no-fly zone doesn't stop it; `wor.Field.FlightLimits None` puts the limits back | [ ] |
| 8 | `wor.Field.Activate` while flying | Flight off; it falls | [ ] |
| 9 | 2-player PIE: the guest flies and hovers | It flies on both screens without being pulled back to the ground | [ ] |
