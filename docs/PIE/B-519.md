# PIE — B-519: the built tier, rings and weapons (D-224, asset overhaul stage 2b)

**Branch:** `feature/B-519-built-tier` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is:** stage 2b of `Design/AssetOverhaulPlan.md` (§4, §4.1). A piece's **built tier** is its own tier and its socket's averaged, a half rounded down (F0 … S6). An F ring with a B gem is D; an S ring with an F gem is C. An empty socket keeps the piece's own tier.
- **Rings:** only the skill half reads the built tier: the spell slots, the spells' damage (the tier gap) and their wear. **The ring's stat lines stay on its own tier.**
- **Weapons:** only the elemental side reads it. A plain hit and its build-up stay on the weapon's own tier. An infused hit splits: the plain part on the weapon's tier, the infusion's extra share on the built tier; its status build-up reads the built tier (there is one status bar, Q-519).
- **Combat Tier** counts each weapon and adapter at its built tier.
- **A skill's power** reads your own copy's tier (the S3 default; it matters once stage S3 drops tiers).
- **The Equipment detail** gains a "Skill tier" line with its parts, for example "D (F + B)", while the socket holds a gem or an evolution.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Loadout.AdapterTier` (the average and rounding; an F ring with a B Citrine has D's slots, F's once unsocketed; an F weapon with a B Garnet: a D ability's elemental side is matched, its physical side reads F; a ring spell's channel is the built tier); `WoR.Loadout.Rules` (an S ring with an F evolution lists C's count); `WoR.Combat.BrokenSources`. Full suite 2247 passed, 0 failed.

**Not proven:** the damage numbers and build-up in a real fight, the wear amount on a real cast, Combat Tier on the HUD, and the detail line's look. These are seen only in PIE.

**Known limits:**
- A weapon crystal's own infusion wear, its spell slots, Crystal Amp, durability, prices, dismantle and repair keep their old tiers (the plan's "keep" rows).
- The evolution seat's spells read the evolution's own tier, unchanged.
- **Older rings now hold fewer spells** when their gem was above the ring (an F ring with a B gem drops from 5 slots to 3). Picks past the new count are hidden, not deleted.

**No editor step.** Use the stage 2 rings (`DA_Ring_Adapter` spares), the sword and the crystal stock.
