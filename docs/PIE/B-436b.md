# PIE — B-436b: the active card (piece 2)

**Branch:** `feature/B-436b-active-card`.
**What changed (`Design/BattleHUDRefinePlan.md` decision 2, piece 2; Q-436b):**
- **No tier letter on a party card's name** (Crown, 2026-11-23, after QA 1: "We don't need the tier on names"). The "(C)" built first is gone from the full and the small cards. An enemy's tier stays at the bottom of its target marker (B-436c).
- The HP bar is a **darker red** and the EP bar a **darker blue**. The numbers on the bars are white with a **thin dark outline** (no shadow, no box behind them).
- Under **30% HP** a living party member's HP bar turns a **hot pink-red and pulses**, dimming and brightening once a second. It stops as soon as they are healed past 30%. A downed card doesn't pulse.
- Already there before this piece: the numbers sit inside the bars (B-503m), and the build-up bar is on every card (B-585).
- **Not built on purpose:**
  - no class line under the name, since D-254 removed it from fight cards;
  - no status icons on a party card, since D-261 put them in Intel.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.BattleHUD` covers:
- low HP only under 30% and only while alive;
- the pulse is full at each second and dimmest half-way;
- on a duel, the hero's card has no tier letter by its name; it turns low under 30% (`wor.UI.Print` says so) and stops on a heal or a fall.

**Not proven there:** the look (colours, outline, the pulse on screen) at both window sizes.

**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`).
- Play on the QA save folder only.
- Run every check at `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`, then repeat 1 and 3 at `SetWindowSize(Width=1920, Height=1080)`.
- `wor.UI.Print` prints each card as `party <Name> ... (low HP, pulsing)`.
