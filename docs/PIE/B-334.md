# PIE — B-334: infusion costs (D-126)

**Branch:** `feature/B-334-infusion-costs` (in `dev`; written in a cloud session, built and tested on the desktop).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Infusion.Cost` (updated): EP = the base × the charge level (x1 at L0 / L1, x2 at L2); an infused ability keeps D-035's x1.5; no stat surcharge; the HP cost is 0 at L1 and charged at L2.
**Setup:** build the branch, start a trial fight with a Caster (innate spells) and a Generic (Raw infusion on an ability). Watch the EP number in the command menu and the HP bar.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick an innate spell, infusion L0, then L1 | The EP cost shown is the **same** at L0 and L1 | [ ] |

| 6 | Infuse through an adapter or weapon crystal at L1 / L2 | Still no EP; the crystal wears as before | [ ] |
| 7 | The source picker | Still offered, same options as before (D-126 keeps it) | [ ] |
| 8 | Let an enemy AI fight at low HP | It may still infuse at L1 (no HP risk) but never kills itself with an L2 | [ ] |
