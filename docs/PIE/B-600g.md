# PIE — B-600g: the turn camera like Expedition 33

**Branch:** `feature/B-600g-shoulder-turn-view` (built 2026-11-24, off `fix/B-604d-defend-camera-wider` with `fix/B-600f-target-view-and-hold-home` merged in). Ruling: Q-600g.
**Already proven by tests:** build OK; full suite 2595 passed, 0 failed (camera and settings specs 124); data validation 0 new errors (on 5d32621eb).
**Setup:** the QA save (slot 0), the test trial's fight. You need two party members, a single-target skill, a character with banked actions (2 or more actions in a turn), and an enemy that attacks one character.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Settings, Gameplay: the turn view row | Over the shoulder, without having picked it | [ ] |
| 2 | Start the fight; your first character's turn | Behind your character at about chest height, over the right shoulder: the character in the left third, the whole enemy in the middle | [ ] |
| 3 | Use a single-target skill and end the turn | After the hit, a hard cut to a low wide view from behind the party; the attacker walks home in it; then a fast sweep (about a third of a second) into the next character's over-the-shoulder view. From the hit's end to the settled view, about 1 s (log: `[CombatCamera] Hand-over wide: ... sweep`) | [ ] |
| 4 | With banked actions, use one action but don't end the turn | The same cut to the wide, then the sweep goes back over the same character's shoulder and the menu opens again; the turn strip does not move | [ ] |
| 5 | End a turn when an enemy acts next | The wide, then no sweep: a slow push-in toward the enemy over about 2.5 s (log: `... push-in`) | [ ] |
| 6 | The enemy attacks one character | B-604d's side view of both (unchanged) | [ ] |
| 7 | After the enemy's attack, your next turn | The wide, then the sweep into your character's view | [ ] |
| 8 | Settings: pick Centred Wide, then another fight | Centred Wide is kept now that you picked it | [ ] |

**Notes / what went wrong:**
- Known: an enemy's walk home is not shown on the wide; the wide comes when the next turn starts.
- Known: no motion blur on the sweep yet.
- Known: the menu opens when the game asks for the action, not timed to the end of the sweep; say if it shows too early.
- On a boss, check the push-in does not clip into it or cut it off.
- Every timing is one named constant in `CombatCameraTypes.h` (`WideMinSeconds`, `SweepSeconds`, `EnemyPushInSeconds`, `EnemyPushInFraction`, `ActionEndHoldSeconds`); say which feels off.
