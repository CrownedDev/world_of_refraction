# PIE — B-064a: Ice, a Water damage type

**Branch:** `feature/B-064a-ice-cloud` (cloud pre-write, UNBUILT: no engine in the writing session; stacked on `feature/B-273b-hit-kind-cloud`, build and merge that first).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Elements.Ice` (an Ice spell fits a Water ring, not a Fire one; the flag on a non-Water spell is invalid; On Water hit hears Ice; Water resistance doesn't reach an Ice hit; Ice resistance resists Ice and Water; a full Ice bar slows Action and Spell Speed and deals 10% max HP; Water's break deals none; Water immunity blocks Ice), plus B-273b's specs and unchanged: `WoR.Status.BreakTable`, `WoR.Effects.ElementBreakNames`, `WoR.GameNames`, `WoR.EffectText`, `WoR.GAS.EffectMirror`, `WoR.Effects.Keyed`.

**Setup:** there is no Ice spell asset yet. In the editor, open any **Water** spell data asset (or duplicate one, named per `docs/Conventions/ContentNaming.md`) and tick **Identity > Deals Ice**. Give it to a Refractor with a Water ring or crystal.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tick Deals Ice on a **Fire** spell and run Data Validation on it | An error: "Deals Ice needs a Water spell (this one is ESpellElement::Fire)". Untick it again | [ ] |
| 2 | Try to slot the Ice spell into a **Fire** ring | Refused, as any Water spell is. A **Water** ring takes it | [ ] |
| 3 | In a fight, cast the Ice spell at an enemy; `wor.StatusBars.Print` | The enemy's bar line reads `pending ESpellElement::Water / EPhysicalDamageType::None -> EWoRDamageType::Ice` | [ ] |
| 4 | Keep casting it until the enemy's bar fills | The break shows as "Ice break" (placeholder name, Q-064a2); the enemy takes about 10% of its max HP at once and its next attack / spell plays slower for one of its turns, as after a Water break | [ ] |
| 5 | Fill another enemy's bar with a plain Water spell | "Frozen", the same slow, **no** extra damage | [ ] |
| 6 | `wor.Resistance.Compare` on a combatant | An `Ice` line (0 unless tuned) | [ ] |
| 7 | Equip gear with a **Water Amp** line, cast the plain Water spell and the Ice spell (Verbose `LogWoRCombat`, `[Amp]` lines) | The Water spell's Amp includes the Water line; the Ice spell's doesn't (All and Spell lines still count) | [ ] |
| 8 | `wor.Effects.UseGAS 1`, repeat 3-5 | The same numbers and breaks | [ ] |
| 9 | `wor.Breaks.Print` | An `IceBreak -> ActionSpeedDebuff percent 30.00, ... also SpellSpeedDebuff, deals 0.10 of max HP` row | [ ] |

**Known:** the bar tint and the impact VFX still follow the key (Water) for an Ice hit; Ice-keyed resistance buffs and gear, Water damage buffs reaching Ice, and the AI's estimate by hit kind are B-064c. The class table's Ice column is 0 for every profile until tuned. Both Ice break sizes are named constants (`ICE_BREAK_SPEED_SLOW_PERCENT`, `ICE_BREAK_DAMAGE_MAX_HP_FRACTION`), `// TODO: tune`.
