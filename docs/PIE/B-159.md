# PIE — B-159: invalid picks refused, invalid parts dropped

**Branch:** `feature/B-159-refuse-or-drop` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Loadout.Picks` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - a Spear ability on a Sword is refused with a reason, and the loadout is unchanged
  - a Sword ability on a Sword is assigned
  - more abilities than the weapon allows are trimmed to the cap, and the dropped part is named
  - utility crystals over a tier band's cap are trimmed, in every loadout, not just the active one
  - a repeat pick, or a pick into a weapon or ring the loadout isn't using, is refused
  - an empty (dropped) ability entry is filled first and never costs a real ability
  - the drop is announced once per loadout, naming the removed ability or crystal

**What changed (D-081 = A):**
- A pick that is itself invalid is **refused** with the rule's reason. There is no loadout screen yet, so try it from the console: `wor.Loadout.TryAbility <ability name>`.
- When a character's loadouts **load**, their invalid parts are **dropped** at once, with a "Removed: ..." message each, instead of at the next fight.
- Before a fight, the cleaner now also trims too many abilities on a weapon, item quantities above 3, and utility crystals over the tier caps (B-155).
- Not yet: a gear change (swapping a weapon's crystal) doesn't drop what it breaks until the fight starts. Loadouts keep copies of gear, so that waits on B-085 (B-171).
- Dropped utility crystals return to the inventory (Q-159 = A, added later); see check 6.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and filter it on `LogWoRLoadout`. The load-time drop message fires before any UI exists, so for now it only shows there.
