# PIE — B-440c: the loadout screen, piece 3 (the socket picker)

**Branch:** `feature/B-440c-loadout-socket-picker` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/loadout/2_socket_picker.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.LoadoutScreen` (15 cases; 2 new): an empty crystal socket offers the carried gems (highest tier first) and the owned evolutions, the stone socket only carried stones, each with the budget after it; a second S-tier and an evolution in use stay with their reason; a filled socket offers nothing; X says what comes off before it does it.
**What changed:** Left / Right on a weapon or adapter row lights its crystal socket, then its stone socket (the socket's label turns lilac). A on an empty socket opens the socket picker; A puts the lit gem, stone or evolution in. X on a filled socket first says what will happen, and a second X does it: **a gem and the stone come off together and are used up** (the existing rule, CL-28b); an evolution goes back to your evolutions with some wear. Decisions: **Q-440c** (reference-backed default; whether taking a gem off should give it back is yours to change).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** the look, the keys and gamepad; that the gem leaves the pouch.
**Left out:** the mouse can't pick a socket yet (a click on a row is A on the row); apparel's socket (B-440f); the mockup's Compare panel (Element / Tier / Budget before and after): the detail panel shows the line's details and "Budget after"; Y Sort.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor. A member whose pouch has a gem and a stone (rest-spot or chest finds, or the vendor), and a weapon with an empty socket.
