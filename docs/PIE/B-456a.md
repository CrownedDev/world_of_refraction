# PIE — B-456a: the vendor screen, piece 1 (the screen, Sell and Dismantle)

**Branch:** `feature/B-456-vendor-screen` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.VendorScreen`: each vendor's service tabs (D-193) and which are built; a Sell row's name, kind and pay; a loadout's primary listed greyed with its reason; the screen opens on the first built tab; the first A asks and the second A sells; Back cancels the question; a refused row says why and asks nothing; changing the tab drops a pending question; X sells the whole stack. The services' own rules: `WoR.Shop.VendorServices` (B-445).
**What changed (D-193, D-181):** the real vendor screen, laid out as the mockups in `docs/Design/screen_mockups/hub/vendors/`: the vendor's name and the money on top, its services as tabs, the character tabs, a row per thing the service takes, and a panel for the focused row. **Sell** and **Dismantle** work on it. The other tabs (Buy, Item slots, Repair, Curses, Merge) are listed and say they aren't on this screen yet; they come next (B-456b onwards).
**How to open it for now:** the console. Talking to the vendor still opens the old shop window until Buy is on this screen (B-456b).
**Left out:** icons (a colour strip shows the element), the second essence a dismantle shows in the mockup.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, PIE in the hub with a party that owns a spare weapon and some crystals.
