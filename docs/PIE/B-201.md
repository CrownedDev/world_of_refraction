# PIE — B-201: a failed item use spends its charge

**Branch:** `fix/B-201-failed-use-spends-charge` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Items.Use` checks, on a real battle loadout (an empty item slot, then two Garnets), that a use that lands and a use that fails each spend one charge, and that the failed one still reports failure with its reason.

**What changed:** Crown changed Q-060. A use that can't land now loses its charge, like a thrown item that misses, and the turn ends. Before (B-060) it kept the charge. Also fixed: an item that sat behind an empty item slot spent its charge from the wrong slot, or not at all.

**Setup:** a fight with a consumable crystal or stone in a later item slot, with an empty slot before it if you can arrange it. Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use a healing or damage item normally | It works as before; its charges go down by one; Output Log "Consumed 1 use from item slot" | [ ] |
| 2 | Use the item that sits after an empty item slot | Its own charge count goes down by one, not another slot's | [ ] |
| 3 | If you ever see "[ItemExecutor] ... failed: <reason>" in the Output Log | The charge count still drops by one and the turn moves on (the turn ending is not re-tested by the spec; it was already so in B-060) | [ ] |
| 4 | Let an AI member with items play a few turns | It doesn't throw items at targets they can't land on (that would now waste charges) | [ ] |
