# PIE — B-042: GAS stage 3, part 1 (Stun GE + turn tracker)

**Branch:** `feature/B-042-gas-turn-tracker` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.GAS.TurnTracker` (7 cases) checks:
- the native Stun GameplayEffect gives the `Status.Stun` tag and takes it away when removed;
- the tracker counts the holder's turns by the one rule (an effect put on during the holder's own turn counts that turn; it ends as the holder's next turn after the counted ones starts);
- a GE removed some other way leaves the tracker;
- spans under 1 turn and non-lasting GEs are refused;
- the effect manager's turn start and end drive it, also on a real combat character.

The full suite (639) is green.

What changed:
- Every combat character now carries a **Turn Effect Tracker** component. It counts turns for GAS effects, because GAS counts seconds.
- Nothing puts a GAS effect on anyone in play yet; the effects still run on the old system. So the fight should play exactly as before.
- The rest of GAS stage 3 is queued as B-042b to B-042g.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor.
