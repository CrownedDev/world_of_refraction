# PIE — B-313b: the rest of the pouch move (Q-313, Q-313b)

**Branch:** `feature/B-313b-pouch-rest` (in `dev`; built on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**What changed:** (1) every count a party member's crystal inventory gives (the shop's room check, dismantle, merge, the inventory summary and dump) now comes straight from the member's **pouch**, not from the old copy of it. (2) A member's preset item picks hold no items since B-313, so the item-slot rules no longer judge them: no "Duplicate item types", "item slot Quantity out of range" or "Too many S-tier crystals" warning comes from a member's item slots, and nothing trims them. An S crystal **attached** to the member's gear still counts toward the one-S rule (D-143). Enemies keep every item rule. Nothing should look different in play except fewer of those warnings.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Pool.PouchItemUse` (the counts come from the pouch even when the old copy is emptied; a member's picks give no item-slot finding while the same picks do for an actor with no pouch; clearing leaves them); the `WoR.Pool` / `Loadout` / `Inventory` / `Items` / `Economy` / `Crystals` / `Combat` / `Give` / `Shop` / `Party` / `Trial` subsets green (481); data validation 0 new.
**Setup:** a trial with your party; a member carrying a few gems and a stone in its pouch (`wor.Inventory.PrintSlots` lists the pouches).
