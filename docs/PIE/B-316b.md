# PIE — B-316b: skills per character, give / request (Q-316, Q-316b)

**Branch:** `feature/B-316b-skills-per-character` (in `dev`; built on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**What changed:** a character's learned spells and abilities are now its own and kept: they used to come back from its character asset at every spawn, so a skill learned or bought was lost on travel. The first time a member is drawn its asset's skills go into the store; after that every spawn loads them from there, and a learn, a forget, a give or a buy changes them there. On the give placeholder (`wor.UI.Give`) the giver's skills are choices too: **Give** moves the skill (one copy: the giver loses it), **Ask for it** has the receiver request it (an AI member or one of your own says yes at once; another player's member waits for the co-op screens). The shop's buy-for now sells spells and abilities to the chosen member (the payer may already know it). Save version 16 (older saves wiped, D-138). A run-only recruit keeps its asset's skills and trades none. Evolutions still can't be bought for another member (no evolution give yet, Q-323).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Pool.Skills` (a learned spell survives a re-spawn; a given spell moves, one copy; a request to an AI member moves the ability; a spell bought for another member is learned by it while the payer keeps its own; the skills survive a save round trip), `WoR.Economy.BuyFor`; the `WoR.Pool` / `Inventory` / `Save` / `Economy` / `Shop` / `Party` / `Loadout` subsets green (320 tests); data validation 0 new.
**Setup:** start PIE in the hub with a party of at least two; `wor.Pool.PrintSkills` lists every character's skills in the store.
