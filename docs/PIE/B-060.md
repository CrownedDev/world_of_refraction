# PIE — B-060: a failed item use keeps its charge

**Branch:** `fix/B-060-item-use-success` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Items.Use` checks that a use that lands reports success, a use that cannot land reports failure with a reason, and an area item counts as used when one target takes it.

**What changed:** before, every item use counted as a success, so an item whose effect could not land still lost a charge. Now only a use that lands spends a charge. The turn still ends either way. Normal item use looks exactly as before.

**Setup:** a fight with at least one consumable crystal or stone in your item slots. Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use a healing or damage item normally | It works as before; its charges go down by one; Output Log "Consumed 1 use from item slot" | [ ] |
| 2 | Use a stat stone on an ally and on an enemy | Buff on the ally, debuff on the enemy; one charge each | [ ] |
| 3 | Use an area item on a group | It lands on the group; one charge is used | [ ] |
| 4 | Use a Healing Stone on a heal-blocked ally | The heal is blocked but the stone is still used (it reached its target). Only a use that cannot reach a valid target keeps its charge; that is rare in play and covered by `WoR.Items.Use`. If you ever see "[ItemExecutor] ... failed: <reason>" in the Output Log, the charge count should not drop and the turn moves on | [ ] |
