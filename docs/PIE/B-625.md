# PIE — B-625: not enough EP shows why

**Branch:** `fix/B-625-not-enough-ep-reason` (built 2026-11-25 from dev 3723c9396).
**What it is:** picking a skill or spell the fighter couldn't pay the EP for was refused on confirm, and the menu dropped back with no message. Now the row is greyed with "Not enough EP (N needed).", the way the Act panel shows other refusals (too few actions, a broken source). In the target step, an infusion level that costs more than the fighter has greys the targets with the same reason; picking a lower level makes them pickable again.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.CommandMenu.Keys` (a skill one EP short is greyed with the reason and pickable at enough EP; a level that can't be paid greys the target and can't be confirmed; level 0 ungreys it). The full suite plus validation on the final tree are in the hand-off.
**Lords at 0 EP (QA save):** not a start-EP bug. Every lord has the same EP stats, and a fighter starts a fight at full EP unless the party record carries EP from an earlier fight of the same run. A member that ended a trial fight at 0 starts the next one at 0 and refills 10% a turn (ET-29); the carry is cleared when the run ends. A Darkness Lord that broke into Broken Darkness stays broken and starts at 0 by design (B-348c). To check the save, type `wor.Save.Print`: it lists each member's saved EP.
**Setup:** the QA save (slot 0), a fight with a Caster.
