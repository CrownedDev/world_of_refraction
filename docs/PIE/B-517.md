# PIE — B-517: the picks live on what is worn (D-224, skills track S1)

**Branch:** `feature/B-517-picks-on-worn-entry` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is:** part 1 of the skills track's first stage (`Design/AssetOverhaulPlan.md` §5.1, "no skills on gear"). The skills you put in a weapon's or a ring's slots used to be stored on the piece itself. Now they are stored on the worn entry: the place in your loadout that holds the piece.
- A preset keeps its own skills for the same sword.
- Giving the sword to someone else doesn't take your skills along.
- The class kit's starting skills go on the kit's worn entry.
- **Old saves are wiped** with a warning (save version 42).

Part 2, B-518, takes the built-in skills off gear. Until then a weapon still shows its built-ins in the slots you haven't changed.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Loadout.WornGear`:
  - picks go to the record and back slot by slot, and the owned piece holds none;
  - two records of one sword keep their own picks;
  - a piece's old picks are ignored;
  - an empty ring takes no spell picks.
- `WoR.Save`: the worn record keeps its picks through a save.
- `WoR.Trial.ClassDefaults`: the kit's skills are on the worn entry.
- `WoR.Loadout.WornGear` and `WoR.Save`: a custom loadout (a preset) keeps its own picks through the pool's form and a save.

**Not proven:** the Equipment screen, presets and giving gear, seen only in PIE.

**Known limits:**
- Swapping a different piece into an entry starts it with empty slots. Before, it brought the skills stored on it.
- In a run, a character whose record names gear left at home doesn't keep edits made there, the same as the rest of that record.

**No editor step.**
