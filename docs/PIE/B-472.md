# PIE — B-472: loadout costs (D-206: the gear is free, only what is in it costs)

**Branch:** `feature/B-472-loadout-costs-cloud` (cloud, UNBUILT: the desktop builds and runs the tests first).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Loadout.ClassLimits`, `WoR.Loadout.Budget`, `WoR.UI.LoadoutScreen`.
**Setup:** build, run `Scripts/ue/test.sh`, start PIE in the hub with a Generic, a Resonator and a Refractor in the party. Open the loadout screen from the game menu.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On a Generic, look at a bare weapon row | Its cost line says **Free** and the budget counts 0 for it | [ ] |
| 2 | Put a crystal on that weapon | The cost line says **Crystal 1**; the budget goes up by 1 | [ ] |
| 3 | Put a stone in its stone socket | The cost line says **Crystal 1 + Stone 1 = 2** | [ ] |
| 4 | Give the Generic two evolved weapons | Both fit (2 + 2 = 4 of 5); no "over the budget" refusal | [ ] |
| 5 | Add a stone to each evolved weapon | The second stone is refused (6 of 5), still listed greyed with the reason | [ ] |
| 6 | On the Refractor, equip an evolved weapon | Budget **2 of 2**; it fits | [ ] |
| 7 | Try to put a stone on that evolved weapon | Refused: over the budget (3 of 2) | [ ] |
| 8 | On the Resonator, add bare adapters one by one | Five fit (each costs 0); a sixth is refused | [ ] |
| 9 | On the new game screen, look at the Resonator line | "Up to five adapters and one weapon. Budget 5." | [ ] |
| 10 | Open an old save whose Refractor had an evolved weapon and a stone | On the next fight start the stone is removed with a "Removed: ..." message and is back in the inventory (not lost, not doubled) | [ ] |
| 11 | Check the pouch capacity (`wor.Inventory.Print` or the pouch screen) | Weapon and adapter pouch costs are as before (bare weapon 1, crystal weapon 2, evolved weapon 3, adapter 1, evolved adapter 2) | [ ] |
