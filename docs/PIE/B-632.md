# PIE — B-632: the Pause menu in two columns

**Branch:** `feature/B-632-pause-v2` (from `dev` 391dbdfdf).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/pause/v2/1_pause_trial.png`.
**Decision:** Q-632 (what Items found and Time count; Character and Body rows gone; no Help toggle; the Equipment note).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.PauseMenu` (page order, This run counts, a save's count and time, the time format, the note), `WoR.UI.JoinMenu`, and the full suite, headless.
**Setup:** the QA save folder only (`-saveddirsuffix=QA`), Play in a new window.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the hub, open the game menu (Tab) | "Paused" with the hub's name under it. Left: Menu, with Resume, Equipment, Inventory, Party, Journal, Settings and (apart) Quit to title, each with a short grey line. No money chips, no party cards | [ ] |
| 2 | Look at the right column | No This run block in the hub. Mind · Body · Spirit: a row per member, the element's badge, name, class, and the three numbers | [ ] |
| 3 | Move down the pages | The lit row is violet; under the line the note's heading is the page's name and its text says what it is. It shows without pressing anything | [ ] |
| 4 | Press H (or View on a pad) | Nothing changes (the Help toggle is gone) | [ ] |
| 5 | A on Equipment, then B | The loadout screen opens over the menu; B comes back to the menu on Equipment | [ ] |
| 6 | Enter a trial, win a fight, open a chest, open the menu | "Paused" with the trial's name. This run: the trial's name, Fights won 1, Run gold (amber) as your Gold, Items found counts the chest's item and the fight's drop, Time in minutes:seconds | [ ] |
| 7 | Close and reopen the menu a minute later | Time has moved on by about a minute | [ ] |
| 8 | Light the last row | It reads Leave trial in amber, "Back to the hub; the run ends"; the note warns the run is lost | [ ] |
| 9 | A on Leave trial, then B | The quit question shows under the note; B drops it and keeps the menu | [ ] |
| 10 | Save at a rest spot, quit to the title, continue | Items found and Time come back as they were at the save, and the time runs on | [ ] |
| 11 | Footer | A Open, B Resume; a click on each does the same | [ ] |
