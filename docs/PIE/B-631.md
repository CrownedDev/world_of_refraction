# PIE — B-631: the settings in three columns (switches, the preview, Details with every choice)

**Branch:** `feature/B-631-settings` (on top of `feature/B-630-journal`).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/settings/v2/` (1 Gameplay, 2 Controls).
**Decision:** Q-631 (what each column shows; A next choice, Y default; Battle first on Controls; a click lights a row, a click on the lit row steps it).
