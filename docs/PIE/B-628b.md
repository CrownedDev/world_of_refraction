# PIE — B-628b: the party seat cards with Mind / Body / Spirit, HP, EP and the weapon

**Branch:** `feature/B-628b-party-seat-vitals` (from `dev` 391dbdfdf).
**Why:** Crown's Pause v2 (`menus/pause/v2`) moved the member cards off the game menu and onto the Party screen.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.PartyScreen` (full and carried HP / EP, the live pillar levels, the weapon line) and the full suite, headless.
**Setup:** the QA save folder only (`-saveddirsuffix=QA`), Play in a new window, a party of two or three.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the hub, open the game menu (Tab), Party | Each seat card: the name, the class, a line "Mind N    Body N    Spirit N", who plays it; at the top right Seat N with the weapon's name under it | [ ] |
| 2 | Look under the words on a card | An HP bar (red) and an EP bar (blue), full in the hub, each with its value on it | [ ] |
| 3 | Compare with the Character numbers (`wor.UI.PrintParty` in the console) | The card's Mind / Body / Spirit, HP and EP match the printed line | [ ] |
| 4 | Y on a member, change the active loadout's weapon, B back | The card's weapon name is the new one | [ ] |
| 5 | In a trial after a fight that cost HP, open Party | HP and EP read what the member carries; a low HP bar is the brighter red | [ ] |
| 6 | A Caster with no weapon piece | The weapon line reads Innate spells | [ ] |
