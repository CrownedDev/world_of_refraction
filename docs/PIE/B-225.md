# PIE — B-225: Effect timing per D-051 / Q-044c / Q-044d

**Branch:** `feature/B-225-effect-timing` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** the effect specs now check the ruled timings:
- `WoR.Effects.Baseline` (UseGAS 0 and 1):
  - an N-turn effect loses a turn at each of the holder's turn ends and ends as the holder's next turn starts;
  - a damage-over-time effect pays at each of the holder's turn starts, exactly N times, with no extra hit on landing.
- `WoR.Combat.HangFixes`: a 1-turn stun on the enemy holds through its turn and ends as its next turn starts.
- `WoR.Combat.RitualFire`: a ritual's Execution turn runs the caster's turn-start step. A burn that kills the caster there stops the ritual and ends the fight.

The full suite (731) is green.

What changed (this is a deliberate change in play, from your rulings):
- **Every timed effect counts the holder's own turns** (D-051):
  - a 2-turn effect landing on the enemy's turn lasts through the holder's next 2 turns;
  - one landing on the holder's own turn counts that turn as turn 1.
- **Damage and heal over time pay at the holder's turn start, exactly N times** (Q-044c):
  - before this, a 2-turn burn hit on landing and then at 2 turn ends (3 hits);
  - now it hits at 2 turn starts;
  - one landing on the holder's own turn pays once on landing instead of at that turn's start, which has already passed (Q-063).
- **A ritual's Execution turn is one of the caster's turns** (Q-044d): burns, heals and expiries happen at its start, before the ritual fires.
