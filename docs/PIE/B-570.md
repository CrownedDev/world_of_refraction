# PIE — B-570: the trial pool, stage 6: duplicates (D-225, TrialPoolDesign §6)

**Branch:** `feature/B-570-trial-duplicates` (built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-18). **Built unseen:** not played yet.

**What it is:** when a won fight drops a weapon your character already has one of, you choose what to do with it.
- The drop always goes into the inventory first (so nothing is lost if you never answer).
- Back on the trial map, a prompt asks: **Replace yours**, **Add it (keep both)**, or **Convert to essence**. Back keeps both.
- Replace: the new one takes the old one's place in every loadout. The old one stays in the inventory, unworn (taking gear apart is the Blacksmith's, in the hub).
- Convert: the new one is dismantled for Gear essence.
- A dropped skill your character already knows becomes Skill essence at once; the results say "already known".

**Defaults chosen (Crown may change, Q-570):** the prompt is for weapons only. Adapters are not duplicates (two of one adapter is normal, B-530). Crystals stack as before. A known skill converts, because a character can't hold a skill twice. Shops still refuse a known skill (ER-20 unchanged). Evolutions and apparel: not built. A co-op guest's duplicate keeps both.

**Editor steps:** none.
