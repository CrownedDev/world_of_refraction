# PIE — D-221 stage 1b (B-508, B-509, B-510): how a run's ending leaves your gear

**Branches:** `feature/B-508-run-start-snapshot`, `feature/B-509-kept-endings-hub-gear`, `feature/B-510-lost-run-tier-drop` (all in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** every way a run ends now treats your gear by D-219 and D-221:
- **Extract or boss win:** you keep what you wear at the end. That includes the crystals in your own pieces' sockets. Found gear you are **wearing** stays as **hub gear**, which you can't sell or dismantle.
- **Out of wipes:** you keep what you wear, but no found gear. Every worn piece **drops 3 tiers** (S to C), never below F.
- **Quit, or a game closed mid-run:** the worn pieces drop 3 tiers first. Then the gear you wore when the run began comes back.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Run.EndLoadout` (5): a quit and a closed game put back the run-start gear and wheel pick; the other endings keep the run's gear; the run-start copy is saved with the run.
- `WoR.Run.EndGear` (5): sockets kept at full wear; hub gear kept, saved and refused for sale; out of wipes keeps no finds; a home gem moved to the pouch isn't taken back.
- `WoR.Run.TierDrop` (5): 3 tiers, never below F, once; nothing on a kept ending; a quit drops what is worn at the quit.

**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. Co-op guests are host-only (B-230). A trial still starts on the hub's gear: the class default kit comes with B-511 and B-512.
**Your old save is wiped** on the first load (version 41), with the usual warning: start a new game.

**The debug lines:**
- `wor.Loadout.PrintWorn` prints what each member wears.
- `wor.Run.PrintKept` prints the open run: marked pieces with their tiers, and the run-start copy.
- The log line `[Pool] Lost run: ... drop 3 tier(s)` shows each tier drop.
- A piece's tier shows on the loadout and inventory screens.
