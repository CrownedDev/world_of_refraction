# PIE — B-180: changing rows (D-096)

**Branch:** `feature/B-180-row-changes` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Combat.RowMove` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - a step forward works, the front-row bonus applies at once, and a second step that turn is refused
  - a step back works
  - stepping into an ally's cell is refused (Q-180, added later; it used to swap)
  - the step comes back on your next turn
  - a step is refused while stunned, in the last row and off your turn
  - a push and a pull move the target one row, with the row bonus
  - a push does nothing when the cell behind is taken

**What changed (D-096):**
- On your turn the command menu has **Step Forward** and **Step Back** (placeholder buttons). A step is free and does not end your turn; you get one per turn.
- If an ally stands where you step, the step is refused (Q-180: no swapping in battle).
- Skills can now push a target back or pull it forward one row (new effect types **Push Back** / **Pull Forward**, authored with Duration 0). No skill uses them yet.
- Open details (pushing into a taken cell, bosses, fallen allies): **Q-180**.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a fight with at least two party members.
