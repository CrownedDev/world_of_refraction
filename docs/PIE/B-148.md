# PIE — B-148: Silenced seals half your Energy

**Branch:** `feature/B-148-silence-seal` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Effects.SilenceSeal` (4 cases) checks:
- With 100 max EP and 80 current, a silenced character can spend 30 EP, not 31.
- Spendable EP never goes below 0.
- Defend and a 0-cost ability still work with every point sealed.
- The seal lifts when Silenced ends.

**What changed (D-038, D-070, D-090):** Silenced no longer blocks spells. While it lasts (1 of the holder's turns), **half of max EP is sealed** (`SILENCE_SEALED_EP_FRACTION`, to tune). Spells, abilities and infusion can only spend the rest. Basic attack, Defend and items still work. Broken Darkness EP is sealed too. The effect is now called plain **"Silenced"**. The EP bar doesn't show the sealed part yet; that's UI work (B-111 / HUD).

**Setup:** a fight where an enemy (not Broken Darkness) can fill your Darkness bar, or an Onyx S-rank item to silence an enemy. Open the console with the tilde key; Output Log open.
