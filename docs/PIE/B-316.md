# PIE — B-316: buy for another party member

**Branch:** `feature/B-316-give-request-buyfor` (in `dev`; built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Economy.BuyFor` (6 tests, green on the desktop; the payer is funded with Elemental essence too, B-333).
**Setup:** a party of two or more (one AI-controlled) in the hub with some Prisms. Open the Output Log.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open a vendor; type `wor.Shop.BuyFor` (or press the Buy-for button if the WBP has one) | Toast and log `Buying for: <next member>`; again cycles back to `Buying for: yourself` | [ ] |
| 2 | Buying for the AI member, put a weapon and some gems in the cart and Confirm | Prisms leave your wallet; log `bought for ...: 1 piece(s) of gear and N item(s) handed over` | [ ] |
| 3 | `wor.Pool.PrintStore`, `wor.Inventory.PrintSlots` | The weapon is listed under the AI member only (one instance); the gems are in its pouch | [ ] |
| 4 | Buy for the AI member a weapon it already owns | Its duplicate choice comes up (`wor.UI.Duplicates`) | [ ] |
| 5 | Buying for another member, try a spell | Refused, nothing spent (log: `can't be bought for another character yet (B-316b)`) | [ ] |
| 6 | Close and reopen the shop | It starts at `Buying for: yourself` | [ ] |

| 7 | Buy for a member whose pouch or armoury is full | Refused before paying (log: `refused, nothing spent`) | [ ] |
| 8 | Buy for another member a weapon you already own | No duplicate prompt for you; the receiver gets it | [ ] |

**Known (as built):** your own armoury must have room for the gear even though it only passes through; gems that landed in the shared storage (your pouch was full) stay there. Both log a warning and are B-316b's to lift.
