# PIE — B-587: the Intel close-up (D-262)

**Branch:** `feature/B-587-intel-closeup`.
**What changed (D-262):**
- When Intel opens, and on every tab change, the fight camera **cuts** (no blend) to a front close-up of that tab's character: the B-400 target view's front shot, a quarter nearer, with the character in the middle of the screen's left two-thirds. Party and enemy tabs both.
- The Intel panel sits on the **right**, under the turn order (was centred), and is narrower (the right third at any 16:9 size). No row of tabs: one title, `◀ Q  Broken Darkness Lord  E ▶` (LB / RB on a pad), a small `Party` / `Enemy` / `You` label above it, an enemy's name in the enemy colour; the arrows show only with more than one character and take a click. While it is open the command menu, the bottom-right key hints and the enemy cards hide (Expedition 33's way); the turn order stays. A long stat or effect name wraps instead of running into its value.
- **V / R3 cycles (D-262 additions):** a tap opens on your party, the next tap jumps to the first enemy, the next closes; holding it (0.5 s) closes at any time. Settings > Gameplay > **Intel key**: Cycle (default) / Open and close. The panel's footer says what the next tap does.
- If another fighter stands in front of the character, the close-up takes the other three-quarter side, or a higher angle, whichever is clear.
- Closing Intel (V, Back, the same card again, or the Status button replacing it) cuts back to the turn view; if the target picker was open under it, its target view comes back.
- **Camera Movement Off:** the camera doesn't move; the panel is still on the right.
- The close-up happens only on your own turn view. On an enemy's turn, during an action's shots or a cinematic the camera holds and only the panel opens.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.Camera` "Intel's close-up" (each enemy and ally framed from its front, feet and head on screen, left of the panel's edge, larger than in the target view, for a person and a 4 m golem) and "Director … Intel's close-up" (open, each tab change and close are cuts, one move each, a push on the same tab doesn't cut again, the Status button replacing Intel cuts back, Movement Off keeps the camera still and Intel still opens; Movement back On and a new party turn with Intel open show the close-up again), the QA cases (a recruit sized by its capsule, not its talk sphere; a clear angle past a fighter in front), and `WoR.UI.Intel` (the menu, the corner hints and the enemy cards hidden while Intel is open, the turn order kept; one title with the side label and arrows; the tap cycle party → enemy → closed and Open and close), `WoR.UI.Settings` (the Intel key row, Cycle by default). `WoR.UI.Intel` (B-586's panel) unchanged. Not proven there: the look in a real arena (other fighters in the way, the real capsule heights), the panel's real width at both sizes.
