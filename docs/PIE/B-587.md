# PIE — B-587: the Intel close-up (D-262)

**Branch:** `feature/B-587-intel-closeup`.
**What changed (D-262):**
- When Intel opens, and on every tab change, the fight camera **cuts** (no blend) to a front close-up of that tab's character: the B-400 target view's front shot, a quarter nearer, with the character in the middle of the screen's left two-thirds. Party and enemy tabs both.
- The Intel panel sits on the **right** (was centred) and is narrower (it fills the right third at any 16:9 size); a long name shrinks to fit its tab.
- Closing Intel (V, Back, the same card again, or the Status button replacing it) cuts back to the turn view; if the target picker was open under it, its target view comes back.
- **Camera Movement Off:** the camera doesn't move; the panel is still on the right.
- The close-up happens only on your own turn view. On an enemy's turn, during an action's shots or a cinematic the camera holds and only the panel opens.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.Camera` "Intel's close-up" (each enemy and ally framed from its front, feet and head on screen, left of the panel's edge, larger than in the target view, for a person and a 4 m golem) and "Director … Intel's close-up" (open, each tab change and close are cuts, one move each, a push on the same tab doesn't cut again, the Status button replacing Intel cuts back, Movement Off keeps the camera still and Intel still opens; Movement back On and a new party turn with Intel open show the close-up again). `WoR.UI.Intel` (B-586's panel) unchanged. Not proven there: the look in a real arena (other fighters in the way, the real capsule heights), the panel's real width at both sizes.

**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`, "How an agent plays"). Play on the QA save folder only. Run every check at `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`, then repeat 2, 3 and 5 at `SetWindowSize(Width=1920, Height=1080)`. Over the Shoulder turn view (Settings) unless a check says otherwise; Camera Movement On. `wor.Camera` prints `intel close-up showing / off (n moves)`; `wor.UI.Print` prints the open tab.
