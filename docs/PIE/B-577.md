# PIE — B-577: the Healing Stone boosts healing instead of giving HP

**Branch:** `feature/B-577-healing-stone`.
**What changed (D-252, D-253):** using a Healing Stone gives no HP. Its living target receives more healing for some turns (F: 15% for 4 turns). A dead target is refused and nothing is spent. The AI no longer uses it as its low-HP heal.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Items.HealingStone` (9 tests) and the full suite, headless. Not proven there: the fight menu, the buff shown on the card, and the description on screen.
**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`, "How an agent plays"). Play on the QA save folder only.
**Setup:** in the hub, `wor.Economy.GiveCrystal HealingStone F 3`, then `wor.Inventory.PrintSlots` and note the Healing Stone's count.
**To know:** no item gives HP on use until the Health Potion (B-579). Check 5 needs a heal from a skill; skip it and say so if the party has none.
