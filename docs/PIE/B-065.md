# PIE — B-065: cleanse leaves gear effects alone

**Branch:** `feature/B-065-cleanse-skips-gear` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Effects.CleanseGear` (4 cases) checks four things:
- Gear effects are marked.
- Cleanse removes an enemy's Burn but keeps your own gear debuff.
- Removing all buffs keeps gear buffs.
- Iolite on an enemy strips its cast buffs but keeps its gear buffs.

**What changed (D-015):** cleanse (Cleanse Self / Cleanse Allies) and Iolite remove only effects **someone else applied**. Effects a character's own gear gives it are immune, yours and the enemy's. Before, they were stripped and never came back.

**Setup:** a character with a ring or weapon that gives an effect (it shows in `wor.PrintEffects` as "from gear"), a cleanse spell or Iolite, and an enemy that can Burn you or has gear buffs. Open the console with the tilde key; Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start a fight; `wor.PrintEffects` | Your gear effects are listed with ", from gear" | [ ] |
| 2 | Get Burned (or debuffed by an enemy), then cast your cleanse | The Burn/debuff is gone; your gear effects are still listed | [ ] |
| 3 | Buff an enemy (or face one with gear buffs) and use Iolite on it | Its cast buffs are gone; its gear buffs are still there | [ ] |
| 4 | (If you have gear that triggers on a block/parry and buffs you) trigger it, then have an enemy dispel you | The gear-triggered buff on you stays; one it put on someone else can be cleansed | [ ] |
| 5 | Finish the fight and start another | Gear effects come back as usual; nothing left over from the last fight | [ ] |
