# PIE — B-297: the 2:1 turn cap (D-049)

**Branch:** `fix/B-297-turn-cap` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Turns.TurnCap`: a combatant more than twice as fast never takes more than 2 turns in a row, and a Wind delay doesn't break the cap. Turn-order, first-strike and bonus-turn specs unchanged. Full suite green.

What changed: very fast characters (or very slowed enemies) could take 3, 4 or more turns in a row. Now nobody takes more than 2 turns for each turn of the slowest, and never more than 2 in a row while an opponent can act. Emerald bonus turns are not capped yet (Q-297 asks you).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. A fight where one side is much faster (stack turn-speed gear, or slow an enemy hard with a Turn Speed debuff).
