# PIE — B-170c: action pips 3, several actions per turn

**Branch:** `feature/B-170c-multi-action-turns-cloud` (cloud, UNBUILT; stacked on `feature/B-170b-turn-rotation-cloud`, which is on `feature/B-170a-action-pips-cloud`, so build and test those first, then this).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Turns.MultiAction` (switch 0: one action ends the turn as built; switch 1: two cost-1 actions in one turn with one turn start and one turn end; End Turn banks the rest; Defend ends the turn and costs nothing; a cost above Max is refused, one above the bank waits; arming a ritual spends its cost and the turn goes on; two switches in a turn, free; a first turn plays the fight-start bank and the next regains), plus unchanged at the default 0: `WoR.Combat.TurnStartOrder`, `WoR.Combat.RitualFire`, `WoR.Loadout.TurnSwitch`, `WoR.Turns.Rotation`, `WoR.Combat.ActionPips`.

**With the switch off (default) nothing changes.** With it on, a turn spends banked actions. There is no End Turn button and no pips on screen yet (B-170e): use the console for End Turn and `wor.Actions.Print` to see the bank. The AI still takes one action per turn (B-170f).

**Setup:** type `wor.Turns.ActionPips 1` in the console **before** touching an enemy team. A default character has Max 2 and regains 1 per turn; `wor.Actions.AddPoints 100` (Max 3) and Reflex gear (regain 2 or 3) give more to play with.
