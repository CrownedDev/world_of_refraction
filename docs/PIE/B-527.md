# PIE — B-527: ring slots from the crystal, two tier lines, named adapters, prebuilt adapters for sale (D-226)

**Branch:** `feature/B-527-adapter-slots-names-shop` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is:** D-226, four small changes:
1. **Slots from the crystal:** a ring's spell slot count reads the tier of what is in its socket (a gem, or an evolution), not the ring-and-crystal average. An F ring with a B crystal has B's slots. An empty ring keeps its own tier's count. Skill power and wear still read the average; the ring's stat lines its own tier.
2. **Two tier lines:** the Equipment detail shows "Adapter (F)" and "Crystal (B)" instead of "Skill tier D (F + B)" (the brackets: D-227 (5), B-527b). A weapon shows "Weapon (F)" in the same place. The crystal line shows only while the socket holds something.
3. **Named adapters:** an adapter with its own name ("King's Ring") shows that name whatever is in its socket. One without (its name empty, or "Adapter Ring") is named by its socket, as before ("Fire Ring").
4. **Prebuilt adapters for sale:** a merchant can stock a prebuilt adapter (a "Prebuilt Find" asset, B-522b). It costs the adapter's price plus the socketed piece's, and arrives with the crystal or evolution in it.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Loadout.AdapterTier` (an F ring with a B Citrine has B's slots), `WoR.Loadout.Rules` (an S ring with an F evolution has F's), `WoR.GameNames` (King's Ring keeps its name; an "Adapter Ring" is named by its socket), `WoR.Trial.PrebuiltFind` (a merchant's prebuilt ring costs the ring plus the gem, and arrives socketed). Full suite in the session note.
