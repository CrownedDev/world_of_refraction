# PIE — B-342: three rows, no columns (D-134)

**Branch:** `feature/B-342-three-rows` (in `dev`; built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Combat.RowEffect` (new, 3 tests): front deals and takes ×1.20, back ×0.80, middle ×1.00, on the party side and the enemy side, through the damage maths.
- `WoR.Combat.RowMove`, `WoR.Combat.Grid` and `WoR.Combat.FormationStart` were updated:
  - a row step or push into a row an ally stands in now goes through
  - the once-per-turn step (D-096) still holds
  - a member starts in its chosen row

**What changed:**
- Rows have no columns. An ally in a row never blocks a step; Q-180's "That cell is taken." is gone.
- The row effect is a bigger placeholder, front +20% dealt and taken, back −20% (`// TODO: tune`; was ±5%).
- The party screen still shows a column; it no longer matters (flagged obsolete).

**Balance:** the row effect goes from ±5% to ±20%, so re-run the balance sim after building (`WOR_BALANCE=nightly Scripts/ue/test.sh WoR.Balance.`) and compare the bands.

**Setup:** build the branch, open the Output Log, and start a fight with at least two party members.
