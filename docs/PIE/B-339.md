# PIE — B-339: one placeholder inventory screen

**Branch:** `feature/B-339-inventory-screen` (in `dev`; built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Inventory.Verbs` (9 tests: the verb routing and the refusals), green on the desktop.
**Setup:** in the hub with a party of two or more, the leader owning a spare weapon (not its primary), two copies of one weapon, and a few crystals in its pouch. Open the Output Log. Type `wor.UI.Inventory`.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Look at the screen; press **Next item** through the list | `Character: <leader>`; its own gear and pouch stacks (`Own: ...`), then the chest's gear and stored stacks (`Chest: ...`); only the verbs that apply are enabled | [ ] |
| 2 | On the spare weapon, set **Next receiver** to another member, press **Give** | Status `Give: <weapon>`; it leaves the list; `wor.Pool.PrintStore` lists it under the receiver | [ ] |
| 3 | On another own weapon, press **Store** | It moves to the `Chest:` part of the list; only **Take** is enabled on it | [ ] |
| 4 | On that chest weapon, press **Take** | It is back under `Own:` | [ ] |
| 5 | On a pouch stack press **Store**; turn on **Whole stack** and press **Store** again | One, then the rest, move to storage (`wor.Inventory.PrintSlots`) | [ ] |
| 6 | On the stored stack press **Take** | Back in the pouch | [ ] |
| 7 | On a spare weapon press **Dismantle** | It is gone; Gear essence goes up | [ ] |
| 7b | On a weapon in a saved loadout (not the primary) press **Dismantle** | The D-016 warning and `Press Dismantle again to confirm`; a second press dismantles it; pressing Next item instead cancels | [ ] |
| 7c | On the primary or worn gear | Store, Give and Dismantle are greyed out; during a run Dismantle is greyed out on home gear | [ ] |
| 8 | On the weaker of the two copies press **Convert to Gold** | It is gone; Gold goes up; the other copy stays. Convert is greyed out on gear that isn't a weaker copy | [ ] |
| 9 | **Next character** to a member not spawned in this level, try Dismantle | Refused: `That character isn't here to do it` | [ ] |
| 10 | Close; `wor.Inventory.PrintVerbs` | Each member's rows with their verbs | [ ] |
