# PIE — B-463b: track a quest

**Branch:** `feature/B-463b-track-quest` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.JournalScreen` (tracking case): A tracks a quest, one at a time; the line reads "title: current goal" and follows the step; it is saved and restored; it goes when the quest finishes; a secret quest can't be tracked. `WoR.UI.PauseMenu`: the Journal row previews the tracked quest.
**What changed:** in the journal's Quests page, A on an unfinished main or side quest tracks it (the hint reads "A  Track", then "A  Stop tracking"; the reader's line says "tracked"). The game menu's Journal row then previews "Tracking: {quest}: {step}", and in a trial the tracked step shows top right on the screen. Decisions: **Q-463b** (reference-backed default).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** the HUD line's place next to the money chip; the look.
**Left out:** the full trial HUD (Prisms, wipes left, party HP: B-465); a marker in the world for the tracked step.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, play in the hub with a started main or side quest (`wor.Quest.Start <QuestId>` if none; `wor.Quest.Print` lists them).
