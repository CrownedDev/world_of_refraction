# PIE — B-443: the hub HUD (interact prompt and money chip)

**Branch:** `feature/B-443-hub-hud` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/hub/hud/1_hub_hud_prompt.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.HubHUD` (5 cases): when the money chip shows; the interact key's label per device; every prompt verb has a word; a rest spot prompts "Rest" and a memory spot prompts nothing; with nothing in reach there is no prompt and no money chip.
**What changed:** in the hub and on the trial map, the thing you can interact with gets a floating prompt (key, verb, what it is), and the money shows top right only near a vendor or just after it changed. The party strip is the one you already have.
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** where the prompt floats and whether it follows smoothly as the camera turns; how both pieces look.
**Still there on purpose:** the old yellow / cyan "Press E ..." debug lines in the corner. They go in the sweep once this passes.
**Left out:** the place name top left; NPC talk and the recruit card (their mockups are being redrawn); key glyph art (the key shows as a letter: "X" on a gamepad, "E" on the keyboard).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, PIE in the hub.
