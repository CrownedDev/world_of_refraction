# PIE — B-071: your recruited party fights with you

**Branch:** `feature/B-071-party-into-battle` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Party.IntoBattle` (6 cases) checks six things:
- The leader stays yours while other members can go to the AI.
- A member you play gets the command menu even though the camera's character is the leader.
- A member switches between the AI and you on its own turn; an enemy can't be switched.
- AI allies play at Hard while enemies play at the encounter's level.
- When your fighters fall you take over the AI members, unless "Let AI finish" is set.
- A gearless Generic sits out; a Refractor can fight gearless.

**What changed:**
- Each party member spawns as their own character (D-003) and is played by you or the AI, per member. The leader is always yours (D-004).
- AI allies always play at Hard (D-044, Q-071).
- When nobody you play is left standing, you take over the AI members, or the AI finishes if you chose that (D-045).
- A member you play can hand itself to the AI on its turn (D-026).
- A Generic or Resonator with no gear sits the fight out (D-016).

**Setup:** the hub with at least two recruitable characters; recruit two so the party is 3. Open the console with the tilde key; Output Log open. **Placeholder screen:** `wor.UI.PartyControl` (click a member to flip You/AI, click the fall row to flip the setting, Close). `wor.Party.Print` prints the same.
