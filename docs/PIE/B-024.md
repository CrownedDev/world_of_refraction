# PIE — B-024: ring and weapon managers folded into the loadout

**Branch:** `refactor/B-024-fold-weapon-ring-managers` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds, and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Loadout.RingWeaponQueries` spec.
- The spec was written and passed on the old code first. It checks:
  - the active ring, the primary ring and the active ring's element
  - switching to the next ring skips a broken one, and does nothing when no other ring is usable
  - when a ring's crystal breaks, the active ring moves forward **exactly once**; a broken weapon crystal doesn't switch rings
  - the active weapon and attack
- It all still passes after the change.

**What changed for the player:** nothing. The loadout component now answers the ring and weapon questions itself and handles "ring crystal broke, switch rings". The old Ring Manager and Weapon Manager still exist, but they only pass calls through. They come out in B-154 after this check.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a fight with a Resonator wearing two or more rings with crystals. A Generic or Caster with a weapon crystal helps for check 4.
