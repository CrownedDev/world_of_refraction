# PIE — B-042j3: absorbers, part 3 (D-092): Reflect by element, a stopped hit and On Take Damage, DoT previews

**Branch:** `feature/B-042j3-absorb-part3` (in `dev`; built on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**What changed:** (1) a Reflect effect with an **element** reflects hits of that element only ("reflect 50% of the Fire damage you take"); one without reflects every hit of its kind, as before. The three reflect kinds stay one effect family. (2) **On Take Damage** conditionals (gear effects that fire when you take an unguarded hit) now fire **after** the hit lands, and only when it did damage: a hit the absorbers (Barrier / Shield / Ward) stopped entirely fires none (D-092 rule 8). (3) The damage-over-time **preview** (what the AI reads to judge a lethal tick) counts your absorbers: a Ward that will eat the next Burn tick makes the preview smaller; nothing is spent by the preview.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.HitPath` (a Fire-scoped reflect bounces Fire only; an unscoped one every hit), `WoR.GAS.Absorb` (a Fire tick previews after a Fire Ward, a Water tick whole, nothing spent); the `WoR.Combat` / `WoR.GAS` / `WoR.Effects` / `WoR.Skills` / `WoR.Defense` / `WoR.Text` / `WoR.AI` subsets green (501); data validation 0 new.
**Setup:** a skill or gear effect of type **Reflect** with Element **Fire**, Magnitude 0.5 (50%), aimed at Self; an enemy with a Fire attack and one with a Water attack. For check 3: a gear effect with condition **On Receiving Damage** (Self) and a visible payload (a heal, a buff), plus `wor.Absorb.Add` (B-042j) for a big Barrier.
