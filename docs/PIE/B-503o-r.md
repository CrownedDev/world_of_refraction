# PIE — B-503o to B-503r: the fight follows the ACTIVE weapon (Crown's step 3, align the code)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503pqr-active-entry-alignment` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** the loadout entry is the one truth. The battle **stance** is the stance of the weapon the fight swings (B-503p). A primary weapon whose slot type was left **None** (a fresh or auto-filled loadout) now stands armed and lists its **Attack, Abilities and spells**, as a Weapon-type one does (B-503q). An **evolution in a weapon's or ring's socket** sets the infusion mode (Physical / Status / Balanced) of that source; a crystal keeps the weapon's or ring's mode (B-503r, Q-503r). The field swing uses the entry's own attack override (B-503o, already in B-503m4; now tested).

**Already proven by tests:** the stance follows a Generic's weapon switch; a None-type primary stands, attacks and lists its slotted ability; an evolution in the socket gives its own mode, a crystal the weapon's, Raw the weapon's; a weapon entry's override attack is the field swing's.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. The infusion mode has no on-screen label, so check 4 reads it from the damage only.
