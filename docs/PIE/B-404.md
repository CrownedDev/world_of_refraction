# PIE — B-404: wide beats and the party handover

**Branch:** `feature/B-404-wide-beats` (in dev, built on the desktop 2026-11-12 from the cloud's `feature/B-404-wide-beats-cloud`; no save change). Against a boss the handover ends on B-405's low turn view; a target highlighted during the beat gets its B-400 target view at the beat's end.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.Camera` "wide beats and the party handover (B-404, study R12, R16)": a hero → hero turn change after an action passes through the wide (NotYourTurn) transform and ends on the next hero's turn view with a real blend; no beat for the same actor, the first turn, movement off or an enemy's turn. `WoR.Combat.Camera.Director`: `OnTurnHandover` fires exactly once per turn start (hero → hero, hero → enemy, enemy → hero, the same hero twice), also when a turn or an action starts inside the beat; a fight's end inside the beat drops it; a preview never fires it. Unchanged: the rest of `WoR.Combat.Camera`.
**This is a feel check:** does a turn change read like Expedition 33's (wide, then a smooth ~0.5 s move behind the next hero; no cut)? Times are first guesses (`TODO: tune` in `CombatCameraTypes.cpp`, `CombatCameraTurnChange`: `WideBeatBlendSeconds` 0.3, `WideBeatHoldSeconds` 0.2, `HandoverBlendSeconds` 0.5).
**Setup:** a fight with a party of 2–3 heroes against 1–2 enemies, camera movement on, Centred Wide and then Over the Shoulder (`wor.Camera.Style 1`). Watch the Output Log with `LogWoRCombat` (the beat logs `Wide beat: A -> B`; `log LogWoRCombat Verbose` also shows each `Handover A -> B`).
