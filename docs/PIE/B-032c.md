# PIE — B-032c: reflect returns a share of the HP lost; Double Hit skips a killed target

**Branch:** `fix/B-032c-reflect-hp-lost` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.HitPath` (now 6 tests):
- An overkilling hit on a 10-HP reflecting target reflects half of 10, not half of the hit.
- A Last Stand save still reflects half of the 10 HP the target had.
- Double Hit fires once on a target the first hit killed, twice on a survivor.

`WoR.Defense.Guard`'s Perfect Guard reflect test still passes. Full suite: 631 green.
**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, start a fight where an enemy has a reflect effect (Perfect Guard reflect), and a hero with Double Hit.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hit the reflecting enemy without killing it | You lose the reflected share of the damage it took, as before (`reflected N damage back to`) | [ ] |
| 2 | Finish the reflecting enemy with a big overkill hit | The reflect is a share of the HP it had left, not of the whole hit | [ ] |
| 3 | Kill an enemy with the first hit of a Double Hit | No second hit or second damage number on the corpse | [ ] |
| 4 | Double Hit an enemy that survives the first hit | The 50% second hit lands, as before | [ ] |
| 5 | Hit an enemy that has Last Stand ready and a reflect, with a lethal hit | It survives on Last Stand HP; you still lose the reflected share of the HP it had | [ ] |

**Notes:**
- Q-032 (approved by default): reflect returns what you actually took, as Pokemon's Counter / Mirror Coat do.
- The reflect now lands after the hit, so the attacker can't die to the reflect before the hit lands.
