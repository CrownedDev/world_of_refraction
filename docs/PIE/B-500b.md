# PIE — B-500b: the battle transition's prism light

**Branch:** `feature/B-500b-transition-prism-light` (in dev, built on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is (D-212, storyboard D frames 1 to 3; Crown: "weren't we gonna add colours?"):** the hit flashes white. Light runs out through the cracks as they spread: white near the hit, splitting into the spectrum further out. Each shard catches a colour, fanned around the hit like the storyboard (red to the right, yellow and green below, cyan to the left, blue and violet above), and its edges stay lit through the hold.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.BattleTransition` (the flash is full at contact and gone after its time; the light reaches each shard as its crack does and never runs backwards; the hold keeps its share of the edge light; every band of the spectrum lands on some shard; each shard's hue is in its mesh).
**Not proven:** how it looks. The code only sends numbers to the shard material; **nothing shows until the editor steps below are done**. Nothing was seen running from the writer's worktree.

## Editor steps first (for the editor worker)

All in `M_BattleTransition_Shard` (`/Game/VFX/Transition/`). The data asset needs no change: the new **Prism** values come from the code defaults.

1. **Five scalar parameters** (exact names; the code sets them):

   | Parameter | Default | Set | Meaning |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `Flash` | 0 | every frame | brightness of the white flash at the hit (6 at contact, 0 after a quarter second) |
   | `LightReach` | 0 | every frame | how far the light has run from the hit: 0 at contact, 1 = the farthest shard, ends at 1.5 |
   | `EdgeGlow` | 0 | every frame | brightness of the light on the edges (4 while the cracks spread, 1.4 in the hold) |
   | `ShardColor` | 0 | once | how strongly a shard's face takes its colour (0.45) |
   | `WhiteReach` | 0.25 | once | the flash covers, and the light stays white, this far from the hit |

2. **Vertex colour** now carries the hue in **A** (new). R = 1 on bevels and sides, G = a random per shard, B = distance from the hit (0 at the hit, 1 farthest), as before.
3. **Build these values** (names are only labels):
   - `Spectrum` = the colour for hue `VertexColor.A`: the engine's **HueShift** material function with colour (1, 0, 0) and Hue Shift Percentage = A. Or a Custom node (input `Hue`, output Float3): `return saturate(abs(frac(Hue + float3(0.0, 2.0/3.0, 1.0/3.0)) * 6.0 - 3.0) - 1.0);`
   - `Lit` = saturate((`LightReach` − B) / 0.15): 0 until the light reaches the shard, then 1.
   - `SpectrumShare` = saturate((B − `WhiteReach`) / 0.25): 0 near the hit (white), 1 further out.
   - `LightColour` = lerp(white, `Spectrum`, `SpectrumShare`).
   - `Picture` = the existing `Frame` × `FrameBrightness`.
4. **Emissive** = the sum of three things:
   - the picture, tinted: lerp(`Picture`, `Picture` × `LightColour` × 2, `ShardColor` × `Lit` × `SpectrumShare`)
   - the edge light: `LightColour` × `EdgeGlow` × `Lit` × R
   - the flash: white × `Flash` × saturate(1 − B / `WhiteReach`)²
5. Save, then in PIE run `wor.Transition.Play 5` and compare with `docs/Design/screen_mockups/combat/transition/D_sheet.png` frames 1 to 3. The 0.15, 0.25 and × 2 above are starting points: change them by eye.

**Notes / what went wrong:**
- No colour at all: the material steps aren't done, or a parameter name differs from the table (they must match exactly).
- The flash blooms through the level's own bloom; the transition's stronger bloom comes on with the break. If the flash looks weak, raise Flash Strength.
- The colours follow the storyboard's fan around the hit, with a little random per shard. If Crown would rather have fully random colours, or the game's nine element colours, that is a small change: say so.
