# PIE — B-407: short signature-spell cameras

**Branch:** `feature/B-407-short-signature-cameras` (in dev, built on the desktop 2026-11-12 from the cloud's `feature/B-407-short-signature-cameras-cloud`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.Camera.SignatureCap`: a 4 s sequence on a player skill is cut at the 2 s cap, a 1.5 s one and one exactly at the cap are not, an enemy's 4 s sequence is never cut, and a 4 s Level Sequence reads as 4 s. `WoR.Presentation.NotifyValidation`: a player skill's 4 s sequence gets a warning line, a boss's does not.
**Setup:** a fight where one of your characters has a skill with its own `CameraSequence` longer than 2 s (author a 4 s test sequence on a test skill if none ships; tag the caster track "Caster" and the target track "Targets"), and an enemy with a long sequence of its own if one exists. Camera movement on (`wor.Camera.MovementOff 0`), cinematics on (`wor.Camera.Cinematics 1`), skip after hit off (`wor.Camera.SkipAfterHit 0`). It's a feel check: FeelGuide 9 says your spells are shorter and calmer than a boss's.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run `wor.Presentation.Validate` in the editor | Your long test skill is listed: `warning: CameraSequence is 4.00s, over the 2.00s signature cap ...`. A skill a boss phase adds (`UBossData` → Phases → Added Skills) is not listed | [ ] |
