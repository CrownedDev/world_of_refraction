# PIE — B-399: calm menus (a sub-menu steps the camera in; nothing moves while choosing)

**Branch:** `feature/B-399-calm-menus` (in dev, built on the desktop 2026-11-12 from the cloud's `feature/B-399-calm-menus-cloud`; against a boss or mini-boss the sub-menu step keeps B-405's low height). No save change (B-398's v34 stays).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.Camera` "calm menus (B-399, study R2, R3)": menu depth 0 is the base turn view for every style; depth 1 is closer to the hero and lower with the enemy centre still in the middle band; Over the Shoulder one step in keeps the hero lower-left and the enemy centre mid-frame; deeper menus hold the one step; a target picker keeps the depth it was opened from. `WoR.Combat.Camera.Director`: through the real command menu, the main menu makes no move, a sub-menu one, switching sub-menus none, Back one more; with camera movement off nothing moves.
**This is a feel check:** does opening Skills feel like Expedition 33's skill list (one small step closer and lower, then dead still), and does the camera hold still while you browse and pick?
