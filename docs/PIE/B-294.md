# PIE — B-294: colour-blind element palette

**Branch:** `feature/B-294-element-palette` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Elements.Palette`: no two of the nine element colours are under CIEDE2000 10 apart in normal, protan, deutan or tritan vision (closest Water / Void 10.8, protan); the colour-difference maths matches Sharma's reference pair; Ice and Physical have their own colours; Darkness text is flagged for an outline. Element table specs unchanged. Full suite green.

What changed: every element colour (bars, menu tints, glows, doors, arena tint) is the new palette. Fire is a deep red, Lightning amber-yellow, Earth ochre, Wind teal, Water mid blue, Void light violet, Reality dark indigo, Light warm cream; Darkness stays near-black. Drafts to look at: `docs/Design/screen_mockups/combat/hud/v3/` (hit, palette).
