# PIE — B-567b: an enemy's limits merged (D-225, Crown 2026-11-17)

**Branch:** `refactor/B-567b-kit-limits-merged` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). Not seen in the editor yet.

**What it is:** an enemy's Enemy > Kit > Limits are now five:
- **Most Gear** (tick box): one number, its slot count: weapons, adapters and an evolution together (apparel has its own place). Its class decides which kinds fit and caps it: a Generic 2 weapons and 1 adapter (3), a Refractor 1 item, a Resonator 1 weapon and 5 adapters (6).
- **Most Skills** (unticked: 3).
- **Kinds**: weapon, adapter, evolution, apparel (empty: any). An evolution is allowed when Evolution is listed. Beside it, **Weapon Types** as before.
- **Elements** and the **tier range**, as before.
- Gone: Most Weapons, Most Adapters, Evolution Allowed.
- Until evolutions and apparel are rolled (a B-568 shortcut), Kinds of only Evolution or Apparel takes nothing from the pool.

**Old values don't carry over:** the removed fields are dropped when the asset loads, so `DA_Enemy_Generic` must be re-authored (step 1).

**Editor steps:**
1. Open `DA_Enemy_Generic`. Under Enemy > Kit > Limits: tick **Most Gear** = 1, and add **Weapon** to **Kinds**. Its Always Has (the Arming Sword at D) stays. Save.
