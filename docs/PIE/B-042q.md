# PIE — B-042q: GAS stage 3, part 5g (more stats on GAS)

**Branch:** `feature/B-042q-gas-stat-sources-rest` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.GAS.StatSources` (8 cases) checks:
- for seven stats (spell / raw damage, action / spell speed, status multiplier, Defense, crit damage), GAS holds the stat's own part and multiplies in gear, stones and buffs, giving exactly today's number;
- Defense stops at 85% and crit damage never drops below a normal hit, on GAS as in today's code;
- a buff updates the GAS effects in place, and a removed one comes back;
- `wor.CompareAttributes` reports a missing GAS effect.

The full suite (698) is green.

What changed:
- Status multiplier, Defense and crit damage are now built on GAS too.
- The game still reads today's numbers, so nothing you see should change.
