# PIE — B-209 (rewritten): memories by conditions

**Branch:** `feature/B-209-memory-conditions` (in `dev`). The first pass (`docs/PIE/B-209.md`, the five sources) still applies.

**Already proven:**
- The editor builds, and 527 tests pass (with the alternative routes, rewards, and gear and skill conditions); data validation shows no new errors.
- `WoR.Memory.Conditions` (7 cases): each condition kind; gear on the right wearer at its level, or the upgraded item; a combatant's worn gear; a two-memory chain unlocking in one event, in either order; a hidden condition never met; a source's own memory (no conditions: always; unmet: not).
- `WoR.Memory.Fragment` / `WoR.Memory.Sources`: a fragment or spot whose memory's conditions aren't met stays put.
- Not provable headless: the asset registry finding your memory assets, the real party, and a real fight's win. These checks cover them.

**Setup (in the editor, needs saving):** three memory assets (`UMemoryData`), each with **Unlock Conditions**:
- **M1**: *Character* = one of your party's characters.
- **M2**: *Prior Memory* = M1, and *Enemy Beaten* = an enemy a trial fight has.
- **M3**: *Character* = a character **not** in your party; put M3 on a `MemoryFragment` on the trial map as its Memory.
