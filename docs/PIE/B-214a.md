# PIE — B-214a: field skills part 1 (dash, the active field skill, Enhanced Sprint)

**Branch:** `feature/B-214a-field-skills-core`, then `fix/B-214a2-drain-while-moving` (both merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Field.Skills` (pick and refuse rules, EP drain stopping at 0, element speed order, the sprint speed while on, the toggle, a new pick stops it, the dash cooldown) and `WoR.Save` (the choice survives save and load). Not provable headless: the feel of the dash and the faster sprint, and the pawn Blueprint's sprint.
**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, play the hub or a trial map with the leader. The keys come in B-214g, so use the console (`~`). For checks 4–8 the leader needs an element: a Caster (e.g. Lightning or Wind), or a weapon with a crystal in it.
