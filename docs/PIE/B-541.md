# PIE — B-541: the hub mirror; Male Nobody and Female Nobody (D-234 points 1 and 2)

**Branch:** `feature/B-541-appearance-mirror` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is (Crown), step 1 of 4: the look only:**
1. The two base bodies are called **Male Nobody** (Manny) and **Female Nobody** (Quinn) wherever you read them.
2. The **Spirit** screen's list starts with Male Nobody, then Female Nobody, then the character forms. A on a Nobody takes any form off and wears that body.
3. New game: the Body row is called **Starting spirit**; its cards say Male Nobody / Female Nobody.
4. The game menu's **Forms** row is now **Spirit**.
5. A **mirror** box in the hub (E: Open · Mirror) opens the Spirit screen. The mirror will hold three rows (Spirit, Presets, Cosmetics), but only rows that work show; Spirit is the only one today, so it opens straight away.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.FormsScreen` (the two Nobodies first; the worn one follows the base body).
