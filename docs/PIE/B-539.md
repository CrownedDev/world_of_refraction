# PIE — B-539: Enter selects on the quick strip; Left reaches the body cards (D-234 point 3)

**Branch:** `feature/B-539-strip-enter-select` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is (Crown):**
1. On the quick strip, **Enter selects** the lit piece, as A does on a pad. A click on a tile still picks it.
2. The PC hint says **Enter Select** (the "Click Pick" hint is gone); the pad hint says A Select.
3. New-game screen: the Body cards (Manny, Quinn) were always shown, but the keys reached them only by pressing Up past the top class. Now **Left** from the class cards goes to them too (and B-538 made them clickable).

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.NewGame`: Left from the class cards reaches the body cards.
