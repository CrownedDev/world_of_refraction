# PIE — B-556: the picks set the starting gear and the trial kit (one-player stage 4)

**Branch:** `feature/B-556-picks-set-gear` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). **Built unseen** (overnight run): nobody has looked at it in PIE yet.

**What it is:** the new-game picks now give the player their gear (D-228), all F tier:
- **Generic:** its two picked weapons. A weapon and a shield are one hand set, with the shield in the off hand. Two other types take both weapon places, the first picked in hand.
- **Resonator:** two empty adapters, plus 3 F-tier crystals of each picked type in its pouch, to socket itself.
- **Refractor:** nothing beyond its innate spells.

The innate spells and Broken Darkness pools come from the class table (B-549), no longer from an old Lord's inventory (the stage 3 shortcut is gone). The starting gear is minted once, on the first visit to the hub or the first trial.

**The trial kit:** each trial starts on the class kit (`DA_Loadout_ClassDefaults`, Kit | Defaults) with the picked pieces laid over it. A Generic's two weapon places are its picks; a Resonator's first two adapter places are its two adapters; every other place stays the class kit's. The kit is reset at each trial start, as before (D-221).

**Not in this stage:** two of the same weapon type (dual wield) can't be picked yet: the list takes each type once (see the hand-off).

**Editor steps first (the editor session):**
1. Open `/Game/Data/Loadout/DA_Loadout_ClassDefaults`. Under **Picks**:
   - **Pick Weapons:** add one row per weapon type on the new game screen (Sword, Greatsword, Spear, Staff, Dagger, Axe, Hammer, Bow, Gauntlets, Scythe, Gun, Shield), each pointing at an **F-tier** weapon of that type. A piece is minted at its asset's tier, so a higher-tier asset gives a higher-tier piece. List any type with no F-tier weapon; that pick gives nothing until one exists (logged).
   - **Pick Adapter:** an F-tier adapter ring with no crystal.
   Save the asset.
2. Click **Print Defaults** (Debug): the end lists `Picks: adapter <name>; 12 weapon type(s)`, each with its tier.
