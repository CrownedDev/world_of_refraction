# PIE — B-503l: holding R / LB for Reality and Broken Darkness (Q-503l)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503l-field-hold-element` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** Crown's Q-503l answer (B). In a trial, a **hold** of R / LB (about half a second) steps the field attack's element through the elements of the character's other gear. **Reality** on the innate element or an evolution reads Unknown, then (R) Fire, (R) Lightning and so on; an adapter or a weapon crystal does not cycle. **Broken Darkness** on Darkness reads Darkness, then Dark Fire, Dark Lightning. With a weapon the type follows (Darkness Sword). A quick **press** still steps the source and skips these.

**Already proven by tests:** the press and hold step rules (a press skips hold steps and wraps; a hold steps them and wraps back; a one-element source stays); a Reality Refractor with a Fire crystal (Unknown, a hold to (R) Fire and back, a press to the crystal's Fire, a hold there stays); a Broken Darkness Refractor with a Fire crystal (Darkness, Dark Fire), and none once it is not Broken Darkness.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. The hold steps only exist when the character has other gear with an element.
