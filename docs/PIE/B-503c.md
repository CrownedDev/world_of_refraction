# PIE — B-503c: the field attack's pulse (D-215)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503c-field-pulse` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** with any entry that isn't a weapon in use (a ring, the innate element, an evolution), the field attack throws an energy pulse from the hand. It flies up to 7 m straight ahead of the character, stops at the first enemy or wall, and starts the fight like the swing does. It is drawn in the entry's element colour (held Water: Ice's pale blue; no element: the neutral colour).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Field.Attack` (9 tests; new: the pulse reaches further than the swing with a thinner sweep, and it waits for the cast animation's release before it leaves).
**Not proven:** anything on screen, and the 7 m hit on a real enemy. Nothing was seen running from the writer's worktree.
**Playable before the editor steps:** yes. With no assets set the pulse leaves at once with no animation and shows as a plain coloured line with a ball at its end for under half a second.

## Editor steps (for the editor worker; the checks work without them, as the plain line)

1. **The cast animation:** run `wor.Editor.BuildFieldPulseMontage` in the editor's console and save the asset (B-503d, `docs/PIE/B-503d.md`). It makes `AM_Field_Pulse` in `/Game/Data/Skills/Field/Pulse/` from the throw in `AS_Cast_FireBall`, with a **Combat Notify**, family **Impact**, index 0, at the release: the pulse leaves then.
2. **The burst:** make the Niagara system `FXS_Field_Pulse` in `/Game/VFX/Field/`, with two **user parameters** (exact names; the code sets them):

   | Parameter | Type | Meaning |
   |---|---|---|
   | `PulseColor` | Linear Color | the element's colour |
   | `PulseEnd` | Vector | where the pulse stops, in world space |

   The code spawns it at the right hand (`hand_r`), turned to face the end point (X forward). A simple version: a small bright ball or streak that travels from the system's origin to `PulseEnd` in about 0.2 s, and a small flash at `PulseEnd`, all in `PulseColor`. It must end by itself (no loop).
3. **Turn them on:** in `Config/DefaultGame.ini`, under `[/Script/world_of_refraction.FieldMovementComponent]`, uncomment `FieldPulseMontage` and `FieldPulseVFX` (correct the paths if the assets are elsewhere), then restart the editor.
4. **The map character's Anim Blueprint** (`ABP_TestCombatCharacter`) already has the **Slot 'DefaultSlot'** node (checked in B-503d): nothing to do.

## Checks

**Setup:** play a trial map with a character that has an innate element or a ring. `wor.Field.CycleAttack` until a line marked `(pulse)` is in use. Attack with Left mouse / RB, or `wor.Field.Attack`. `wor.Field.Print` ends with the pulse's cast and burst in use (`none` before the editor steps).

**Decided by default, Crown may change (Q-503c):**
- The pulse goes where the character faces, level with the ground, not where the camera looks.
- It hits at the moment it leaves (the look then flies to the spot); the fight's own opening covers the flight.
- Walls stop it.
- It costs no EP and shares the swing's 0.6 s cooldown.
