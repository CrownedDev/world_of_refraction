# PIE — B-032d: per-impact defense for every spell shape

**Branch:** `feature/B-032d-per-impact-defense-cloud` (cloud, UNBUILT, from dev).
**Behaviour change:** multi-hit and multi-entry spells are now defended once per impact (one parry per impact), like single-hit spells. The switch `wor.Combat.PerImpactDefense` is on by default; `wor.Combat.PerImpactDefense 0` brings back the old lumped hit so you can compare. Decided as Q-032d (reference-backed: Expedition 33's parry on every hit).
**Hits change count (Q-032d, kept by Crown 2026-11-12):** a spell now hits once per arrival, not HitCount times, so on-hit effects (Lifesteal, Burn, Double Hit), crit rolls, the minimum-damage floor and reflect fire per arrival: a 1-entry spell with HitCount 3 goes from 3 hits to 1; a multi-entry spell with HitCount 1 goes from 1 hit to one per entry.
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Combat.PerImpactDefense` (13 tests):
- the impact plan: a spell's impacts are its arrivals, summed over its cast entries;
- a 2-hit spell and a 2-entry spell each resolve impacts 0 and 1 on their own; the lumped path resolves none;
- a Guard at the cast guards impact 1 only; impact 2 lands in full;
- a timed-out action lands both impacts, the same hits as when they arrive;
- a spell's window stays open past the cast animation: projectiles still flying are each defended at their arrival;
- through the running ability (`wor.Abilities.UseGAS 1`) each arrival is taken once;
- nothing defended: the same hits and total as the lumped path;
- an ability with no Impact notify lands its impacts as fallback impacts when its animation ends.

Run with the other defense specs: `WoR.Combat.*`, `WoR.Defense.*`, `WoR.GAS.Abilities`.
**Setup:** pull the branch, build, start the demo fight. Find a spell with **several cast entries** and one with a **HitCount above 1** (`wor.Combat.PrintSpellImpacts` in the console lists every loaded spell: its impacts per target, the split, and "per impact" or "lumped").

**Notes:**
- A spell's impact is what lands (a projectile, an AOE or Instant resolve). A HitCount that doesn't match its arrivals is split evenly, with a warning naming the spell; fix it in the editor so its DamageSplit applies.
- An interruptable cast entry still only interrupts when it is the target's one impact (multi-entry spells never could).
- Impacts left when a window closes early (8 s failsafe, timeout, interrupted montage) land one by one with that window's press: no Perfect, no luck dodge.
- Support spells keep their old window; B-032b decides it when it deletes the lumped path.
- With `wor.Combat.InstantImpacts 0` (the default), a montage-less multi-hit or multi-entry spell now waits for the 8 s window failsafe before its impacts land, as B-032's single-entry spells already did (B-291).
- A montaged multi-entry spell with only a SpellRelease notify (no Cast notify for its later entries) sends entry 0 alone; the rest land at the 8 s failsafe. B-032b checks for these in the editor.
- An ability with a projectile cast entry gets no fallback impacts at the swing's end.
- Not built in the cloud: build first and run the specs above.
