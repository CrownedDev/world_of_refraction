# PIE — B-360: the co-op party (D-150), slice 1

**Branch:** `feature/B-360-coop-party` (in `dev`; built on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**What changed:** in multiplayer every party slot is a player's character or an AI seat: nobody's turn rotates any more (an unclaimed member, which should not exist, falls to the host). A player joining your run asks for a seat with their own character; **you (the host) choose which AI seat they take**, and that member's gear and pouch move to your inventory. With no AI seat free the party is full and the join is refused. **Not in this slice (B-360b):** two players bringing the same character as separate copies (a character already in the party is refused for now).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Party.Coop` (the cap is three; a join takes the host-picked AI seat, its gear and pouch go to the host, the joiner commands it; no AI seat free or a character already in = refused), `WoR.Net.RunState` (an unclaimed member is the host's, nothing rotates); the `WoR.Party` / `WoR.Net` / `WoR.Coop` / `WoR.Pool` / `WoR.Turn` subsets green (133); data validation 0 new.
**Setup:** PIE with **2 players, Listen Server** (Play As Listen Server, Number of Players 2). The host's party: you plus one **AI seat** (`wor.UI.PartyControl`, or `wor.Party.SetControl 1 AI`) holding a gem or two in its pouch. Open the Output Log on both windows.
**Placeholder:** the host opens the seat choice with `wor.UI.JoinSeat` (one line per request, a button per AI seat, Refuse, Close). Console twins: `wor.Party.PrintJoinRequests`, `wor.Party.AcceptJoin <player id> <slot>`, `wor.Party.RefuseJoin <player id>`. The client asks with `wor.Party.RequestJoin <CharacterData asset path>` (e.g. `/Game/Data/Characters/Data/Caster/Fire/DA_Caster_Fire`).
