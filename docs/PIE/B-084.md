# PIE — B-084: dismantling equipped gear

**Branch:** `feature/B-084-dismantle-equipped` (in `dev`).

**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Economy.DismantleEquipped` (3 cases) checks:
- the warning names the loadouts, says upgrades are lost, and says a Generic left with no gear can't fight;
- after dismantle no loadout keeps a copy, and the gearless Generic is refused at fight start;
- gear no loadout holds has no warning;
- a Resonator's dismantled ring slot is removed, its other ring stays active, and it may still fight.

**What changed:** D-016. Dismantling a weapon or ring now asks first when a saved loadout holds it, then takes it out of every loadout before dismantling. Refused during a fight.

**Note:** battle characters are built fresh from their character data, so a dismantle on your hub pawn does not reach the next fight yet (that is the run/pool work, B-085/B-090). Fight-start refusal is proven by the test only.

**Setup:**
- PIE in the hub or trial with your character holding at least one weapon or ring that is in a loadout, and one that is not.
- Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the console, type `wor.UI.Dismantle` | A screen with the first weapon or ring, Next item, Dismantle, Close | [ ] |
| 2 | Press Next item until an item that is NOT equipped shows, press Dismantle | It goes at once: "Dismantled ...", Gear essence goes up | [ ] |
| 3 | Pick your equipped weapon, press Dismantle | The warning: "This is equipped in loadout 1. Dismantle anyway?", "You'll lose its upgrades." and, if it was your only gear, "... will have no gear and can't fight until you equip something." | [ ] |
| 4 | Press Cancel | The warning closes; the item is still there | [ ] |
| 5 | Press Dismantle again, then Dismantle anyway | "Dismantled ..."; the Output Log shows `took instance ... out of 1 saved loadout(s)` | [ ] |
| 6 | Open your loadout (or its debug print) | The weapon is gone from the loadout, no copy left | [ ] |
| 7 | Close | The screen closes and game input comes back | [ ] |
