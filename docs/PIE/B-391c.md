# PIE — B-391c: buildup bar, gear, durability header and the absorb cue (Q-111, Q-391c)

**Branch:** `feature/B-391c-hud-pieces-2-cloud` (cloud pre-write, UNBUILT, on `feature/B-391b-hud-pieces-cloud`). Build B-391 and B-391b first.
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.UI.BattleHUD`: the buildup fill and its clamps, the crack positions and how many show (none below half, three at 80%, all at the cap), the "Status x / y" line, the tint (element colour, a Broken Darkness attacker darkened, physical neutral), the gear line and tooltip, the durability header's slot rule, "Negated" only for a 0 hit on Broken Darkness. On a duel: a card's buildup line follows `AddStatusBuildup`, 80 / 100 shows three cracks, the party card's gear line matches its loadout, a 0 hit on a Broken Darkness foe floats "Absorbed", and the menu's header matches the acting character.
**How to open it:** start any fight with `wor.UI.NewHUD 1` (the default). Compare with the old HUD: `wor.UI.NewHUD 0`, then start the same fight. `wor.UI.Print` prints every card's buildup line, crack count and gear, and the menu's `durability:` line.
**Behaviour change:** party cards now take the mouse (so the tooltip can show). Enemy cards still let clicks through (check 9).

**Known:** the bar cracks but doesn't **shatter** or lie in shards during a break yet (Q-111 A); the break's status chip is the sign for now (art pass). The bar's seed at fight start uses no attacker, so a bar already part-filled by Broken Darkness before the HUD exists shows the plain element colour until the next hit. Gear and durability follow the loadout events, which fire on the server: a guest's card and header update on its next menu open, as the old HUD. "Negated" is decided from the hit (0 damage on a Broken Darkness target); the hit event doesn't say why it was 0, so a ward that stops a whole hit on Broken Darkness also reads "Negated". A combatant that joins mid-fight (a summon) gets no card, bar or numbers: nothing in the code adds a combatant mid-fight or announces one (no summons exist yet).
