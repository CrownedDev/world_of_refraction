# PIE — B-403: shot-pattern tuning (melee rush, side-on impact, held spread)

**Branch:** `feature/B-403-shot-pattern-tuning-cloud` (cloud pre-write, UNBUILT, from `dev`; on B-371's shot patterns).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Combat.Camera` "shot patterns": a melee wind-up rushes (0.3 s or less, motion blur, toward the target), a spell keeps the slower push without blur, movement off gives the slow push; the impact looks across the caster-target line, low, on the caster's side, with both on screen at 1000 cm and 150 cm; a small FOV punch and a light shake, bigger on a crit; a spread keeps one transform, no shake even on a crit, no punch, every target on screen. `WoR.Camera.Framing`: every shot frames its moment on an empty arena in every style (an impact now needs the caster too).
**Setup:** a fight with a melee ability, a projectile spell and a skill that hits several targets, none with a `CameraSequence`. Camera movement on, skill cinematics on. `wor.Camera.Print` prints the shot now playing (with blur and blend time); `wor.Camera.ShotPreview` prints every shot for a sample fight without one.
**This is a feel pass (B-157).** Compare each moment with the reference fight (`docs/analysis/MaelleCombatPresentationStudy.md` §1: confirm rush 5:33.8, normal hit 5:35.4, area skill 6:53). The numbers are first guesses (`CombatCameraShots` in `CombatCameraTypes.cpp`, `TODO: tune`; Q-403).
