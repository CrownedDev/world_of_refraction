# PIE — B-567: the trial pool, stage 3: the enemy's parts (D-225, TrialPoolDesign §3-4)

**Branch:** `feature/B-567-enemy-parts` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). Nothing changes in a fight yet: the roll (stage 4) reads these. Not seen in the editor yet.

**What it is:** every character asset has an **Enemy | Kit** section:
- **Always Has:** pieces (a weapon, an adapter, a prebuilt find, an evolution or apparel), each at the tier you give it, and skills. Every enemy of this type gets them.
- **Limits** (each with a tick box; unticked means any): most gear, most skills per piece (unticked: every slot), most innate (unticked: D-240's places), kinds, weapon types, elements, a tier range (inside its zone's). (B-567b merged the old most weapons, most adapters and evolution allowed; see sheet B-567b.)
- The class rules always hold on top: a Generic carries 2 weapons and 1 adapter, a Refractor one item, a Resonator 1 weapon and 5 adapters. Skills never pass the slots.
- **Print Enemy Kit** (Debug) logs the kit, the limits it ends up with, and any problem against the class. Data validation warns on an always-has list the class can't carry.
- `wor.Trial.PrintZones` now also warns when an always-has piece's tier is outside its zone's range (the given tier is kept).

**Defaults chosen (Crown may change, Q-567):** see Q-567 (what counts against which limit; the element limit covers crystals and spells; a kit tier range is cut to the zone's).

**Editor steps:**
1. Open the Generic trial enemy (the one that reads `DA_Inventory_Generic` or `DA_Test_Inventory`). Under Enemy > Kit > Always Has, add the Arming Sword at **D**. Tick Most Gear = 1 and set Kinds to Weapon (B-567b; was Most Weapons 1, Most Adapters 0) (the design's example 2; its world levels stay as authored, 1 / 7 / 1: the example's 1 1 1 was made up). Save. Done 2026-11-17 (5d354692d) on `DA_Enemy_Generic`.
2. Leave the other trial enemy's kit empty (example 1: nothing set). Save.
3. On each, press **Print Enemy Kit**.
