# PIE — B-112: a full Light bar lowers crit chance

**Branch:** `fix/B-112-light-break` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. The `WoR.Effects.StatusBreaks` Light case checks four things: a Light break lowers crit chance by the set value; crit chance is 0 while it lasts; it holds through the holder's next turn; and it's gone as the turn after starts.

**What changed (D-030, D-090):** a full **Light** bar used to empty and apply nothing. It now applies "crit chance down" of 100% (no crits; `LIGHT_BREAK_CRIT_CHANCE_DOWN_PERCENT`, to tune) for 1 of the holder's own turns.

**Setup:** a fight where you can fill an enemy's Light bar (a Light spell or Light-infused attack). Open the console with the tilde key; Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fill an enemy's **Light** bar | `wor.PrintEffects` lists "Dimmed" (crit chance down) on that enemy | [ ] |
| 2 | Let that enemy take its next turn and attack | It doesn't crit | [ ] |
| 3 | Wait for the enemy's following turn | The debuff is gone (`wor.PrintEffects`); it can crit again | [ ] |
