# PIE — B-456c: the vendor screen, piece 3 (the Jeweler's Item slots)

**Branch:** `feature/B-456c-item-slots` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.VendorScreen`: the Item slots tab has a row per party member with the pouch's slots and the next slot's price; a pouch the wallet can't pay, or a full one, is greyed with the reason; on the screen the first A asks, Back cancels, the second A buys one slot for the focused member (their pouch grows, the payer's wallet pays the price, nobody else's pouch changes). `WoR.Inventory.ItemSlotUpgrade`: the price, the cap, hub only.
**What changed (D-193, D-097; mockup `hub/vendors/2_jeweler_item_slots.png`):** the Jeweler's **Item slots** tab works on the vendor screen. A row per party member: the name, a mark per slot (lit for each the pouch has), and the next slot's price or "full". The panel says what the pouch will hold, the price, and the price of the slot after. No rule or price changed.
**Follows D-016:** the first A asks, the second buys (one row, one purchase, like Sell and Dismantle).
**Left out:** icons; the old placeholder (`wor.UI.ItemSlots`) is kept in the code until this passes.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, PIE in the hub with a party of two or more. Give yourself Gold: `wor.Wallet.Add Prisms 5000`.
