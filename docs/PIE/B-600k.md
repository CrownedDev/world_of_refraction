# PIE — B-600k: the close Melee Rush shot frames the attacker

**Branch:** `fix/B-600k-rush-frames-attacker` (built 2026-11-25 from dev 4d451b497).
**What it is:** the Melee Rush hit cuts to a close three-quarter on the target. It stood a fixed 2.3 m from the target, so a skill that strikes from farther out (a long reach) left the attacker beside or behind the camera. Now the camera stays behind the attacker on any reach; short reaches look as before.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.Camera.Styles` (reaches 1 to 4.5 m, every re-cut angle: both whole bodies in frame, the attacker clear of the lens and in a side third from 1.5 m) and the full suite plus validation on the final tree: Tests: 2617 passed, 0 failed; Data validation: 12 error(s), 0 new.
**Not proven:** the look in game (real bodies, weapons, the arena).
**Setup:** any fight; a character with a Melee Rush skill (Force Strike, Over Under), and one with a long reach if there is one (a lunge).
