# PIE — B-198: a Luck dodge plays the dodge animation

**Branch:** `fix/B-198-luck-dodge-animation` (in `dev`).

**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Defense.LuckDodge`, "the dodge montage (B-198)", checks the side: right on the first impact, left on the second, and so on. The montage itself can only be seen in PIE.

**What changed:**
- A Luck dodge (D-085, "Lucky dodge!") now plays the dodge montage. Before, the defender showed nothing, or a missed Guard's block.
- The side alternates by impact (Q-198). If you pressed Dodge (mistimed), your own dodge animation is kept, not replayed.
- Known: it starts at the impact, so it reads a little late; a multi-hit attack where several impacts are Luck-dodged restarts it each time (may jitter).

**Setup:**
- A character with high Luck (so Luck dodges happen often), with dodge montages set on its loadout.
- Output Log open (look for "[LuckDodge] Lucky dodge!").

| 1 | Let enemy attacks hit without pressing anything until "Lucky dodge!" shows | The character plays its dodge (right) at that hit and takes no damage | [ ] |
