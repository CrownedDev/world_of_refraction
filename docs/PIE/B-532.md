# PIE — B-532: a prebuilt piece keeps its crystal

**Branch:** `feature/B-532-prebuilt-crystal-lock`, with B-533's change (`fix/B-533-one-socket-take-off`): each socket comes off alone. Both in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16.

**What it is (D-231, Q-532):**
1. A weapon or adapter granted already built keeps its crystal in the first socket. "Built" means a prebuilt find from a chest or a merchant, or starting gear handed out with its crystal (the Earth Ring).
2. Whether that crystal can be taken out is a setting on each prebuilt: **Crystal Removable** on the Prebuilt Find asset and on a class's starting-gear place. **It is on by default (Crown, B-533):** a gem taken out is destroyed, an evolution goes back to your evolutions. Turning it off locks the crystal in.
3. **B-533 (Crown):** on every piece, each socket comes off alone: taking out the crystal leaves the stone, taking off the stone leaves the crystal. What comes out is destroyed. So a fixed prebuilt's stone can come off; only its built-in crystal stays.
4. The second socket takes a stone only. That was already true on every piece; it is unchanged.
5. A piece the player built from an empty one is unchanged.
6. **Save version 43:** an older save is wiped with a warning. Start a new game.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Trial.PrebuiltFind` covers these cases:
  - a fixed prebuilt refuses a second gem, takes a stone, keeps its gem (the screen says why), and lets the stone off alone;
  - a removable one lets its crystal out and takes another;
  - a removable prebuilt's evolution goes back to the owned evolutions, not destroyed;
  - a fixed evolution stays;
  - a player-built ring's gem comes out alone, its stone kept.
- `WoR.Inventory.TwoSockets` (B-533): one socket off at a time, the other kept; what comes out is destroyed.
- `WoR.Loadout.StartingGear`: a ring handed out with its crystal is prebuilt and fixed; an empty sword is not.
- `WoR.Save.GearDetail`: the flags come back after a save.

Full suite: 2293 passed, 0 failed (B-533).
