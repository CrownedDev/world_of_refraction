# PIE — B-064: element resistance changes damage

**Branch:** `feature/B-064-element-damage-resistance` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Damage.Element` (6 tests), which checks:
- Resistance scales elemental damage 1:1, capped at ±50%.
- Ignore Defense drops resistance but keeps weakness.
- A Fire Caster takes less Fire and more Water damage, and non-elemental hits as before.
- The "ignore resistance" flag is honoured.
- The new element event fires once per hit with the element.

Full suite: 619 green.
**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, start a trial fight with an enemy Caster of a known element (e.g. a Fire Caster: +15% Fire, −15% Water).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hit the Fire Caster with a plain attack (no infusion) | Damage as before B-064 | [ ] |
| 2 | Cast a Fire spell on it | About 15% less damage than the same spell on a neutral target | [ ] |
| 3 | Cast a Water spell on it | About 15% more damage | [ ] |
| 4 | Use an Ignore Defense skill with Fire on it | The Fire resistance no longer cuts the damage | [ ] |
| 5 | Equip gear with rolled Fire resistance on a party member and let a Fire enemy hit them | Less Fire damage than without the gear | [ ] |

**Notes:**
- Only per-element resistance counts (class row, gear rolls, element-specific buffs); the general Resistance stat doesn't (D-036).
- Physical damage still goes through Defense only (Q-145).
- Damage numbers can now tint by element: bind `OnElementDamageDealt` on the Action Executor (the HUD doesn't use it yet). It fires after the `OnDamageDealt` listeners, so the target may already be dead.
- Elemental DoT ticks are not resisted yet: Q-064, job B-064b. Water damage also reads Ice resistance (nothing grants it yet, B-064a).
