# PIE — B-162: weapon requirements scale the weapon's damage

**Branch:** `feature/B-162-weapon-requirement-factor` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Weapon.RequirementFactor` (8 tests), which checks:
- The factor is 1 with no requirement, at the requirement and over it.
- Under the requirement, it is the requirement-gap ladder.
- The worst pillar decides.
- The factor never drops below the floor.
- An ability through an under-requirement weapon deals the ladder's share less.
- At or over the requirement, an ability is unchanged.
- Spells ignore the weapon's requirement.
- The debug readout shows the live factor.

Full suite: 600 green.
**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor. You need a weapon with a requirement (Details panel → Requirements, e.g. Body 4) and a party character whose Body level is below it (e.g. 1). Start a trial fight with that weapon equipped.

> **Changed by B-220 (D-101, BaselineNumbers.md Decided):** the requirement now cuts only the weapon's own part (its Power), not the whole hit. Every weapon has Power 0 until it's authored, so checks 1, 2 and 5 show no cut until you give the test weapon a Power (in its data asset, Weapon|Damage). The log line is now `[WeaponPower] <skill>: weapon part N (requirement xF)`. Use `wor.Weapon.Print` to see the factor.
