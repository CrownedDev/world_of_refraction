# PIE — B-569: the trial pool, stage 5: drops (D-225, TrialPoolDesign §6)

**Branch:** `feature/B-569-trial-drops` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). **Built unseen:** not played yet.

**What it is:** a won fight in a trial now drops one thing for each player character (not for recruits or AI seats), on top of the pillar levels.
- It comes from the fight's pool (weapons, adapters, skills, evolutions, prebuilt finds) or is a crystal (every element's, leaning to the trial's).
- Whatever the fight's enemies used (a piece they wore, a skill they knew, a crystal they socketed) is 3 times likelier than the rest.
- A piece an enemy wore drops at the tier it wore; another piece at a tier from the zone's range; a skill at its own tier.
- A weapon or adapter arrives empty and skips the pickup grade roll; a prebuilt find arrives built.
- The results show "Dropped: Arming Sword (D), empty" under the turns.
- The same fight retried drops the same thing (seeded by the run, the fight and the player).

**Defaults chosen (Crown may change, Q-569).** Shortcuts: apparel isn't given yet; a boss's first-defeat extra drop isn't built; a duplicate takes today's path until stage 6; the Q-518 stopgap stays until Crown's PIE pass of stage 4 (B-568).

**Editor steps:** none.
