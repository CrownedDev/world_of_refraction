# PIE — B-028: one loadout rule set

**Branch:** `refactor/B-028-one-loadout-validator` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Loadout.Rules` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - the editor now caps evolution spells by tier (the old check passed 3 spells on an F-tier evolution)
  - BD rules follow the character
  - a wrong-weapon-type ability is reported
  - ownership is only checked where it can be

**What changed:**
- The three loadout checks now share one rule set (D-073 = A). Where they disagreed, the fight's answer wins:
  - the evolution spell cap goes by tier
  - BD rules follow the character
  - spell weight budgets are checked in the editor too
  - Resonator costs come from the real crystal
- The pre-fight error list now also reports what the slot cleaner used to drop silently: an ability for the wrong weapon or wield mode, and a weapon or ring spell without a matching crystal.
- Two new pre-fight messages: more than 6 abilities on a weapon, and an item quantity outside 0-3. They are reported, not removed yet (B-159).
- In the editor, a character's **Validate** now also checks its inventory's loadouts with its real BD flag (as warnings). The editor check uses no mastery discount on spell weights (**D-079**).
- Nothing else changes in play. The same slots get cleared before a fight.
