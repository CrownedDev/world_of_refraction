# PIE — B-328b: the zone's tier guide, enforced as a report (D-120, Q-328)

**Branch:** `feature/B-328b-zone-guide-enforced` (in `dev`; built on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**What changed:** `wor.Trial.PrintZones` now also lists every encounter on the map whose authored enemies sit below the guide tier of the zone they stand in ("Camp_3: zone tier 4 asks for Tier A or higher; Goblin is Tier F"), as Warning lines, or says every encounter fits. The enemy's tier here is its asset's `EnemyTier`, a static proxy: a geared enemy's fielded Combat Tier averages its gear and skills and can land above or below it, so the report is exact for gearless enemies and a hint for the rest. Nothing is enforced in play: it is the map author's checklist.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Trial.EnemyZone` (a weak enemy deep in is reported by name, a strong team fits, a weak enemy outside every zone fits); the `WoR.Trial` subset green (38); data validation 0 new.
