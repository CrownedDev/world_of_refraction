# PIE — D-221 stage 1 (B-505, B-506, B-507): what a character wears is kept

**Branches:** `feature/B-505-worn-gear-record`, `feature/B-506-maps-wear-the-record`, `feature/B-507-save-worn-gear` (all in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** each character now has one "wearing now" record (its gear, the weapon in hand, apparel, spells and the wheel pick). The first time a character appears, its own loadout becomes the record; after that every map (hub, trial, fight) puts the record on, every change writes it at once, and the save keeps it (save version 40). Before, the character's own loadout was rebuilt from its asset on every map, so changes to it were lost.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Loadout.WornGear` (5) and `WoR.Loadout.WornGear.Maps` (4): no loss turning a loadout into the record and back; the record is seeded once; a gear change survives the next map and a fight; an enemy sharing the asset neither reads nor changes it; a hub pawn wears it once tied to its member. `WoR.Field.WheelWeaponCarry` (3): the wheel's weapon is the record's ACTIVE entry. `WoR.Save`: the record and the wheel pick come back after a save and load.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. Co-op guests are host-only (B-230).
**Your old save is wiped** on the first load (version 40), with the usual warning: start a new game.

**The debug line:** `wor.Loadout.PrintWorn` (or `wor.Loadout.PrintWorn Lord`, any part of the character's name) prints each record: primary, secondary, adapters, the `[ACTIVE]` piece, evolution, apparel and the wheel pick.
