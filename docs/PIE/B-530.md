# PIE — B-530: an adapter pick puts the weapon away; adapters skip the duplicate rule; the adapter icon

**Branch:** `fix/B-530-hub-adapter-pick` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is:** three fixes from Crown's first hub PIE (new game as the Generic).
1. **Picking a ring kept the weapon in hand.** The field wheel still had the old rule: on a weapon user, a ring adds its element to the weapon in hand ("Earth Axes"). Every ring is an adapter now (D-223), so every adapter is an element source like the innate or an evolution. Picking it puts the weapon away and the field attack is the pulse (D-218). Only a weapon's own pick shows the weapon.
2. **The log repeated "received a second DA_Ring_Adapter".** The Generic's start has five spare empty adapters of one asset. The one-copy rule (D-115: a second copy goes to Gold or is dismantled) now covers weapons only. Every adapter is its own piece (D-223), so several of one asset are kept with no notice.
3. **The adapter icon (D-229), look only.** An adapter shows the socket frame with its crystal's icon inside. With nothing socketed, the frame's centre is empty. This uses the Equipment screen's own socket frame and element badges; no new art. It shows on the quick strip tile and the Equipment screen's rows (the entry list and the Add window's adapter lines), in place of the text-only tile.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Field`: a weapon user's ring is a pulse with no swing; its pick hides the weapon and the sword's pick shows it again.
- `WoR.Economy.DuplicateGear`: five authored spares of one adapter are all owned, a sixth too, with no notice and no pairs.
- `WoR.UI.IconTable` and the Equipment model spec: the adapter icon is the frame, with the crystal's badge inside or an empty centre.

Full suite in the session note.

**No editor step.** The frame and the badges come from the icon table (`DA_UI_IconTable`, filled in B-486). If its empty-socket icon were unset, the tile would show the name, as before.
