# PIE — B-279d: fight a character form as a mini-boss

**Branch:** `feature/B-279d-form-minibosses` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Character.FormMiniBoss`: the form's character leads the encounter's team (still at most three) and makes the fight a mini-boss (a boss stays a boss); spawned like a real combatant, it holds its authored primary weapon. Full suite green.

What changed: an Encounter Data asset has a **Mini Boss Form** slot. Put a character form there and that character leads the team, in its form's look, with its own loadout; the fight can't be fled.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. You need a `CharacterForm` asset whose character has an inventory with a loadout (none is authored yet). On a placed enemy team (`AFieldEnemyTeam`) or encounter, set its Encounter Data's **Mini Boss Form**. For phases, a `UBossData` naming that character.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Select the Encounter Data, press **Print Encounter** | "led by form <name> as a mini-boss"; the character listed first | [ ] |
| 2 | Touch the team in PIE | The fight starts; the form's character is on the enemy side, first in line | [ ] |
