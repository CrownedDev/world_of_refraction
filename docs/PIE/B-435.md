# PIE — B-435: same-element infusion sources stack (D-174)

**Branch:** `feature/B-435-same-element-stacking` (in dev, built 2026-11-14; written by the cloud, built and tested on the desktop).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Infusion.SameElementStack`: an infused ability uses every same-element source the character has; a stacked innate adds its EP, a stacked gem takes its wear; buildup is x1.25 with two sources and capped at x1.50; a different-element or broken source doesn't join; spells and uninfused actions don't stack. `WoR.Infusion.Cost`, `WoR.Infusion.SourceResolver` and the AI infusion specs still pass.
**What changed:** when an ability or attack is infused, every one of the character's sources with the same element joins in (innate, weapon gem, ring gem, evolution). Each pays its own cost: a stacked innate or evolution adds its EP, a stacked gem takes its wear, and the level-2 HP backlashes add up. Status buildup gets +25% per extra source, up to +50%. Damage is unchanged. **Spells don't stack** (Q-435, default: a spell keeps its one source, so a free ring spell doesn't start costing innate EP).
**Also fixed on the way:** an evolution in the character's own slot used to pay no level-2 HP (and no wear on abilities) when used as the infusion source; it now pays both.
**Setup:** a Fire-innate character with a Fire gem in the weapon, an ability that builds a status, an enemy with a visible buildup bar. In the console: `Log LogTemp Verbose` to see the stack line.
