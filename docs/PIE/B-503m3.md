# PIE — B-503m3: the LB wheel (D-217, Q-503m3)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503m3-field-source-wheel` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** step 1 of Crown's four (apply the UI). In a trial, **holding R / LB** (half a second) opens a small wheel in the middle of the screen with every stop the character can use, in the strip's names: the weapon plain (Sword), each source's element (Fire Sword), and for Reality / Broken Darkness their (R) Fire / Dark Fire steps. The **right stick** picks, the **mouse wheel** steps it, **letting go** confirms. A **quick tap** still steps to the next source. A placeholder look in C++ (no `.uasset`); the real look comes after Crown's check.

**This pass is look only** (Crown's step 2). Say what to change about the wheel's look and feel; the code and data are aligned afterwards.

**Already proven by tests:** the stick picks entry 0 at the top, then clockwise, nothing inside half tilt; the mouse wheel steps both ways and wraps.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. Whether the camera really holds still on the right stick depends on how the pawn's camera key is mapped (check 4).
