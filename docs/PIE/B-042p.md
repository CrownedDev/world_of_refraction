# PIE — B-042p: GAS stage 3, part 5f (stat sources on GAS)

**Branch:** `feature/B-042p-gas-stat-sources` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.GAS.StatSources` (5 cases) checks:
- for spell damage, raw damage, action speed and spell speed, GAS holds the stat's own part and multiplies in gear, stones and buffs, giving exactly today's number;
- a buff updates the GAS effects in place, and a removed one comes back;
- the cap matches;
- every other GAS stat still equals today's number.

The full suite (695) is green.

What changed:
- These four stats are now built on GAS from their layers.
- The game still reads today's numbers, so nothing you see should change.
