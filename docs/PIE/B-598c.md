**Branch:** `feature/B-598c-B-614-v5-picker` (on top of `feature/B-575c-chest-off`).
**Mockups:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/loadout/v5/` and `menus/inventory/v4/` (Crown 2026-11-25: "Looks good").
**Decision:** Q-598c (pages in place of D-249's two sides; the Gear / Pouch selector by Up; the Inventory page's A and X); Q-614 (what the picker's kind selector turns).
