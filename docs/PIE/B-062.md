# PIE — B-062: effect identity (who an effect belongs to)

**Branch:** `feature/B-062-effect-identity` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Effects.Identity` (14 cases) checks:
- each wearer of once-per-match gear gets their own use;
- an instant's once-per-match use is spent;
- the same attacker filling a bar again refreshes the effect, and two attackers give two;
- two items with one effect add up, and the same item refreshes;
- a "doesn't stack" effect holds one copy and gives one use;
- a re-apply from the same caster replaces the value.

**What changed:** an effect now belongs to whoever gave it, so:
- **Full bars:** the same attacker filling your bar again refreshes its effect (one Burn, timer reset). A second attacker adds their own. Before, every full bar added another copy.
- **Gear:** two items with the same effect add up (Sword +10% and Ring +10% = +20%). Before, they merged into one.
- **Skills:** two enemies casting the same debuff on you give two debuffs. One caster re-casting refreshes their own, with the new value, even if it is weaker. Before, a weaker re-cast was ignored. Garnet's Burn and infusion DoTs follow the same rule.
- **Once per match:** each character gets their own use.

**Setup:** any fight with two enemies that can fill the same status bar (e.g. Fire) on one of your characters. Open the console with the tilde key and the Output Log.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Let one enemy fill your character's Fire bar, then type `wor.PrintEffects` | One Burn. The line shows the enemy as its source and `Status Bar Trigger` | [ ] |
| 2 | Let the **same** enemy fill the Fire bar again before the Burn ends, then type `wor.PrintEffects` | Still one Burn; its duration is back to full | [ ] |
| 3 | Let the **other** enemy fill the same bar | Two Burns, one per enemy; both tick | [ ] |
| 4 | Equip two items that share an effect (same effect definition), start a fight, type `wor.PrintEffects` on that character | Two copies, one per item; the stat shows both added | [ ] |
| 5 | Cast the same debuff twice in a row from one character on one enemy | One copy; its duration refreshes | [ ] |
| 6 | Play a full fight as usual (Defend, buffs, DoTs) | Nothing else behaves differently; Defend still refreshes | [ ] |
