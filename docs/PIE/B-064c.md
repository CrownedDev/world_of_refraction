# PIE — B-064c: Ice, the rest

**Branch:** `feature/B-064c-ice-rest-cloud` (cloud), built and merged as `feature/B-064c-ice-rest` (in dev).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Elements.Ice` (the profile asset's row keeps Ice; gear Ice lines sum; the Ice key survives only on a Water resistance buff / Ward; a Water Ward catches Water not Ice, one keyed Ice catches both, an Elemental Ward catches Ice; an Ice-keyed resistance buff resists Ice and Water once each, not Fire; a Water-scoped damage buff raises a Water spell, not an Ice one; a Water Status Multiplier raises Water buildup, not Ice), `WoR.Resistance.Columns` (eleven columns, Ice last), and unchanged: `WoR.Resistance.OnePhysical`, `WoR.Resistance.Seam`, `WoR.Damage.Scope`, `WoR.Damage.Element`, `WoR.GAS.Absorb`, `WoR.GAS.EffectMirror`, `WoR.Effects.Keyed`.

**Setup:** B-064a's Ice spell (a **Water** spell with **Identity > Deals Ice** ticked) and a plain Water spell on a Refractor with a Water ring. No asset in the game uses the new fields; the checks below tick them on a test copy (name it per `docs/Conventions/ContentNaming.md`) and put them back after.

**Known:** `UActionExecutor::ApplyDamage`'s secondary damage is still plain Water; the impact VFX and bar tint follow the key (Water) for an Ice hit; Defense, Reflect and InvertDamage can't be keyed Ice yet, nor an Element-scoped boost (a Water-scoped Defense buff simply doesn't reach an Ice hit now); no ability deals Ice; the class table's Ice column is 0 everywhere until tuned (tune it in the profile asset). The gear resistance roll never rolls Ice (Q-064c; the roll is retired anyway, B-335). No Blueprint-facing field was removed; the new fields are appended `UPROPERTY`s (`FResistanceBonus::Ice`, `FResistanceProfileRow::Ice`, `FResistanceProfileDisplay::Ice`, `FSkillEffectPayload::bKeyedIce`, `FActiveSkillEffect::bKeyedIce`, `FWoRAbsorbCharge::bKeyedIce`, `FWoREffectIdentity::bKeyedIce`), so old assets and saves read 0 / false.
