# PIE — B-220: weapon Power and scaling

**Branch:** `feature/B-220-weapon-power` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Weapon.Power`: a higher-Power weapon hits harder; a Body-A weapon gains more from Body than a Body-C one.
- `WoR.Weapon.RequirementFactor`: under the requirement still cuts (the weapon part).
- The full suite (799) is green.

What changed:
- `Power` (per upgrade level) and `StatScaling` grades on weapon assets. Both default to 0 / none, so damage is unchanged until you author them.
- **The requirement penalty now only cuts the weapon part**, so until Power is set, being under a weapon's requirement costs nothing (the B-162 check changes accordingly).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. Open a weapon asset you use in the demo fight.
