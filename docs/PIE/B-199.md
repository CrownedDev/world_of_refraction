# PIE — B-199: a Void break plays the holder's next turn at random

**Branch:** `feature/B-199-void-random-action` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Effects.VoidRandomAction` checks four things: a Void-broken member you play has its turn played without you; the turn after is yours again; an unbroken one waits for your command; the random pick is always an action the rules accept.

**What changed:** filling a combatant's Void bar now takes their next turn away, like Final Fantasy's Confuse. The game picks one of their usable actions at random (Defend, basic attack, a spell, an ability) and aims it at a random target on the side it is made for: attacks at an enemy, heals and buffs at the holder or an ally. You can't switch who plays a Void-broken member. That applies to your members and to the AI's. Items, Flee and switching are never picked (Q-199).

**Setup:** a fight where Void buildup can fill a bar: a Void spell or weapon on one side. Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fill an enemy's Void bar | On its next turn, Output Log "[AIDecisionManager] <enemy> is Void-broken - random action <name> (N choices)"; it acts | [ ] |

| 3 | The turn after | The member's command menu opens as normal (the break lasts one turn) | [ ] |
