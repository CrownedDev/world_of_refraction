# PIE — B-558: a recruit wears its own spirit (one-player stage 4b, second half)

**Branch:** `feature/B-558-recruit-spirit-looks` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). **Built unseen** (overnight run): nobody has looked at it in PIE yet.

**What it is (D-238):** a Spirit recruit (B-557) in the party chooses its spirit from the same list as the player: Male Nobody, Female Nobody, and every spirit the player has unlocked.
- On the Spirit screen (the mirror, or Game menu > Spirit), **Z / C** (or **LT / RT**) step through the party members, as on the loadout screen. The title says whose spirit it is.
- **A** wears the lit row for that member. **Y** switches its body.
- The same rules as the player's apply: only unlocked spirits, only in the hub or at a rest spot, never in a fight.
- **Its colour:** on its starting body, with no spirit worn, a recruit shows its Blueprint's own look, which carries its element or class colour. With a spirit worn, that spirit's mesh shows. On the other Nobody body it shows that plain body.
- It shows on the recruit standing in the hub (at load and right after a change) and on its pawn in a fight.
- Saved with the party member.

**Not in this job:**
- The player's own body colour (D-238: a new character takes its element or class colour). The player's Nobody body has no coloured material yet; this needs an editor look at the mannequin materials.
- A recruit's worn spirit does not turn on that spirit's bound-gear bonus. That follows the player's worn spirit only.
- Co-op: another player's character on the screen can't change spirit here.

**Editor steps:** none beyond B-557 (Fill Recruits, each Body set).
