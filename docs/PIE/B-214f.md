# PIE — B-214f: field skills part 6 (the field attack)

**Branch:** `feature/B-214f-field-attack-cloud` (cloud, **unbuilt**: build first; it carries B-214b to B-214e).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Field.Attack` (the strike: a real element only, held Water = Ice, the weapon's physical type, the buildup; the source switch; a swing at nothing and its cooldown). Not provable headless: striking an encounter, the level change, the buildup in the fight.
**Setup:** pull the branch, build, play a trial map with an encounter, the leader holding a weapon with a Fire crystal (or a Water one for check 5). The keys come with B-214g: use `wor.Field.Attack` / `wor.Field.SwitchSource`.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Far from enemies, `wor.Field.Attack` | `Attack: None` and the strike it would carry (`Fire`, the weapon's Slash / Pierce / Impact, buildup 25); nothing else | [ ] |
| 2 | Walk up to an enemy from the front (it sees you), stop 1–2 m away, `wor.Field.Attack` | The fight starts; the log says `struck on the field` and `First strike: Party`; the party acts first | [ ] |
| 3 | In that fight, look at the enemies' status bars before anyone acts | Every enemy in the fight starts with Fire buildup (the log: `Field strike: Fire / ... buildup 25 on N of N enemies`) | [ ] |
| 4 | `wor.Field.SwitchSource` twice, then `wor.Field.Print` | `attack source Innate (...)`: the character's own element now | [ ] |
| 5 | With a Water source, strike an enemy with `wor.Field.Attack Hold` | The log shows `Water (Ice)`; the enemies' bars fill as Ice | [ ] |
| 6 | Walk into an enemy without attacking | The fight starts as before (B-185's first strike rule), no field strike buildup | [ ] |
| 7 | 2-player PIE: the guest strikes an enemy | The fight starts with the party first and the buildup | [ ] |
