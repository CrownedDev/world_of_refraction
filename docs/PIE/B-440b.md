# PIE — B-440b: the loadout screen, piece 2 (change gear)

**Branch:** `feature/B-440b-loadout-change-gear` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/loadout/1_gear_generic.png` (the A Change / X Unequip hints).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.LoadoutScreen` (13 cases; 4 new): each gear row's place and a row for each free place; the picker offers the owned weapons or adapters the place takes (not the ones already worn in this loadout), each with the budget after it, and keeps refused lines (over the budget, worn by another member) with their reason; put in hand, swap, take off; the last primary can't come off, and a Generic's second weapon moves up when the first comes off (D-116); a Resonator's adapter added in the free place and taken off.
**What changed:** the loadout screen can now **change** weapons and adapters. A on a row opens a picker of what you own for that place; A again puts the lit one in. X takes the lit piece off. A two-weapon member's picker starts with "Put … in hand".
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** how the picker looks, the amber budget, the keys and gamepad, the mouse.
**Left out of this piece:** apparel (A or X on an apparel row says it can't be changed here yet: new row **B-440f**); sockets (B-440c); skills (B-440d); saved loadouts (B-440e).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor. Use a party member who owns more weapons or adapters than it wears (or give one with `wor.UI.Give`). Esc ends PIE in the editor: use the gamepad, or `wor.UI.Pause` and Backspace.
