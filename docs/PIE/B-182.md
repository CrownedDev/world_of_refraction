# PIE — B-182: Perfect Guard plays the parry animation

**Branch:** `fix/B-182-perfect-guard-parry-anim` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Defense.Guard` checks the rule: only a successful Perfect Guard switches to the parry animation; a normal Guard keeps the block, a Dodge keeps its own, a missed press changes nothing.

**What changed:** pressing Guard still plays the block animation straight away (the game cannot know yet whether the timing is perfect). When the hit lands and the press was Perfect, your character switches to the parry animation, like a deflect in Sekiro. A normal Guard looks as before.

**Setup:** a fight against an enemy with a melee attack; your character has block and parry montages on its loadout. Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guard a hit with normal timing | The block animation only; Output Log shows only the block montage playing, no parry | [ ] |
| 2 | Guard a hit with Perfect timing (right on the impact) | The block animation starts, then the parry animation plays at the impact; Output Log "Playing <parry montage>" after "PERFECT" | [ ] |
| 3 | Perfect Guard every hit of a multi-hit attack | The parry animation plays at each perfect impact; the fight carries on normally after the attack | [ ] |
| 4 | Dodge a hit | The dodge animation only, as before | [ ] |

Known, visual only: if you press Guard for two hits before the first lands and the first is Perfect, the parry may still be showing for the second (normal) Guard. A Luck dodge still shows the block animation (B-198). The AI presses at the impact, so its block is replaced by the parry in the same frame.
