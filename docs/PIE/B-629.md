# PIE — B-629: the Skills tab (side-scroll entry selector, slots, Details, the picker's Fits / All learned)

**Branch:** `feature/B-629-skills-tab` (on top of `feature/B-598c-B-614-v5-picker`).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/skills/v1/` (1 the tab, 2 picking a skill).
**Decision:** Q-629 (the entries list goes, the slots are the tab's top level; the picker stays a list for now).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.LoadoutV2Skills`, `WoR.UI.LoadoutSkills`, `WoR.UI.LoadoutV2Screen` and the full suite, headless.
**Setup:** the QA save folder only (`-saveddirsuffix=QA`), Play in a new window, in the hub. A Generic with a weapon and an adapter shows two entries; the Caster shows its weapon and Innate spells.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the game menu (Tab), Inventory, then E twice to Skills | No list of entries: the left column reads "Skill slots N / M", "Entry 1 of 2 · one list for the whole entry", the ‹ entry name › plate, then the entry's slots. A filled slot shows its EP at the right; an empty one reads Empty slot | [ ] |
| 2 | Up / Down over the slots | The middle column shows the lit skill's details (name, tier, EP, power, targets, effects); an empty slot says "Empty slot: pick a skill for it" | [ ] |
| 3 | Right, then Left (or click the entry's name) | The plate turns to the next entry and back; the slots, "Skill slots N / M" and "Entry X of Y" follow; the first slot is lit. With one entry nothing turns | [ ] |
| 4 | Look at Details on the right | The entry's name, then Slots, Used, Free (Innate spells: Points N / M too), and three buttons: A Change this slot, Y Take it out (greyed on an empty slot: "The slot is empty."), X Short / full | [ ] |
| 5 | X, then click the Short / full button | The middle column switches between the full effect tree and the short form, each time | [ ] |
| 6 | Q / E and Z / C on the slots | Page and character change from here (the slots are the tab's top level). B closes the screen | [ ] |
| 7 | On Equipment, light a weapon and press K (R3) | The Skills page opens on that weapon's slots; B goes back to Equipment on the same line | [ ] |
| 8 | On Skills, A on an empty slot | The picker: title "Slot N · pick a skill", the ‹ Fits this slot  All learned › plate with Fits this slot underlined, the skills that fit this slot | [ ] |
| 9 | Light a skill in the picker | Details: "Slot N on <entry> · if you put in <skill>", then Replaces (nothing (empty slot)), Skills now → then, Slots free now → then, EP | [ ] |
| 10 | Right (or click All learned) | All learned underlined; skills already in the entry show "✓ SLOTTED" and are greyed; skills for other gear are greyed with what they need. Details adds a Can't line on those. A on one is refused with the reason, the picker stays | [ ] |
| 11 | Left, then A on a skill that fits | Back to Fits this slot; A puts it in, back to the slots with that slot lit and its EP shown; Used and Free change in Details | [ ] |
| 12 | Y on that slot | It comes out; the slot reads Empty slot | [ ] |
| 13 | A on a filled slot, light a different skill, look at Details | Replaces names the slot's skill; EP reads its EP → the new one's | [ ] |
