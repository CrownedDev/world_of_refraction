# PIE — B-163: broken sources (D-083)

**Branch:** `feature/B-163-broken-sources` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Combat.BrokenSources` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - a broken ring crystal keeps its F-tier spells usable and greys out the rest, with a reason
  - an intact ring blocks nothing
  - a broken weapon crystal greys out every spell it gave, keeps plain attacks, and offers no weapon-crystal infusion
  - the executor refuses a broken-source spell with the same reason the menu shows (this covers the AI too)

**What changed (D-083):**
- When a weapon's or ring's crystal breaks, its spells **stay in the menu, greyed out**, with the reason as the description. Before, they vanished.
  - **Ring:** only its F-tier spells stay usable ("Source broken: this ring casts F-tier spells only.").
  - **Weapon:** none of its spells can be cast, and you can't infuse through it ("Source broken: the weapon's crystal is broken."). Physical abilities and plain attacks still work.
- A Resonator's ring still auto-switches to the next usable ring when its crystal breaks (B-024), so check 2 is easiest with a Generic or Caster ring, or a Resonator with only one ring.
- "Elemental abilities" are read as abilities infused through the weapon's crystal (abilities have no element of their own). See **Q-163**.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a test fight from the trial level. `wor.BreakCrystal` breaks the acting character's primary weapon crystal. There is no ring command: for check 2, set the ring's crystal durability to 0 in its loadout (inventory asset), or cast its spells until it breaks.
