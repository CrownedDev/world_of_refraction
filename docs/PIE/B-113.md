# PIE — B-113: infused status buildup (no game change)

**Branch:** `fix/B-113-status-mult-once` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Status.InfusedBuildup` (2 cases) checks two things. First, the infusion charge band grows with the user's Status Multiplier. Second, the landed buildup is multiplied by the Status Multiplier again.

**What changed:** nothing in play. The rules review suspected a bug: the Status Multiplier counts twice on an infused hit. It does, but by design. Every charge band is scaled by its stat, and damage counts its stat twice the same way (Q-113, approved default). This job confirmed it and pinned it in a test.

**Setup:** any fight with an infused spell or ability.
