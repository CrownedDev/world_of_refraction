# PIE — B-170a: action pips 1, actions on the character

**Branch:** `feature/B-170a-action-pips` (in dev; the cloud's branch, built, full suite green).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Combat.ActionPips` (Max 2 with no action points, +25 +50 = "Max 2 (+75/100)", 175 = "Max 3 (+75/100)", the safety cap; regain 1 / 2 / 3 at Reflex 0.5 / 0.8; the regain never fills past Max and adds nothing at or above it; a gain passes Max only when allowed (the extra is lost at the holder's turn end since Q-170f2, B-170f); spend refuses a cost above the bank; lose never goes below 0; a fight starts with the regain capped at Max; `OnActionsChanged` fires once per change; `CurrentActions` replicates), plus unchanged: `WoR.Coop.CharacterDataReplication`, `WoR.GAS.StatSources`.

**Nothing in play changes.** No turn reads the actions yet (B-170b, B-170c). These checks only confirm the numbers through the console.

**Setup:** start any fight in PIE (the usual battle map), then open the console.
