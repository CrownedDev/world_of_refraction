# PIE — B-572: the trial pool, stage 8: seeing an enemy's kit (D-225, TrialPoolDesign §7)

**Branch:** `feature/B-572-kit-sight` (built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-18). **Built unseen:** not played yet.

**What it is:** the field variation of the kit-sight skill. Used near an enemy on the trial map, before the fight, it costs 10 EP and opens that enemy's kit card: its name, class and pillar levels, each piece with its tier and slots, and its skills. The kit is the one that enemy will fight with this run.
- The run remembers the enemy's type once it has been read.
- A skill asset carries the ability through a new field, **Kit Sight** (None / Field / Always On). No skill has it set yet, so `wor.Trial.KitSight 1` gives it to you for testing.
- There is no key yet: `wor.Trial.InspectEnemy` uses it on the nearest enemy.

**Not built, waiting on Crown (Q-572):** the key for the field use; how the always-on variation shows gear as you get close; what a remembered type shows on other enemies of that type; the two skill assets.

**Editor steps:** none to test. Later: two skill assets with Kit Sight set.
