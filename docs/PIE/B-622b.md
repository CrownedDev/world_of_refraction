# PIE — B-622b: weapon buffs are never tinted

**Branch:** `fix/B-622b-untint-weapon-buffs` (built 2026-11-25).
**What it is:** Crown: "wait rally doesnt get a tint as buffs cannot be infused" (D-275; this replaces B-622's "Yes, tint only"). A weapon buff or debuff such as Rally keeps its own look, with an infusion on or off. A ritual's arming charge still takes its infusion's colour (B-624: rituals are attacks).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Presentation.WeaponBuffTint` checks that a Fire caster's Rally, sent with its Fire infusion riding on it, is not a Fire action and its VFX gets no tint; with none on, also no tint. The full suite plus validation on the final tree are in the hand-off.
**Setup:** Rally in the sword's abilities, a fighter with an elemental infusion source (a Caster's Innate, or an adapter), and enough EP.
