# PIE — B-503m: card and strip fixes (Crown's PIE of B-503k)

**Branch:** `fix/B-503m-field-card-bars` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** five fixes from Crown's PIE notes. R / LB no longer flashes the strip. The cards' level comes from the map: the hub map (`TestLevel_Nav`) shows names only, any other map shows the trial card, run or not. The HP number is drawn on the HP bar and EP has its own bar with its number on it (no separate HP / EP lines), in the field and in battle. The status build-up bar has a dim "Status" label, and the bars' empty part is a quiet tint. Holding R / LB now takes 0.5 s (was 0.4 s; B-503m2, after a first 0.6 s).

**Why the hub showed the trial card (most likely):** a save that closed mid-run is restored on the hub's first frame and only ended a tick later, and the HUD reads its level once when it is built. Playing a trial map straight from the editor had the opposite problem (no run, so hub cards). Both now follow the map.

**Already proven by tests:** the level (no world: hub; a non-hub map with no run: trial; with a run: trial); a trial and battle card builds an EP bar with its number and an HP number, the hub card neither; the EP bar's fill in the field and in a fight.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. The hub-map case can't be tested headless (the test map is never the hub), so check 1 is the proof.
