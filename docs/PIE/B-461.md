# PIE — B-461: the inventory screen, piece 2 (filters, Sort, evolution and apparel tiles, the place name)

**Branch:** `feature/B-461-inventory-piece-2` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.InventoryScreen` (4 new cases): the Gear tab lists the member's evolutions and apparel, with their verbs refused and not on another member's tab; each tab has its own filter row and a filter shows only its kind; Tier sorts highest first and Name A to Z; on the screen, Up reaches the filter row, Right filters, Back returns to the tiles, Y sorts, a new tab starts on All.
**What changed (D-181, `UIScreens.md` #4):** the inventory screen has a **filter row** over the Gear and Chest tiles, **Sort** on Y (R on the keyboard), **evolution and apparel tiles** on the Gear tab, and **the place name** top right.
**Decided by default (Q-461, you may change any):** each tab's filters are All and only what that tab can hold (Gear: Weapons, Adapters, Evolutions, Apparel; Chest: Weapons, Adapters, Crystals; the Pouch has no row), so the mockup's Crystals filter is on the Chest, not on Gear; Sort steps Kind, Tier, Name; Up from the top row of tiles reaches the filters; evolutions and apparel list Give and Store greyed ("can't be given or stored yet", Q-323).
**Not built yet:** icons (a letter stands in: W, A, C, S, **Ev** evolution, **Ap** apparel).
**Do B-441's sheet first** if it isn't done: this one only checks what is new.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, PIE in the hub with a party of two or more that owns a spare weapon, an adapter, an evolution and some crystals. For an apparel piece: console `wor.Loadout.WearApparel <ApparelData asset path>`.
