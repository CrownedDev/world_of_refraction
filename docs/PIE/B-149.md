# PIE — B-149: members start a fight in their chosen cell

**Branch:** `feature/B-149-formation-cells` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Grid.FormationStart` (3 cases) checks three things:
- A member with a chosen cell starts there, with the Back row damage modifier; one without a cell starts Middle/centre.
- Of two members who chose the same cell, the second is placed in its row.
- A real fight start uses the party's cells and puts a Refractor enemy at the back and a Generic enemy at the front.

**What changed (D-042):** members start in their chosen formation cell instead of everyone in the Middle row, so the row bonuses (Front: +5% damage dealt, about 5% more taken; Back: the reverse) apply from turn 1. Enemies start Front or Back by class until their cells are authored (Q-149). **Damage numbers shift a little**, even in the legacy test-actor fights, because the enemy is no longer in the Middle row.

**Setup:** the hub with a party of at least two. Open the console with the tilde key; Output Log open. **Placeholder controls:** `wor.UI.PartyControl` has a "starts:" button per member (each click steps to the next cell), or `wor.Party.SetFormation <slot> <Back|Middle|Front> <column 0-2>`. `wor.GridPositions` prints where everyone stands in a fight.
