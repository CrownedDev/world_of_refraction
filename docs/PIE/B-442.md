# PIE — B-442: the character sheet

**Branch:** `feature/B-442-character-sheet` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/character/1_character_sheet.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.CharacterSheet` (5 cases): the two number formats; the sub-stats grouped under Mind, Body and Spirit, each with a value; resistance lists All, Physical and Spell, then the nine elements, and All is the Defence number; every sub-stat has a name; with no party the sheet says so and closes on Back.
**What changed:** the game menu's Character page opens a character sheet: who they are, their pillars and sub-stats, and how much of each damage type they resist. Read only.
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** how it looks, the keys and gamepad, and whether each number reads the way you expect.
**Left out:** the portrait; "where it comes from" on R3; Y to the loadout; final art.
**What the numbers are (Q-442):** every value is the one the fight uses for that character right now, gear and stones included. A stat that multiplies reads "x1.48"; a stat that is a share reads "9%".

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor. The editor step of `docs/PIE/B-439.md` still applies. Esc ends PIE in the editor: use the gamepad, or `wor.UI.Pause` and Backspace.
