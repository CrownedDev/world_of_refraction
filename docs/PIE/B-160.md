# PIE — B-160: camera cinematic settings (D-078)

**Branch:** `feature/B-160-camera-cinematic-settings` (in `dev`).

**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Combat.Camera` has new `ShouldPlayCinematic` cases:
  - by default, cinematics follow camera movement (movement off skips them)
  - forced on, they play with movement off; forced off, they never play
  - enemy cinematics play unless that setting is off, and turning it off doesn't affect your own side

**What changed:**
- The three D-078 settings now exist on the combat camera. Defaults keep today's behaviour:
  1. **Skill cinematics:** follow camera movement (default), always on, or always off.
  2. **Enemy skill cinematics:** on (default) or off.
  3. **Skip after the hit:** off (default). When on, a cinematic is cut back to the turn view once its caster's first hit lands. A multi-hit skill is cut after its first hit.
- Changing a setting while a cinematic plays cuts it if the new settings skip it.
- No settings screen yet. Use the console:
  - `wor.Camera.Cinematics 0|1|2`: follow camera movement, on, off
  - `wor.Camera.EnemyCinematics 0|1`
  - `wor.Camera.SkipAfterHit 0|1`
  - `wor.Camera`: prints all of them
- No skill has a cinematic until B-157 makes the templates. To see one play, give a skill's **Camera Sequence** (Presentation) any Level Sequence first.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a test fight from the trial level. For checks 3 to 6, set one player skill's and one enemy skill's Camera Sequence to any Level Sequence.
