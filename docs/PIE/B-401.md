# PIE — B-401: no reset between actions

**Branch:** `feature/B-401-no-reset-between-actions-cloud` (cloud pre-write, UNBUILT, on `feature/B-404-wide-beats-cloud`, with dev merged in for B-170c; no save change).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Combat.Camera` "no reset between actions (B-401, study R4)": the second action of a turn targets the turn start's own transform in all three turn-view styles, as a blend shorter than the handover; no return before an action, on an enemy's watch view or with movement off. `WoR.Combat.Camera.Director`: a turn start's ask is no return; each ask after an action is; a refused pick asked again is not; no wide beat and no extra `OnTurnHandover` between actions of one turn; the next hero's turn still gets B-404's beat; movement off stays static. Unchanged: the rest of `WoR.Combat.Camera`.
**This is a feel check:** between two actions of one hero's turn, does the camera settle straight back behind the same hero (Expedition 33), not swing out wide and back? The time is a first guess (`TODO: tune` in `CombatCameraTypes.cpp`, `CombatCameraSameTurn::ReturnBlendSeconds` 0.35).
**Setup:** `wor.Turns.ActionPips 1` **before** touching an enemy (B-170c), a party of 2 heroes against 1–2 enemies, camera movement on. Try Centred Wide and then Over the Shoulder (`wor.Camera.Style 1`). Watch the Output Log with `LogWoRCombat`.
