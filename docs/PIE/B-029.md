# PIE — B-029: the AI estimates damage the way a real hit works

**Branch:** `fix/B-029-ai-damage-estimate` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds, and every test passes, including the new `WoR.AI.DamageEstimate` spec.
- The spec builds a real hit the executor's way and applies it to a defender with some defense. The AI's estimate for the same spell, and for an infused ability, equals the HP that hit takes off (with the AI's usual allowance for crits).
- On the old code the spec failed by one point on each (29 vs 28, 32 vs 31).

**What changed:**
- **Real hits: nothing.** Spells and abilities now get their pre-defense damage and element from one shared calculation (`PreviewActionDamage`). The numbers are the same.
- **The AI:** its damage guess now uses that same calculation. Before, it applied the tier and charge multipliers after defense instead of before, so it was off by a point or so. It also ignored the infusion source it was weighing. AI choices can shift slightly where two options were nearly tied.
- The AI now also knows the real element of a hit (a Generic spell takes the caster's element). Today that doesn't change any damage number, because elements are neutral in the damage calculator.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a test fight against an AI enemy that has spells or abilities.
