# PIE — B-575c: the chest off (D-250 part 3)

**Branch:** `feature/B-575c-chest-off`.
**Crown's words (2026-11-25):** "the chest since theres no limit to our items".
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.InventoryScreen` (two tabs, Give only on gear and stacks, the old screen's verb menu, chest rows hidden from the hub placeholder) and the full suite, headless.
