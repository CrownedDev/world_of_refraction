# PIE — B-174: support skills build no status

**Branch:** `fix/B-174-support-no-buildup` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Skills.SupportNoBuildup` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - Restoration and Enhancement spells count as support and bring no buildup; a Destruction spell keeps its buildup
  - an ability with no damage and a heal effect counts as support; a damaging ability (with or without a heal) does not
  - in a real fight, healing an ally leaves its status bar empty, and an attack still fills the foe's bar

**What changed (rules page 03):** heals, buffs and support skills never build status, on anyone. Before, any skill with a status buildup value built it, even a heal on an ally. A skill counts as support when it is a Restoration or Enhancement spell, or has no damage and a buff or heal effect.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a test fight from the trial level with a party that has a healing or buff spell (or ability).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cast a healing (Restoration) spell on an ally | The ally is healed; its status bar does not move | [ ] |
| 2 | Cast a buff (Enhancement) spell on an ally or yourself | The buff applies; no status bar moves | [ ] |
| 3 | Attack or cast a damage spell on an enemy | The enemy's status bar fills as before | [ ] |
