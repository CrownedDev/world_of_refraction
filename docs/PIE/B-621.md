# PIE — B-621: a self or ally buff lands no hit; Self and Ally survive a reload

**Branch:** `fix/B-621-self-buff-targettype` (built 2026-11-25 from dev 4d451b497).
**What it is:** (1) A no-damage ability or spell aimed at yourself or an ally (a buff, a heal) no longer swings at its target: no DEFEND prompt, no damage, no hit reaction; its buff lands. A single-ally buff now lands on the ally you picked (it used to go to the caster). (2) A skill saved at Self or Ally used to reload as Enemy: a config redirect sent the saved value into an old field. The redirect is gone, and a skill re-saved while it was there takes its target back from that old field.
**Affected assets on dev (they all read Enemy before, and read right now):** Pool Generic: Aura, EnhancementAura, Grace, Might, Warding, Cleanse, Mend, Rally, Renewal, Restore (Ally). Fire: HeartOfFire_, Cauterize, PhoenixRebirth, ResilientFlame, Warmth (Self), LifeSpark (Ally). Water: AdaptiveForm, FlowingGrace, GlacialMastery, AquaticRebirth, Cleanse, Hydration, Refresh (Self), HealingSpring, TidalRestoration (Ally). No ability asset on dev; the Rally ability on `feature/B-595h-rally` is the editor worker's.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.FriendlyNoDamageSkill` (7 cases: ability and spell, Self and Ally; with the old redirect put back, the reload and Might checks fail) and the full suite plus validation on the final tree: Tests: 2623 passed, 0 failed; Data validation: 12 error(s), 0 new.
**Setup:** restart Unreal after the merge (the config change is read at start). The QA save (slot 0) in a fight.
