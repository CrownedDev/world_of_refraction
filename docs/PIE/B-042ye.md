# PIE — B-042ye: per-effect reads on GAS

**Branch:** `refactor/B-042ye-gas-effect-reads` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.GAS.EffectReads`: the held-effect list, buff / debuff counts, the DOT tick preview and the AI's choices are the same at UseGAS 1 and 0; a burn GAS ended is left out of the held list before its stored copy goes (Q-042ye).

What changed: with `wor.Effects.UseGAS 1`, the enemy AI's burn checks, the buff / debuff counts and the character panel's effect list read what GAS holds. With the switch off (the default) nothing changes.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. The demo fight. Run each row with `wor.Effects.UseGAS 1`, then `0`.
