# PIE — B-560: the body takes its element or class colour (D-238)

**Branch:** `feature/B-560-spirit-body-tint` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). **Built unseen** (overnight run): nobody has looked at it in PIE yet.

**What it is:** with no spirit worn over it, a body is Male Nobody or Female Nobody in the character's colour:
- A Refractor takes its element's colour, and Broken Darkness its own black.
- A Generic takes its class brown and a Resonator its class silver.
- The colours come from the same table the old element debug colour used (`ClassColors`).
- The colour is the mannequin materials' **Tint**, on every material slot.
- A spirit worn over it shows that spirit's own materials, so the colour is off.

This holds for the player and for every Spirit recruit: in the hub (standing, joined or not), and in fights. It replaces B-558's "its own Blueprint look": a recruit standing in the hub is now its row's starting body in its colour, not the old `M_DebugElement` material.

**Not in this job:** a character that turns Broken Darkness during a fight keeps its colour from before; only an innate Broken Darkness is black. If a recruit's old element debug colour runs before the dressing, its Broken Darkness absorption colour may not show on the body (check 6).

No editor steps.
