# PIE — B-456b: the vendor screen, piece 2 (Buy)

**Branch:** `feature/B-456b-vendor-buy` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.VendorScreen`: the Buy tab lists the vendor's stock with its cost and skips an empty line; a line the wallet can't pay is greyed with the reason; the screen opens on Buy; A puts the focused line in the cart, Left takes it back, X buys the cart (the wallet pays, the weapon is the member's); a refused line says why and stays out of the cart; R / Y changes who it is bought for and the weapon becomes theirs. `WoR.Economy.BuyFor`: asking why a purchase would be refused spends and grants nothing, and agrees with the purchase.
**What changed (D-193, D-181; mockup `hub/vendors/1_shop_buy.png`):** **talking to a vendor opens the new vendor screen**, on its **Buy** tab: a row per thing on the shelf with its tier and price, a panel for the focused thing (what it is, its Power against the weapon the receiver wears, its abilities, the full price), **"Buying for"** (who gets it) and the **cart** with its total and what you have left after. The Combat Master and the Spell Shop open the same screen with Buy alone.
**Decided by default (Q-456b, tell me to change any):** every vendor uses this screen; X buys the cart at once (the cart with its total is the question); Left takes a line back out.
**Left out:** icons; the old shop window is kept in the code until this passes (it only opens if the new screen can't).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, PIE in the hub with a party of two or more and some Gold.
