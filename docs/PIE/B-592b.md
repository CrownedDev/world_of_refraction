# PIE — B-592b: the HP-cost ghost on the caster's HP bar (D-268)

**Branch:** `feature/B-592b-hp-cost-preview`.
**What changed:** in the new battle HUD's target step, the HP the picked infusion would cost shows on the acting character's card:
- A dark chunk at the end of the HP bar's fill, as wide as the cost, pulsing (mockup `refine_final/7`; no stripes yet).
- If the cost would take the character to 0 HP, the chunk is KILL red and covers what is left (mockup 9's idea). No text line.
- It follows the level and the element (source) as they change, and goes when the step closes (Back) or the action commits.
- Only an HP-paying infusion shows one: level II with Raw, Innate on a spell, or an evolution (D-126). Rings and weapon gems pay wear, so no ghost.
- `UActionExecutor::PreviewActionCost` gives the cost from the same code the commit uses (shared, not copied), with no wear, spend or other change.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Infusion.SameElementStack`, the B-592b case: the preview's HP equals the commit's at L0 / L1 / L2 (innate spell) and for Raw L2; a stacked evolution doubles both. A preview leaves the gem's and evolution's durability, HP, EP and the execution context alone. Lethal at exactly current HP.
- `WoR.UI.BattleHUD`, the B-592b case: Raw L2 through the menu keys shows the ghost at the cost's share of max HP; the level wrapping to L0 hides it; lethal turns it KILL red; Back hides it; Enter (commit) hides it. `wor.UI.Print` shows "HP ghost N%" and "(would kill)".

**Not proven there:** the look (colour, pulse, where the chunk sits), real key presses, a pad, a co-op client.

**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`). Play on the QA save folder only, at `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`; repeat 1 to 3 at `SetWindowSize(Width=1920, Height=1080)`.
