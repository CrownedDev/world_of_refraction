# PIE — B-518: no built-in skills on gear (D-224, skills track S1 part 2)

**Branch:** `feature/B-518-no-builtin-skills` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is:** part 2 of the skills track's first stage (`Design/AssetOverhaulPlan.md` §5.1, "no skills on gear"). Part 1 ([B-517](B-517.md)) moved your picks onto the worn entry. Part 2 takes the built-in skills off the gear itself.
- A weapon or ring has no skills until you put one in. Nothing pre-fills its slots.
- The Equipment screen offers only the skills you know.
- A weapon's or ring's price no longer includes skills. The vendor and shop rows list none.
- A "locked" (conjured) weapon or ring is an ordinary one: its slots are empty and you can fill them.
- **Enemies (Q-518, until stage 5):** an enemy's weapon or ring with no picks fills from that enemy's known skills, in order, up to its slots. So fights still have enemy skills.

Together with B-517's sheet, this is the S1 sheet.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Loadout.SkillSlotList`, `WoR.Loadout.SpellSlotList`: the slots start empty and hold only the picks; emptying a slot brings no built-in back; a locked ring takes picks.
- `WoR.Loadout.WeaponSets`: a pair's one list starts empty.
- `WoR.Loadout.EnemyFill`: an empty weapon and ring fill from the known skills up to the slot count, skipping the wrong type, the wrong element and innate spells; a piece with picks keeps them.
- `WoR.UI.LoadoutSkills`, `WoR.UI.LoadoutV2`: the Equipment screen offers only learned skills, the details box has no "Built-in" line, the compare has no built-ins.
- `WoR.Combat.BrokenSources`: a broken ring's picks stay listed greyed; only F-tier ones cast.

**Not proven:** the lower vendor prices (no spec prices gear with skills), the vendor and shop rows, the fill running for real enemies (an asset scan showed DA_Enemy_Darkness and DA_Enemy_Generic fill 5 abilities and DA_Enemy_Resonator 2), and the enemies' skills in a real fight. These are seen only in PIE.

**Known limits:**
- The asset fields (`PresetAbilities`, `bAbilitiesLocked`, `PresetSpells`, `bSpellsLocked`, `DefaultSpells`) are still on the assets, unread. Stage 6 deletes them.
- A party member's authored loadout skills don't play on their own: what plays is the worn entry's picks (the kit's in a trial, yours in the hub).
- The loadout check's "spell over the holder's cap" (B-196) can't fire any more: the one list holds only its slots. It goes in stage 6.

**No editor step.**
