# PIE — B-471: the recruit card's rules, first part (D-179)

**Branch:** `feature/B-471-npc-talk-recruit-cloud` (cloud, UNBUILT; from `origin/dev`, independent of the loadout branches).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Party.RecruitCard`.
**What this part is:** the recruit card's rules with no card on screen yet (B-471b draws it); the step-out goes through a placeholder console command.
**Setup:** build, run the tests. Start a trial with a party of three (you and two NPC companions) and walk up to a recruit you can meet in the trial.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Face the recruit, run `wor.Party.PrintRecruitCard` | Its name and class, "played by NPC", the rule line, "Party full; can step out:" your two companions with their slots (never you) | [ ] |
| 2 | Press the interact key on it | Nothing joins; the hint says the party is full and how to pick who steps out | [ ] |
| 3 | `wor.Party.RecruitInPlaceOf 2` | "joined; slot 2 stepped out": the companion in slot 2 leaves, the recruit joins for this run | [ ] |
| 4 | `wor.Party.RecruitInPlaceOf 0` (with another recruit) | Refused: the leader's slot never steps out | [ ] |
| 5 | Co-op: a friend holds slot 1; face a recruit with a full party | Only the NPC slot is offered; `wor.Party.RecruitInPlaceOf 1` is refused | [ ] |
| 6 | Co-op with every other slot a player | The card says the party is full of players and the offer is refused | [ ] |
| 7 | A party with room | The recruit joins on interact as before | [ ] |

**Not in this part:** the card and the NPC talk box on screen, V's lines as Talk topics, the journal's "Show where" (B-471b).
