# PIE — B-440a: the loadout screen, piece 1 (gear page, read only)

**Branch:** `feature/B-440-loadout-screen` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockups:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/loadout/1_gear_generic.png`, `4_gear_resonator.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.LoadoutScreen` (8 cases): a Generic's two weapons with their sockets and costs and the one in hand; the wield mode in the kind line; no ACTIVE mark with one weapon; a Resonator's adapters after its weapon, on its budget; apparel outside the budget with its empty slots; every crystal kind, weapon kind and tier has a name; the loadout's title; the screen opens with no party, says so, and closes on Back.
**What changed:** the game menu's Loadout page opens a loadout screen. This piece only **shows** the gear: nothing can be changed yet.
**Pieces to come, one at a time after your review:** B-440b change gear, B-440c the socket picker, B-440d the Skills page, B-440e saved loadouts.
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** how it looks, the keys and gamepad, the scrolling with many rows.
**Left out of this piece:** icons and portraits; the Scaling line in the detail panel; the "Empty adapter" row and the A Change / X Unequip hints (they come with B-440b); final art.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor. The editor step of `docs/PIE/B-439.md` (delete the Blueprint's `IA_ToggleMenu` nodes) still applies. Esc ends PIE in the editor: use the gamepad, or `wor.UI.Pause` and Backspace.
