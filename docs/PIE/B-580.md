# PIE — B-580: death takes buffs and debuffs; Sapphire's revival works and counts the Healing Boost

**Branch:** `feature/B-580-death-and-revival`.
**What changed (D-253):**
- A character that dies loses its buffs and debuffs and its status bar empties. Permanent effects from its own gear stay.
- A dead ally can be picked as the target of a Sapphire, and of nothing else. It comes back at 30% of max HP.
- A Healing Boost the ally had when it died raises that number (an F stone: 34.5%). The revived character has no boost.
- Sapphire's death-save (the ward on a living character, 50%) is raised the same way, and then that character's buffs and debuffs go.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.DeathAndRevival` (11 tests) and the full suite, headless. Not proven there: the fight menu's target list, the card redrawn on death, co-op.
