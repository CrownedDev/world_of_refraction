# PIE — B-279b: wear a character's skin (forms part 2)

**Branch:** `feature/B-279b-form-skins` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Character.FormSkins`: a locked form, a fight, or being away from the hub / a rest spot refuses a switch; an unlocked form is worn at a rest spot and taken off; bound gear gives its bonus only in its owner's skin; the worn skin and base body survive save bytes (a locked skin loads as none); the reveal lists every reference piece. Full suite green.

What changed: an unlocked form can be worn as a skin; wearing it turns on its bound gear's bonus; you can pick Manny or Quinn as your own body. A plain placeholder screen (`wor.UI.Forms`) does it until the real one.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. You need one `CharacterForm` asset (a `UCharacterFormData` with a character, a mesh on the same skeleton as the hub pawn) under a scanned folder; none is authored yet. Open the hub level (or any level and use `wor.Forms.AtRestSpot 1`).
