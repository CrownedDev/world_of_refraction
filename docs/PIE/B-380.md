# PIE — B-380: apparel, part 1 (HP and Defence outside the budget)

**Branch:** `feature/B-380-apparel-cloud` (cloud pre-write, built and specs green on the desktop 2026-11-08; in `dev`).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Loadout.Apparel`: only HP and Defence count, no new budget findings, two slots, a crystal-only socket.
**Setup (editor):** create an **Apparel Data** asset (`DA_Apparel_Test`, naming per ContentNaming.md): Base Stat Bonus → Bonus Max HP 5, Bonus Defense 3. Press **Print Apparel** in its Details panel: `HP +5, Defence +3`. Copy its path (right-click → Copy Reference).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the hub or a trial, `wor.Loadout.PrintAll` | Your loadout line with `apparel: none` and the gear HP / Def | [ ] |
| 2 | `wor.Loadout.WearApparel <the path>` | The line now lists the piece (`1 of 2, no budget`), gear HP 5 more and Def 3 more; your max HP rises | [ ] |
| 3 | Run it twice more | The second adds a piece (`2 of 2`); the third says `no free apparel slot (2)` | [ ] |
| 4 | As a Resonator at a full budget, wear apparel | No budget warning (`wor.Loadout.PrintFindings` shows no budget finding; it replaced `wor.Loadout.CompareRules`, B-172) | [ ] |
| 5 | Add Bonus Luck to the asset and Print Apparel | It flags `[has stat lines apparel ignores]`; Luck does not change in play | [ ] |
| 6 | Give the asset an effect (Referenced Effects, e.g. a Defense buff) and wear it; start a fight | The effect applies at the fight's start like a weapon's | [ ] |

**Known:** apparel is not in the owned store, not saved and not on the gear screen yet (B-380b); a crystal can be set on the entry only in code for now (its element's resistance is proven by the spec).
