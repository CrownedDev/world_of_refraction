# PIE — B-168: Guard (D-084 / D-087)

**Branch:** `feature/B-168-guard` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Defense.Guard` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - a Guard takes half, a Perfect Guard less, a Dodge nothing, and a missed press everything
  - a Parry press is stored and resolved as a Guard, and nothing reflects
  - only a Perfect Guard or a Dodge stops an interruptable hit
  - 50% of status gets through a Guard, 30% through a Perfect Guard
  - a Guard fires On Guard (and old On Block); a Perfect Guard also fires On Perfect Guard and the old perfect / parry triggers
  - a reflect effect sends back exactly its share of the damage taken, with no Defense, resistance or crit (the trigger and the reflect are tested separately; check 7 joins them)

**What changed (D-084 / D-087):**
- Block and Parry are now one defense, **Guard**. Normal timing takes 50%; **Perfect** timing takes **nothing** (Q-168, added later).
- A Guard never reflects damage. Reflecting comes from effects such as "on Perfect Guard: reflect".
- The Parry key still works, as a Guard press. To have just Guard and Dodge, remove the Parry key from the combat input mapping in the editor.
- Dodge is unchanged.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a fight against an enemy that attacks. Try with keyboard and with a gamepad.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | When the defense prompt shows, look at it | It reads "DEFEND! Guard / Dodge" | [ ] |
| 2 | Press the Block key (the key bound to the Block input action in the combat mapping) with loose timing | Output Log "Guard pressed"; the result says "Guard!"; you take about half the hit | [ ] |
| 3 | Press it right on the impact | You take **no** damage (Q-168) and no status buildup (Q-168b); no damage number shows; no damage comes back to the attacker | [ ] |
| 4 | Press the Parry key | It acts as a Guard: Output Log "Parry key pressed (a Guard, D-084)" and the same results as checks 2-3 | [ ] |
| 5 | Repeat checks 2-3 with a gamepad | Same results | [ ] |
| 6 | Dodge | You take nothing, as before | [ ] |
| 7 | Optional: give your character gear or a skill with an "On Perfect Guard" Reflect effect, then Perfect Guard | You gain the reflect buff; a later hit that lands sends its share back to the attacker | [ ] |
