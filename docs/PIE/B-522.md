# PIE — B-522: the Generic starts from StartingGear (D-223, asset overhaul stage 4)

**Branch:** `feature/B-522-starting-gear` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is:** stage 4 of `Design/AssetOverhaulPlan.md` (§1, §3 step 1). A character's inventory asset gains a **Starting Gear** section:
- `StartingGear`: a list of pieces, the same shape as a class kit (`Piece`, `OffHand`, `SkillPicks`, and the new **`Crystal`**: the gem minted into its socket), plus `ActiveIndex` and `Apparel`.
- `StartingEvolution`: the character's own evolution, put in its seat.
- The class kit also gains **`StartingSkills`** (field only; stage S3 reads it).

On a **new game or a new recruit** (no worn record yet), the starting gear is minted into the store once, owned by the character, each `Crystal` socketed at full durability and the evolution owned, and the character wears it. After that the record is the character's and is saved; nothing is minted again. With no `StartingGear`, the old seed runs as before.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Loadout.StartingGear` (the pieces and their crystals, the empty place, the piece in hand, the evolution in the seat at its tier, owned by the character, not kit pieces, the innate list kept). Full suite 2255 passed, 0 failed; validation 16 known, 0 new.

**Not proven:** the new-game flow in PIE (the draw, the Equipment screen, the save and reload).

**Known limits:**
- The innate spells and Broken Darkness pools still come from the old authored loadout until stage 6 moves them.
- A piece's `Crystal` is read only for starting gear, not for the trial kit (a kit reset each trial would mint a crystal from nowhere).
- The new-game draw itself (minted once, drawn, worn) has no spec; checks 1 and 5 cover it.
- Don't list the starting evolution again in `Evolution Equipment`: it would be owned twice.
- An existing save already has a worn record, so it keeps its gear: check on a **new game**.
