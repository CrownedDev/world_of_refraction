# PIE — B-064b: element resistance cuts elemental DoT ticks

**Branch:** `feature/B-064b-dot-element-resistance` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Damage.Element` (now 8 tests). The new test checks that, on a Fire Caster:
- A Fire DoT ticks less.
- A Water DoT ticks more.
- A non-elemental DoT is unchanged.
- A share-of-max-HP DoT (the status-bar cap DoT) is unchanged.

Full suite: 621 green.
**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, start a trial fight with an enemy Fire Caster (+15% Fire, −15% Water).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Put a Fire DoT (Burn) on the Fire Caster | Each tick (`took N DOT damage` in the Output Log) is about 15% below the DoT's value | [ ] |
| 2 | Put a Water DoT (Chill) on it | Ticks about 15% above the value | [ ] |
| 3 | Make it bleed (a physical DoT) | Ticks at the full value, as before | [ ] |
| 4 | Trigger a Reality Burst on any enemy | 25% of its max HP, as before (not resisted) | [ ] |
| 5 | Fill a Fire status bar on the Fire Caster until it caps | The cap DoT ticks 8% of its max HP, not cut by its Fire resistance | [ ] |

**Notes:**
- Q-064 (approved by default): DoT ticks are resisted like hits; Reality Burst and reflected damage are not.
- The AI's "will this DoT kill me / finish them" checks now use the same resisted tick.
