# PIE — B-035: combat-end crystal destruction, flattened

**Branch:** `refactor/B-035-flatten-crystal-destruction` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Durability.CrystalBreak` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - a broken weapon crystal and a broken ring crystal are cleared and each pays its typed essence
  - a broken fusion pays half its gem half's essence and nothing for the stone half
  - intact crystals are left alone
  - a character with no loadout is skipped
- Not covered by a test: a broken **primary evolution** (e.g. Broken Darkness), which needs an evolution asset. Check 3 covers it.

**What changed:** nothing you should see. The end-of-fight code that removes broken crystals moved into its own small file (`CrystalBreak`), and is easier to read now. Log lines are the same.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log. You need a character whose crystal is close to breaking (low durability) and a fight you can win.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Break a weapon or ring crystal during a fight, then win | Output Log: "[CombatOrchestrator] Broken crystal '...' on ... dismantled -> N ... essence", "Destroyed broken crystal ... from Weapon/Ring ...", and "Combat end: destroyed 1 broken crystal(s)"; the socket is empty afterwards and the essence is in your wallet | [ ] |
| 2 | Win a fight where no crystal broke | No "destroyed" lines; crystals stay | [ ] |
| 3 | Optional: with a Broken Darkness character, let the primary evolution break, then win | Output Log: "Broke + dismantled primary evolution on ..."; as before B-035 | [ ] |
