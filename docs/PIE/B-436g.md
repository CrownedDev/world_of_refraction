# PIE — B-436g: the command menu (one frame, Act tab, corner hints)

**Branch:** `feature/B-436g-command-menu`.
**What changed (D-258, D-260, Q-436g; pictures `Design/screen_mockups/combat/hud/refine_final/11_menu_frame.png` and `12_menu_frame_submenus.png`, whose names, costs and counts are examples):**
- The command menu is one frame exactly the full party card's width, sitting just above that card (a small gap, whatever the card's height). No "Your turn" title.
- An "Act: ●●○" tab sits on the frame's top edge on every screen of the menu (D-259's pips, ◆ above Max, the number past 8, "· Skills 1/3" once a skill is used). The pips are no longer on the card. Inspect (click the card) still has the full actions line.
- The main menu is three big rows: Attack, Skills, Items, each with the key of the device in use (keyboard 1 / 2 / 3, pad A / X / Y as coloured rings). A row with nothing to pick stays, greyed, with its reason under the name.
- Skills goes straight into the list with one kind of skill, else to the kinds by name only (no counts). Past the main menu a header says where you are ("Skills > Abilities", "Items", "Attack > choose a target").
- A skill's or item's cost is pips at the row's right; an item keeps its "x2"; a greyed row says why ("Needs 3 actions.").
- Target lists use the same frame; the infusion rows ("Infusion: ... Lv 1", "Source") are small clickable rows at the foot, with their keys.
- Bottom right, right-aligned: Switch weapon, Change ring (when the character has them), Step Forward, Step Back, End Turn, Flee, Defend, each clickable; past the main menu only Back.
- Keys: B / Backspace (or Esc) is Back everywhere past the main menu; RB / G is Defend on the main menu only; on a target screen RB is the infusion level and never Defends. Items moved to 3 / Y. Rows in `Design/InputMap.md`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.BattleHUD`, `WoR.UI.CommandMenu.Keys` and the full suite, headless: the rows and hints of each view, the tab's text, Back one step at a time, RB on a target screen never defending (both orders), the kinds with no counts, the key table. Not proven there: the look against the pictures, real clicks and hovers, the gamepad's glyphs switching live, the layout at two window sizes.
**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`, "How an agent plays"). Play on the QA save folder only.
**Setup:** a party of two or more; the played character with at least one equipped skill (and, for check 4, two kinds of skill, e.g. a Caster with innate spells and a weapon ability) and at least one item in the fight's item slots. `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)` first; checks 1 and 3 are done again at `SetWindowSize(Width=1920, Height=1080)`. `RunConsoleCommand(Command="DisableAllScreenMessages")` before each screenshot. `wor.UI.Print` prints the menu: `tab=`, `header=`, each row (a corner hint marked `hint`), costs and counts.
**To know:** the frame is as wide as today's card, so it is narrower than in the pictures until the card's own piece (B-436b) sizes it; that is expected (Q-436g 4). The rows have no keyboard or pad cursor yet (Q-391): pick by click, or the category keys on the main menu.
