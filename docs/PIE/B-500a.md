# PIE — B-500a: the battle transition opens slowly and finishes fast

**Branch:** `feature/B-500a-transition-slow-open-fast-finish` (in dev, built on the desktop 2026-11-14).
**What it is (Crown's PIE of B-500):** "starts slow, then speeds up". The cracks now take half a second to spread and the frame takes 0.7 seconds to come apart, easing out of the wall instead of bursting. The shards hold for a beat. Then the blow-away waits a moment and accelerates hard past the camera. The opening still fits inside the 2-second join window, so the whole is about as long as before.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.BattleTransition` (a quarter into the break the shards have moved under a quarter of the way; half the blow-away's time covers under a quarter of its distance; the blow-away never moves a shard backwards).
**Not proven:** how the pace feels. Nothing was seen running from the writer's worktree.

**If it looks the same as before:** `DA_BattleTransition_Default` may hold the old numbers. Open it and check Timing: Crack Seconds 0.5, Break Seconds 0.7, Break Ease 2, Blow Away Seconds 0.4, Blow Away Ease 3 (the yellow reset arrow beside a changed value puts the new default back). `wor.Transition.State` logs the values in use.

- On the battle map the first tenth of a second of the blow-away is under the fading held picture; with the slow start nothing is lost there.
