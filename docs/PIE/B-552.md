# PIE — B-552: a swapped-out member keeps their gear; a recruit found in a run starts fresh (D-239)

**Branch:** `feature/B-552-keep-gear-on-swap` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What changed:**
- A character out of the party, benched in the hub or swapped out mid-run, keeps wearing their pieces; nobody else can take them.
- A recruit picked up inside a run starts on its class kit.
- To hand a piece to a new recruit, take it off **before** the swap. Inside a run, a swapped-out member's gear can't be reached. In the hub, invite them back, take pieces off on the Equipment screen, then dismiss them.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start a trial with a full party. Walk to a recruit in the trial and take it in place of a party member who wears a weapon you can see | The recruit joins wearing its class kit (F-tier pieces), not nothing; the Output Log says "joins the run from scratch on its class kit" | [ ] |
| 2 | Open Equipment for the recruit and look at the weapons it can wear | The swapped-out member's weapon shows as worn by another (refused), not free | [ ] |
| 3 | Back in the hub, invite the swapped-out member again and open Equipment | They still wear what they had; take a piece off, then dismiss them | [ ] |
| 4 | Invite another member and look for that piece | The piece you took off is free to wear | [ ] |
