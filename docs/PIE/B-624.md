# PIE — B-624: a sword ritual keeps its infusion

**Branch:** `feature/B-624-sword-ritual-infusion` (built 2026-11-25 from dev 0c2f5a264).
**What it is:** Crown: "so if i use the skill the ritual cast will be infused then if i turn infusion of when the ritual activates it will auto infuse for the attack" (D-276). A sword ritual is armed now and strikes a few turns later. With an infusion picked, the arming charge glows that element. The strike is infused with it even if you turned infusion off in between. All EP, infusion included, is paid when you arm it; the strike costs nothing (Q-624).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.RitualInfusion` (the arm carries the picked element and pays all the EP; the fire strikes with it at no cost, after an uninfused action in between; a Generic spell ritual arms with its source's element; no infusion stays clear). The full suite plus validation on the final tree are in the hand-off.
**Changed for spell rituals too:** a spell ritual's arming cast now takes the spell's element (it was untinted). Its arming montage's Cast notifies get that element too (colour only; they do no damage at the arm).
