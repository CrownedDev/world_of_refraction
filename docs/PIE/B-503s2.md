# PIE — B-503s2: the wheel's weapon reaches the fight (D-218)

**Branch:** `fix/B-503s2-wheel-weapon-reaches-fight` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** Crown's PIE note on B-503s: the axe picked on the wheel was not in hand when the fight started. Every map rebuilds the pawn's loadout from the pool and the character asset, so the pick was lost. Now the weapon in hand is kept on the party member's record (saved) and put back after each rebuild. The trial and hub pawns are now tied to their party member (before, only fight pawns were).

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Field.WheelWeaponCarry` (3): the fight starts with the axe; a hub pawn gets it once tied to its member; an enemy sharing the asset keeps its sword.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. Co-op guests are host-only (a guest's own copy can show the old weapon, B-230).
