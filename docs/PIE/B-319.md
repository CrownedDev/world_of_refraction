# PIE — B-319: one copy per character, the duplicate choice (D-115)

**Branch:** `feature/B-319-duplicate-choice` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Economy.DuplicateGear`: the weaker copy (lower tier, then quality; a tie keeps the one owned first) is paired with the one kept; converting the weaker primary gives Gold and the kept copy takes its slot; a duplicate adapter dismantles for Gear essence; a piece that is no weaker copy is refused.

What changed: when you receive a weapon or adapter you already own, the Output Log says so and the duplicate waits for your choice. Open the placeholder screen with the console command **`wor.UI.Duplicates`**: it shows the copy you keep and the weaker one with its Gold value; **Convert the weaker copy to Gold** or **Dismantle the weaker copy**. Gold = half the tier's buy price (Q-319). The CommonUI screen replaces this later (D-024).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. A trial or hub where you can loot or buy a weapon you already own.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get a second copy of a weapon you own (loot, or buy it) | Output Log: `received a second <name> - choose Gold or dismantle (wor.UI.Duplicates)` | [ ] |
| 2 | Console: `wor.UI.Duplicates` | A dark panel: Keep: name (tier, quality) / Weaker: name (tier, quality) (N Gold), and the buttons | [ ] |
| 3 | Press **Convert the weaker copy to Gold** | Status line says converted; Gold goes up by N; the panel says no duplicates | [ ] |
| 4 | If the weaker copy was your primary, open the loadout | The kept copy is now your primary (never empty) | [ ] |
| 5 | Repeat 1-2 with an adapter, press **Dismantle the weaker copy** | It goes; Gear essence goes up | [ ] |
| 6 | **Close** | Input back to the game, cursor hidden | [ ] |

Nothing opens the screen on its own yet (only the console command); the CommonUI screen will listen to `OnDuplicateGearReceived`.
