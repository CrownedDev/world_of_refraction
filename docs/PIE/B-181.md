# PIE — B-181: the AI skips greyed spells

**Branch:** `fix/B-181-ai-skips-greyed-spells` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes.
- New `WoR.Combat.BrokenSources` cases: an AI with a broken primary evolution does not pick its Breakthrough spells (and does again once it is intact); it does not pick a broken ring's higher-tier preset; a broken weapon crystal's spells are not offered; with nothing broken it picks from every spell.

**Setup:** an enemy with an evolution in its primary slot that can break in the fight (low durability), and the Output Log open.
