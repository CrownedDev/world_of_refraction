## Piece 1 — layout (built 2026-11-13)

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.BattleHUD` (the featured card is a party card, never an enemy's).
**Setup:** open the editor from this branch's folder, start a fight with **two or more party members** (`wor.UI.NewHUD 1`, the default). Check at 1080p; repeat check 1 at 1440p if you can.
**Only positions change in this piece.** The look, the text sizes, the enemy HP bars and the menu's contents are later pieces.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start the fight and look at the screen on your first turn | Turn order **top right**, enemy cards under it. Your acting character's full card **bottom left**. The command menu **stacked just above that card**, fully on screen. Nothing at the bottom right | [ ] |
| 2 | Look at the other party members | Small cards in a row to the **right of the full card**, along the bottom: name, HP bar and the HP line only | [ ] |
| 3 | Act, and wait for the next party member's turn | That member's card becomes the full one at the bottom left; the one that just acted becomes a small card in the row | [ ] |
| 4 | Watch an enemy's turn | The cards do not move: the last party member that acted stays as the full card | [ ] |
| 5 | Open Skills (or any longer sub-list) and Items | The menu grows **upward** from above the card; it does not cover the card and does not run off the top of the screen | [ ] |
| 6 | Give the acting character several statuses (or use a character with many lines on its card) | The full card and the menu do not overlap. If they do, note how many lines the card had | [ ] |
| 7 | Hover the full card, then a small card | The gear tooltip still shows on both | [ ] |
| 7b | Click an enemy, a menu row and empty ground **next to** the small cards along the bottom | Clicks land as before; the small cards only block the spot they cover | [ ] |
| 8 | Click **Statuses** (top centre) | Every combatant's statuses, including the idle party members whose chips are no longer on their small cards | [ ] |
| 9 | Take and deal a few hits | Damage numbers still float over the characters; HP bars and lines update on the full and the small cards | [ ] |
| 10 | Console: `wor.UI.Print` | Each party card's line ends with `featured (full card)` or `compact`, matching the screen | [ ] |

**Decided by default (say if you want it changed):** during enemy turns the full card stays on the last party member that acted, so the cards don't jump. If that member is knocked out on an enemy turn, their card stays full (dimmed) until another party member acts.

**Known in this piece:** the gap between the menu and the card is a fixed height (`FeaturedCardReserve` in `BattleHUDWidget.cpp`), not measured from the card: check 6 is where it would show.
