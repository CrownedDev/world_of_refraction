# PIE — B-503j: one party card everywhere (D-216)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503j-shared-party-card` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** D-216: the party cards in the field are now the battle screen's party cards (one card class, `FBattleHUDCard` in `UI/Combat/Battle/BattleHUDCard.h`), with a show level. **Hub:** the name only. **Trial:** the HP bar, the build-up bar, "HP x / y" and "EP x / y". **Battle:** everything, as before. The field attack chip sits after the cards in the battle card's look (the same dark box, no cut corner); it still lights up when R / LB cycles it.

**Already proven by tests:** the full suite passes; specs check what each level shows (hub: name only; trial: HP, EP, the build-up bar; battle: everything, an enemy its bars and statuses), that a card builds only its level's parts, the trial card's HP / EP numbers from the carried vitals, and that the hub map is the Hub level and a run the Trial level.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. The battle HUD should look exactly as before.

**Known:** the trial's build-up bar is always empty between fights today: the status bars empty after every fight (SB-18, D-013). The trial shows EP for every member (the battle hides it for a Resonator with nothing to spend EP on; the field has no loadout to ask).
