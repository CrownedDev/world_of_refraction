# PIE — B-091: the party shares one armoury

**Branch:** `feature/B-091-shared-gear-pool` (in `dev`).

**Already proven:**
- The editor builds, and 558 tests pass; data validation shows no new errors.
- `WoR.Pool.SharedGear` (10 cases):
  - a party of three with one sword has exactly one, and every member sees it;
  - a second member whose loadout names it gets no copy: it leaves that loadout and the clash is noted;
  - once its wearer switches to another loadout, another member can wear it;
  - a crystal one member attaches shows on everyone's copy;
  - the next level draws the same sword, without banking it twice;
  - a sold sword leaves every member;
  - who wears what, and whose starting gear is banked, survive a save;
  - a character outside the party (an enemy) keeps its own gear;
  - a preset weapon the authored inventory doesn't list joins the starting gear, once even when two presets name it.
- Not provable headless: a real party spawning into the hub and a fight, the placeholder panel, and a restart. These checks cover them.

**Setup:** the hub with at least two recruitable characters; recruit one or two (as in B-071). Open the console with the tilde key, and keep the Output Log open. **Placeholder screen:** `wor.UI.PartyControl`. `wor.PrintPool` dumps the pool, and `wor.PrintInventory` dumps the played character's inventory.

|---|---|---|---|
