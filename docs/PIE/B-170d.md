# PIE — B-170d: action pips 4, effects that give or take actions

**Branch:** in dev (from `feature/B-170d-action-effects-cloud`; built on the desktop 2026-11-12, full suite 1658 passed, 0 failed).
**Proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.ActionEffects` (Gain / Lose Action now and timed, the "+1 now, −1 next turn" pair, gains before losses, Max Actions buff / debuff and the floor, Emerald's ladder and the enemy skip, Emerald unchanged at switch 0, a waiting loss that empties the bank skips the turn, a delayed gain landing with a loss counts before the check), `WoR.Effects.StatusBreaks` (the Ice, Water and Wind rows with the switch on and off), `WoR.Text.Effects` (the new lines and the Extra Turn name). Re-run unchanged: `WoR.GAS.EffectMirror`, `WoR.GAS.EffectBuilder`, `WoR.Status.BreakTable`, `WoR.Turns.MultiAction`, `WoR.Combat.ActionPips`, `WoR.Turns.Belt`, `WoR.Elements.Ice`.

**With the switch off (default) nothing changes**, except two names: the Extra Action effect is now shown as **Extra Turn** (it still grants a bonus turn), and the four new effect types appear in the effect-type dropdown. With it on, the breaks and Emerald work on actions. There are no pips on screen yet (B-170e): use `wor.Actions.Print`.

**Setup:** type `wor.Turns.ActionPips 1` in the console **before** touching an enemy team (the switch is read when the fight starts).
