# PIE — B-635: the Party screen opens the Profile

**Branch:** `feature/B-635-party-profile` (from `dev` 3e17fa463).
**Decision:** Q-635 (A View profile, X Move start place; Dismiss by the mouse; where a stat comes from: pillar, gear, stone, buffs, a cap, total).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.CharacterSheet` (the Profile's five groups; each sub-stat has a bar 0..1, what it does, and its layers; a resistance below zero is a weakness; a curse has text, no number; a refused page has no groups; the screen with no party: nothing to select, Back closes) and `WoR.UI.PartyScreen` (a seat maps to its Profile member, a member with no data skipped), and the full suite, headless.
**Setup:** the QA save folder only (`-saveddirsuffix=QA`), Play in a new window, a party of two or three in the hub. Open the Party screen (Game menu > Party, or `wor.UI.Party`).
