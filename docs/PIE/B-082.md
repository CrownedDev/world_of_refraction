# PIE — B-082: a ring's fusion stone counts

**Branch:** `feature/B-082-ring-fusion-stone` (in `dev`).

**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Loadout.RingFusionStone` (4 cases) checks:
- a ring with a DefenseStone + Garnet (Fire) fusion gives Defense with no weapon, and still offers its Fire spell;
- another active ring gives none; the damage calculator reads it for the defender;
- a Caster's primary ring counts too.

**What changed:** D-009: the stone half of an elemental fusion on a ring now gives its stat (Defense, Reflex, speed, pools...), added to the weapon's stone. Before, every stone read looked at the weapon only. The fusion's bonus stat counts too (Q-082).

**Setup:**
- A Resonator with two rings: ring A with a DefenseStone + Fire crystal fusion and a Fire spell, ring B plain.
- Output Log open.
