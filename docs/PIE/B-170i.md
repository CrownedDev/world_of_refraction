# PIE — B-170i: the Reflex regain, Crown's rework

**Branch:** `feature/B-170i-reflex-regain` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.ActionPips` (the formula and its edges, the progress text, a light Reflex build regains 2 and a focused one 3, the same points give the same regain at Body level 0 and 7). Re-run unchanged: `WoR.Combat.ActionEffects`, `WoR.Turns.MultiAction`.
**What changed:** each turn's regain is now 1 + one per whole step of Reflex value (Q-170i). Reflex value = your Reflex points (gear points at their tier worth) × your Body with every external boost. Step = 15 × your natural Body, so levelling Body changes nothing on its own. The defence window is unchanged.
**Setup:** type `wor.Turns.ActionPips 1` in the console before touching an enemy team. There are no pips on screen yet (B-170e): use `wor.Actions.Print`.

**For Crown to confirm or change (Q-170i):** the step is 15 (light ~15 points → 2, focused ~30 → 3). Reflex stones and Reflex buffs widen the window but don't change the regain. The regain stops at 5.
