# PIE — B-445: selling and dismantling are done at the vendors

**Branch:** `feature/B-445-vendor-dismantle-sell` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Shop.VendorServices`: which vendor does which; a sale removes the piece and pays half its tier's buy price in the hub's currency; the Blacksmith dismantles a weapon for Gear essence; the Spiritualist's Dismantle doesn't take gear and the Jeweler doesn't dismantle; dismantling is refused during a run, and so is selling a piece from home; a loadout's primary is refused for both; one of a pouch stack is sold and the rest stays; the Spiritualist dismantles an evolution, and only the Spiritualist. `WoR.Inventory.Verbs`: the inventory lists Give, Store, Take only, and refuses Dismantle with where it went.
**What changed (D-177, D-193):** the inventory's verbs are **Give, Store, Take**. **Sell** and **Dismantle** are done at a vendor. Blacksmith: Sell, Dismantle (gear and crystals). Jeweler: Sell. Spiritualist: Sell, Dismantle (evolutions). Dismantling is hub-only.
**Decided by default (Q-445, you may change it):** a vendor pays **half the item's tier buy price** (the rule the old "Convert to Gold" already used), and any of the three vendors buys anything that can be sold.
**This is a placeholder:** plain buttons, opened from the console. The real vendor screen with its tabs is B-456.
**Not built yet:** selling evolutions.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, PIE in the hub with a party that owns a spare weapon and some crystals.
