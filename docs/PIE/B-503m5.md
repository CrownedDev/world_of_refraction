# PIE — B-503m5: the wheel is a toggle, one round wheel (D-218, Q-503m5)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503m5-wheel-toggle` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** Crown's look notes on the B-503m4 wheel. **Hold RB** (hold R) half a second and the wheel opens; it **stays up** after you let go. **A** or a **click on a slice** picks and closes; **B, Backspace, RB or R** closes with no change (so does the menu key). On keyboard and mouse the **cursor shows**, the slice under it lights, and the mouse wheel or A / D changes that slice's element; the cursor hides on close. While it is up there is no attack, no camera turn and no walking. The middle shows only what the strip will read ("Sword", "Fire Sword"), with ◄ ► when the slice has more than one option. The look is one **round wheel on a dark disc**, the picked slice lit purple, lines between slices, a middle circle. Placeholder look in C++ (no `.uasset`).

**This pass is look only** (Crown's step 2). Say what to change.

**Already proven by tests:** the slice under the cursor (above the middle is the first slice, then clockwise; the middle and past the rim pick nothing); the slices and their options (B-503m4).
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. Whether the cursor shows over the game view and a click reaches the wheel (checks 4, 5); whether the disc's edges look clean.
