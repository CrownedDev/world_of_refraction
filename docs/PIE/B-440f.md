# PIE — B-440f: loadout screen, change apparel

**Branch:** `feature/B-440f-loadout-apparel-cloud` (cloud, UNBUILT: build first).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.LoadoutApparel` (4 cases), once the desktop has run it; `WoR.UI.LoadoutScreen` must stay green.
**Setup:** build, open the hub, switch the player to a loadout of their own (not the character's lore one). Give the player two apparel pieces: `wor.Loadout.WearApparel <ApparelData asset path>` twice with two assets, then take one off (check 3). `wor.UI.LoadoutApparel.Print` lists each member's apparel. Open the loadout screen (`wor.UI.Loadout`).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Light an apparel row with a piece, press X | The piece comes off; the row reads "Empty apparel"; no warning first | [ ] |
| 2 | On that empty row press A | A picker "In place of Empty apparel:" listing the pieces you own and don't wear in this loadout | [ ] |
| 3 | Light a piece, press A | It is worn in that row; the picker closes; HP / Defence in the details match the piece | [ ] |
| 4 | On a worn row press A, pick the other piece | It replaces the row's piece (still two rows of apparel at most) | [ ] |
| 5 | Wear two pieces, then A on a worn row | The picker lists only pieces not already worn | [ ] |
| 6 | Switch to a lore loadout (the character's own), press A or X on an apparel row | Refused: "…keeps its apparel for now: duplicate it to change it." | [ ] |
| 7 | `wor.UI.LoadoutApparel.Print` | Your pieces, the worn ones marked "(worn)" | [ ] |
| 8 | On a character that owns no apparel, A on an empty apparel row | "You own no other apparel to wear here." | [ ] |

**Notes / what went wrong:**
- Checks 2 to 6 are the only test of the live paths (the owner filter, wearing through TryChangeGear, the lore refusal): the spec covers the pure rules.
