# PIE — B-384: one "all damage %" channel (D-167)

**Branch:** `feature/B-384-one-damage-channel-cloud` (on `feature/B-383-damage-scopes-cloud`; written in the cloud, **not built yet**).
**Proven by tests once built:** `WoR.Damage.AllDamageFold` (AllDamageFoldSpec), plus `WoR.Damage.Scope`, `WoR.Damage.Amp`, `WoR.Damage` and `WoR.TrialGate`.

**Balance shift: read this first.** A single damage boost hits exactly as before. **Stacked boosts hit a little lower**, because they now add instead of multiplying:

| Case (base 100, before defence) | Before (multiplied) | After (one sum) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| One +20% buff | ×1.20 → 120 | ×1.20 → 120 | none |
| +20% Damage Up and +20% damage-dealt passive | ×1.44 → 144 | ×1.40 → 140 | −3% |
| An ability with +20% Damage Up, +20% damage dealt and +20% Raw Damage Up | ×1.728 → 173 | ×1.60 → 160 | −7% |
| A Hard enemy (+20% strength) with a +30% buff of its own | ×1.56 → 156 | ×1.50 → 150 | −4% |

If stacked builds now feel weak (or enemies on Hard feel soft), retuning may be needed: Crown's call.

**Setup:** build, open the editor, start a trial. Have a character with **two damage boosts at once**: e.g. a "Damage Up" spell on themselves and a gear passive that raises damage dealt. Optionally set the difficulty to Hard.
