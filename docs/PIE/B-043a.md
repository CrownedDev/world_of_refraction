# PIE — B-043a: GAS stage 4 part 1, skill costs as GameplayEffects

**Branch:** `feature/B-043a-costs-gas-cloud` (cloud, built and merged into `dev`; specs green, reviewed).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.GAS.Costs` (the same EP after a cost, the same HP after a backlash, a lethal backlash kills, on both paths; the switches; free skills; an immunity that blocks the cost GE pays once the old way). Unchanged: `WoR.Infusion.Cost`, `WoR.GAS.Payouts`, `WoR.Balance.Sim.GASParity`.

**Setup:** `wor.Effects.UseGAS 1` and `wor.Costs.UseGAS 1` (both are needed: the cost path is live only with both). Once B-042g2 is in `dev` the effects switch is gone: `wor.Costs.UseGAS 1` alone. `wor.Costs.Print` shows each combatant's path.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start a fight, `wor.Costs.Print` | Every combatant: `costs paid through GAS`, EP / HP equal to the Energy / Health attributes, `a 10 EP cost spec changes Energy by -10` | [ ] |
| 2 | Cast an innate spell that costs EP; note the preview cost | EP drops by exactly the preview; the Output Log (Verbose `LogWoR`) has `[Costs] … paid N EP through GAS` | [ ] |
| 3 | Use an EP-cost ability, then a ring or weapon-crystal spell | The ability costs its preview; the crystal spell costs 0 EP (crystal wear as before) | [ ] |
| 4 | Infuse a skill at L1 (Raw or Innate), then at L2 | EP and HP drop as before this branch; the HP backlash lands after the action | [ ] |
| 5 | Infuse at low HP so the backlash is lethal | The caster dies after the action, as before | [ ] |
| 6 | While Silenced, try a skill that costs more than the spendable EP | Refused, nothing paid | [ ] |
| 7 | Arm a ritual (a skill with an activation delay) | EP (and any backlash) paid at arm; nothing paid at the fire | [ ] |
| 8 | `wor.Costs.UseGAS 0`, repeat 2 and 4 | The same numbers; `wor.Costs.Print` says `the old way` | [ ] |

**Known:** the cost GE carries the number the one cost function computes (Q-043a), so this sheet checks the hand-off, not a new formula. Both switches stay 0 by default until Crown's PIE.
