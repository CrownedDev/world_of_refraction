# PIE — B-622: a weapon buff's look takes the infusion colour

**Branch:** `feature/B-622-weapon-buff-infusion-tint` (built 2026-11-25 from dev 4f7002104).
**What it is:** Crown's answer "Yes, tint only" (D-275). With an infusion on (the one you keep for strikes, B-613), a weapon buff or debuff such as Rally tints its effects with that element. It uses the same colour path as an infused skill: CoreColor and EdgeColor, plus the SoftTofu `User.Color_`. This is only a look. It costs no EP, applies no infusion, and the action stays uninfused. With no infusion on, there is no tint.
**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Presentation.WeaponBuffTint`: a Fire caster's Rally sent with its infusion has a Fire look and is not a Fire action; with none on, it has no tint.
- `WoR.UI.CommandMenu.Keys`: after an infused strike, the menu sends the weapon buff with that source at no level; a strike is unchanged.
- The full suite plus validation on the final tree: Tests: 2638 passed, 0 failed; Data validation: 12 error(s), 0 new.
**Known:** an Ice pick tints as Water (no Ice colour yet). A weapon buff with an activation delay (armed, fired later) loses the tint; none exists today.
**Not proven:** the colour on screen. It needs a weapon buff whose VFX entry has **Element Tinted** on. Rally is on `feature/B-595h-rally` (the editor worker's); its VFX has a `User.Color_` parameter.
**Setup:** Rally merged (or any weapon ability set to Base Damage 0 with a buff, its VFX entry Element Tinted), a fighter with an elemental infusion source (a Caster's Innate, or an adapter), and enough EP.
