# PIE — B-204: a broken secondary weapon's spells are greyed

**Branch:** `fix/B-204-broken-secondary-weapon` (in `dev`).

**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Combat.BrokenSources`, "a broken secondary weapon crystal (B-204)", checks:
- with the primary weapon intact and the secondary's crystal broken, the secondary's spell is refused with a reason and the AI skips it;
- the intact primary's spell stays castable, even when the broken secondary holds the same spell;
- a spell a broken primary holds is greyed even when the intact secondary holds it too (the primary channels it), and one an intact ring holds is castable (Q-204).

**What changed:** D-083 greys out a broken weapon crystal's spells. Only the weapon in hand was checked, so a Generic's broken secondary weapon still lent its spells (to the player and the AI). Now both weapons are checked. The menu only lists the weapon in hand, so the visible change is mostly the AI (it no longer picks those spells and gets refused).

**Setup:**
- A Generic with a weapon in the primary slot and a weapon in the secondary slot, each with a crystal and a spell. The secondary's crystal has low durability, so it breaks in the fight (`wor.BreakCrystal` only breaks the primary weapon's).
- Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fight with the secondary in hand until its crystal breaks, swap to the primary weapon, open your spells, then swap back | With the primary: its spell is castable. With the secondary: its spell is greyed with the broken-crystal reason (as before) | [ ] |
| 2 | Fight an enemy Generic whose secondary weapon crystal breaks | It never tries the secondary's spell; no "[AIDecisionManager]" Warning about a refused action | [ ] |
| 3 | With nothing broken | Both weapons' spells castable as before | [ ] |
