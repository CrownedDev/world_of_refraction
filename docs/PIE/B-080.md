# PIE — B-080: an infused ability deals its source's element

**Branch:** `fix/B-080-skill-damage-type` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.InfusedAbilityElement` (4 tests):
- A Generic infused through a fire weapon crystal deals Fire.
- Uninfused and Raw deal Physical (no element).
- A Caster's Innate source gives its innate element; its crystal source gives the crystal's.
- A Generic's Innate source (it has no element) stays Physical.

Full suite: 625 green.
**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, start a trial fight with a Generic whose weapon holds a fire crystal (Garnet), against an enemy with known Fire resistance.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use an ability uninfused | Physical damage (no element in the damage number / log), as before | [ ] |
| 2 | Use the same ability infused through the weapon crystal | The hit is Fire: fire colour, a Fire status bar fills, damage changed by the enemy's Fire resistance | [ ] |
| 3 | Infuse it with Raw | No element, as in 1 (HP cost paid) | [ ] |
| 4 | With a Caster, infuse an ability through Innate | Its innate element, as before | [ ] |

**Notes:**
- Q-080 (approved by default): every source gives its own element, so a Caster infusing through a crystal now deals the crystal's element, not its own.
- D-007: an ability starts Physical; an infusion gives it the element of the source you pick. Before this, an infused ability took the user's innate element, so a Generic's infused abilities dealt no element.
- "Physical" is still shown as no element plus Slash / Pierce / Impact until the separate damage type lands (D-037).
