# PIE — B-611: a training dummy in the hub

**Branch:** `feature/B-611-training-dummy` (built 2026-11-25 from dev 4f7002104).
**What it is:** a practice fight against one dummy. It never dies, never attacks and passes its turn at once. Nothing is kept: no rewards, wear, item use, Gold or save. The battle menu's Flee row reads **End practice** and brings the party back to where the leader stood (Q-611).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Hub.PracticeFight`. It checks that no one falls, that the dummy passes, that End practice costs no Gold, that casting wears nothing, that a potion keeps its use, and that the fight is refused with no party. The return stand specs (`WoR.Trial.MiniBossRevive`, `WoR.Trial.RestSpot`) and the full suite also pass.
**Not proven by tests:** the trip to the battle level and back, where you stand on return, the prompt, and the label on screen.
