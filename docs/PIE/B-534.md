# PIE — B-534: Restore a locked crystal

**Branch:** `feature/B-534-restore-locked-crystal` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is (D-231, Q-534):**
1. A gem **locked to its prebuilt piece** (Crystal Removable off) that breaks is **kept on the piece, broken**, at the end of the fight. An unlocked gem still breaks and is destroyed, as before (ER-35).
2. Out of a fight, A on that piece's crystal slot on the Equipment screen offers **Restore**. It spends crystals of the gem's own type from your pouch, cheapest first. One crystal of the gem's tier fills it; one tier down gives half; an A gives a third of an S (the merge values). A higher tier fills it and the extra is lost. No money.
3. In a fight, a kept broken gem gives no element and no Amp; its spells stay listed but greyed. Its stone still counts.
4. The paid repair at the Spiritualist is unchanged and also brings it back.
5. Debug: `wor.Crystal.BreakLocked` wears every locked gem you own to 0.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Durability.Restore`: the amounts (one tier down a half, the same tier fills it, an A a third of an S, two F then an A fill a C and the S isn't used); spends and restores a locked broken gem; refuses an unlocked or unbroken one, spending nothing; the screen offers it only on a locked broken crystal slot.
- `WoR.Durability.CrystalBreak`: a broken gem locked to its prebuilt is kept at fight end, no essence paid.

Full suite: 2298 passed, 0 failed.
