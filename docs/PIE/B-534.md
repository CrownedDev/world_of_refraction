# PIE — B-534: Restore, and the word wear

**Branch:** `feature/B-534-restore-locked-crystal` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is (D-231 "Breaking, restoring and the word", Q-534):**
1. A gem **locked to its prebuilt piece** (Crystal Removable off) that reaches 0 in a fight is **kept on the piece, broken**. An unlocked gem that reaches 0 is still destroyed at fight end (ER-35).
2. Out of a fight, **any socketed gem below full**, and a locked one at 0, can be **restored**. On the Equipment screen, the crystal socket's list shows a **Restore** line under the gem saying what it spends. A on it restores. A on the gem itself still takes it out, as before.
3. Restore spends crystals of the gem's own type, cheapest first. One of the gem's tier fills it; one tier down gives half; an A gives a third of an S (the merge values). A higher tier fills it and the extra is lost. No money.
4. In a fight, a kept broken gem gives no element and no Amp; its spells stay listed but greyed. Its stone still counts.
5. Player text says **wear**, not durability: the Wear line in the details boxes, and the Wear Stone. The number still means what is left.
6. The paid repair at the Spiritualist is unchanged.
7. Debug: `wor.Crystal.BreakLocked` wears every locked gem you own to 0.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Durability.Restore` covers:
  - the amounts: one tier down a half, the same tier fills it, an A a third of an S, and two F then an A fill a C without using the S;
  - a locked gem at 0 restored, its crystals spent;
  - an unlocked half-worn gem topped up;
  - an unlocked gem at 0 and a full one refused, nothing spent;
  - the screen offers it on a worn gem, and at 0 only when locked.
- `WoR.Durability.CrystalBreak`: a locked gem at 0 is kept at fight end, no essence paid.
