# PIE — B-043f: GAS stage 4 part 6, the skill ability pays its action's cost

**Branch:** `feature/B-043f-ability-commits-cost`.
**Proven by tests:** `WoR.GAS.Abilities` "costs and whole fights (B-043f)": a projectile spell pays the same EP at `wor.Abilities.UseGAS` 0 and 1, through one ability commit at 1 and none at 0; with no ability running the action the executor pays (nothing taken); an all-AI 1v1 of two fighters with ability systems runs to its end at 0 and 1 on one battle seed with the same rounds and end HP, every ability ending, every action's cost paid through its ability and every impact through an ability. The strike case also counts one commit at 1. `WoR.Balance.Sim.OneFight` unchanged.

**Setup:** `wor.Abilities.UseGAS 1` for checks 2–7 (default 0). `Log LogWoR Verbose` shows `[Abilities] <actor>: <action> commits N EP (paid)`. `wor.Abilities.Print` shows `costs paid via GAS: N` per skill.
