# PIE — B-063: status breaks (what a full bar does, and for how long)

**Branch:** `feature/B-063-status-breaks` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Effects.StatusBreaks` (11 cases) checks:
- the shared turn counter on its own;
- a full bar lasts one of the holder's turns, and a Burn pays out exactly once;
- a Burn landing on the holder's own turn pays on landing;
- Lightning stuns for exactly one turn;
- Water slows Action and Spell Speed;
- Broken Darkness drains EP;
- Wind moves the holder's next turn back one place.

**What changed (D-030, D-090, D-014, D-051):**
- **One turn:** every full bar's effect ("break") lasts **1 of the affected character's own turns**. Before, it was 2 for most bars. It ends as their following turn starts.
- **Burn and Bleed:** hurt once, at the start of the holder's turn. Before, they hurt on landing and again at each turn end.
- **Water:** slows Action Speed and Spell Speed. Before, it blocked all healing.
- **Wind:** the holder's next turn moves **one place later** in the turn order (shown on the strip at once). They still act that round.
- **Broken Darkness hitting with Darkness:** drains 25% of max EP at the holder's turn start. Before, it did nothing.
- **Void:** unchanged for now (lasts 1 turn, no forced action yet; B-199).

**Setup:** a fight where you can fill enemy status bars (Fire, Water, Wind, Lightning). Open the console with the tilde key; Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fill an enemy's **Fire** bar on your turn | No Burn damage yet. At the start of that enemy's next turn, one Burn hit. At the start of its turn after that, the Burn is gone, with no second hit (`wor.PrintEffects` shows it gone) | [ ] |
| 2 | Fill an enemy's **Lightning** bar | The enemy is stunned on its next turn only (Attack/Defend). The turn after, it acts normally | [ ] |
| 3 | Fill an enemy's **Water** bar | That enemy shows Action Speed and Spell Speed slows; healing it still works; both slows end after its next turn | [ ] |
| 4 | Fill an enemy's **Wind** bar | The turn strip at once moves that enemy's next turn one place later; it still acts that round | [ ] |
| 5 | Fill the same enemy's Wind bar again before its turn | It doesn't move back a second time | [ ] |
| 6 | (If you have a Broken Darkness enemy) let it fill your Darkness bar | At your next turn start you lose about a quarter of max EP, once. Also check that this isn't the same drain as its Broken Darkness aura (overflow) | [ ] |
| 7 | Play a full fight as usual | No stuck turns; Execution turns (rituals) still fire | [ ] |
