# PIE — B-514: the quick strip replaces the RB wheel (D-222), look only

**Branch:** `feature/B-514-quick-strip` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** the round wheel was too compressed (Crown). Holding RB now opens a **Breath of the Wild-style quick strip** (mockup `Design/screen_mockups/hub/hud/v2/3_trial_strip_open.png` and `3b_trial_strip_ring.png`):
- A row of icon tiles through the screen's middle, one per piece of gear (each weapon or pair, the adapters or rings, a Refractor's innate, the evolution). The world dims behind; the cards stay.
- The piece in use is tagged **IN HAND**. The highlighted piece is framed twice, a little bigger, with ▲ ▼ when it has more than one source.
- Under the row: the piece's **name** ("Storm Ring"), then quieter its **type · source** ("Ring · Lightning"; "Sword and Shield · Fire"); the type alone when plain (D-222 "The text and the frame"). **One frame** goes around the icons and the text.
- Every rule stays (D-217, D-218): tap RB attacks; the hold opens it and it stays up; A or a click picks; B, Backspace, RB or R closes; the pick is the weapon in hand and the parry.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Field` (the type line: "Sword", "Sword · Fire", "Ring · Lightning"; a ring's tile takes the ring's icon).
**Not proven:** the look and the keys, seen only in PIE. This is the look check: Crown decides the sizes and colours (the numbers are at the top of `HubHUDWidget.cpp`, `HubHUDStyle`, marked TODO).

## Icons: missing from the icon table (for the icon table and the placeholder list)

A tile shows the piece's own **Icon** (its asset's field), else a weapon's type icon (`T_Icon_Weapon_*`), else (the innate, an evolution) the element's badge, else **the piece's name as text in the tile**. The icon table has none of these yet:
- **Adapter forms:** Ring, Pistol, Trainer (the mockup used PONETI's `64_ring.png`).
- **Shield** (an off-hand piece; the mockup used PONETI's `17_Shield.png`). A pair whose shield has no Icon shows only the main hand's icon.
Until they exist (or a ring asset's own Icon is set), a ring or adapter tile shows its name.

**Choices by default (Q-514, Crown may change):** the strip wraps at its ends; ▲ ▼ show only when the piece has more than one source; a resting mouse cursor doesn't move the highlight; up to 9 pieces (more are logged and not shown); IN HAND marks the entry in use (with a ring picked, the ring's tile carries it); the innate's second line is its source alone ("Fire"); an evolution's type reads "Evolution".
