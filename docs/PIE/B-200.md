# PIE — B-200: the AI counts gear and buffs when it judges status buildup

**Branch:** `fix/B-200-ai-live-status-multiplier` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.AI.BuildupEstimate` (3 cases) checks the AI's own estimate: an attacker with a Status Multiplier buff gets an estimate equal to what its real hit adds to a status bar. Gear and StatusStone go through the same live number but have no test of their own, so check 2 covers them.

**What changed:** the AI judged status buildup by the character's innate Status Multiplier only. A real hit also counts gear, a StatusStone and Status Multiplier buffs, and the AI's choice of how far to infuse a spell skipped the multiplier entirely. It now reads the same live number the hit uses. In play, an enemy whose Status Multiplier is buffed or geared goes for "fill the status bar" moves when the bigger number really would fill it.

**Setup:** a fight on Medium difficulty or higher against an enemy that has a skill with status buildup and a way to raise its Status Multiplier (a buff, or gear/StatusStone). Output Log open.
