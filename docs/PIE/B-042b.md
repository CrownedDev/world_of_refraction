# PIE — B-042b: GAS stage 3, part 2 (effect context, family GEs, spec builder)

**Branch:** `feature/B-042b-gas-effect-builder` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.GAS.EffectBuilder` (10 cases) checks:
- every GameplayEffect context is the game's own (it carries who an effect belongs to);
- a Stun, Silence or Fire Immunity built from a current effect gives its tag;
- a GAS Defense buff sums to the same number as the current effect manager;
- re-applying the same effect replaces it, while another source's copy adds;
- a damage over time and a heal over time pay out at each holder turn start, and stop when they end; a heal over time waits while the holder is heal-blocked; a killing tick stops the rest;
- gear effects are marked as gear; a permanent effect isn't turn-counted;
- types not ported yet and instant effects build nothing.

The full suite (649) is green.

What changed:
- The first GAS effect families and a **spec builder** that turns today's effects into GAS effects.
- Nothing in play uses them yet. Effects still run on the current system, so the fight should play exactly as before. The switch-over (with a console switch to flip back) is B-042c.
- `DefaultGame.ini` now names the game's own GAS globals class.
