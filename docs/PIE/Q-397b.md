# PIE — Q-397b: hidden quests masked by default (spoiler tag)

**Branch:** `feature/Q-397b-hidden-quest-mask` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Memory.QuestJournal`: found hidden quests read "Hidden Quest <n>: masked" (n in found order, newest listed first), a Reveal shows that one only, it stays revealed while the journal is open and is masked again after Close; step and finish notices are masked; a finished one reads "Hidden Quest <n>: done". `WoR.Memory.HiddenQuest`: the discovery notice is "Hidden quest found. Open the journal to view it." and never names the quest.
**Supersedes:** PIE B-397 checks 4, 6 and 7's notice wording and 5's row (the quest is masked until revealed).
**Setup (editor):** B-397's **HQ-Test** (Title "Test hidden quest", goals "Win a fight" / "Win again") and **HQ-Blank**.
