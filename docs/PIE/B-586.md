# PIE — B-586: the Intel panel (D-261, Q-586)

**Branch:** `feature/B-586-intel-panel`.
**What changed (D-261, Q-586; the picture is `Design/screen_mockups/combat/hud/refine_final/13_intel.png`, its numbers are examples):**
- B-581's inspect is now **Intel**: one centred panel with one row of tabs, the party first then the enemies. Your own character's tab reads **You**; its heading keeps the name. Another party member's tab keeps its name.
- A party tab: heading, Spirit of … / Body of …, the full actions line (pips, number, Max, progress: D-259 kept it in inspect), HP / EP / build-up bars, **Pillars** (Mind, Body, Spirit), **Stats** (the sheet's eleven; one an effect changed reads "before → now", green better, red worse), **Effects** (name, amount, turns left).
- An enemy tab: heading, "? means not learned yet", HP bar with no number (Q-391), build-up bar, **Weak to** and **Resists** by element ("?" lines until a hit of that element teaches it), **Effects**.
- A hit teaches its element's line for that enemy type for the rest of the run (another fight with the same enemy type shows it too); a new run starts blank.
- The player's cards no longer show status names; the enemy cards keep theirs.
- Keys: **V / R3** open (on the featured party card's tab) and close; a **click on any card** opens on that card's tab (the same card again closes); **Q / E, LB / RB** step the tabs while open (wrapping), a click on a tab picks it; **Back** (Backspace / Esc / B) closes it. While Intel is open no combat menu key acts (LB / RB don't Defend or change infusion). The Status button still shows everyone and closes Intel.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.Intel` (before → now with and without effects, Resistance's own "before"; an amount for a buff, a debuff, damage over time, Stun, healing over time and a lasting effect; one line learned per hit type, not by a heal or a hit on the party, kept for the next fight of the run, not shared with another enemy type; the "You" tab; open / step / close and never both panels), `WoR.UI.BattleHUD` (Spirit / Body and the actions line now in Intel; the party card has no status line). Not proven there: the look, real keys and pad, the click hit-boxes, a real trial fight's learning.
