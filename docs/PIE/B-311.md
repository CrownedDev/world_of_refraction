# PIE — B-311: player-made loadouts are saved (D-111)

**Branch:** `feature/B-311-custom-loadouts-saved` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Loadout.CustomLoadouts`: a copy of a lore loadout is custom and the lore one can't be deleted; 5 custom loadouts and no sixth; a custom loadout survives the actor's end and the next spawn with the same sword (by GUID); a default seed doesn't replace custom loadouts; save v6 keeps them (Broken Darkness pools too). Full suite green.

What changed: loadouts you make yourself are kept per character (up to 5) and saved; the character's own (lore) loadouts are always rebuilt from its asset.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. A level with the party and the loadout screen / loadout debug.
