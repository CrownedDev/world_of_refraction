# PIE — B-392: two guards (weapon / unarmed)

**Branch:** `feature/B-392-two-guards` (the cloud's `feature/B-392-two-guards-cloud` on dev; built on the desktop 2026-11-12; merged into `dev`). **Save v32:** loading an older save wipes it with a warning (D-060, D-138).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Save` (the guard and its put-away weapon survive save / load); `WoR.Defense.TwoGuards`: no weapon guards unarmed (the character's `BlockMontage`) and refuses Unarmed; a weapon defaults to its guard (`ParryMontage`) and the setting puts it away and back; equipping another weapon brings the weapon guard back; a weapon with no `ParryMontage` plays the unarmed guard; the defense window is the same in both guards.
**Setup:** a fight with a weapon user (Generic) and a Refractor or Resonator with no weapon. For a visible difference, give the weapon's data asset a `ParryMontage` (e.g. SAS V2 `Block`, AssetAudit) and the character's Cosmetics a `BlockMontage` (e.g. DHTH2 `Block1`).
