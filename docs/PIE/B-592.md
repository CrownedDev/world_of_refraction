# PIE — B-592: a compact target panel with X / Y toggles (D-268)

**Branch:** `feature/B-592-target-panel-xy`.
**What changed:** in the new battle HUD the target step (Attack, a skill, an item) starts small:
- The heading ("Attack · choose a target") and one dim summary line ("Sword · No infusion", "Hammer · Lightning · Lv I"), then the target rows.
- **2 / X** shows only the weapon chips (Attack with two weapons). Pressing it again switches to the next weapon, and you stay in the step.
- **3 / Y** shows the element and level chips instead. R / T (LB / RB) change them as before, and a click on a chip picks it. Pressing it again closes them.
- A spell of one fixed element shows the level chips only, and its summary is the level alone ("Lv II").
- Back closes an open line first. There is no "Size x…" or backlash line. The HP-cost ghost is a later job (B-592b).

**Mockups:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/combat/hud/refine_v4/` (1 to 5, `sheet.png`). The target rows stay under the panel, which the mockups leave out (brain, 2026-11-23).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.CommandMenu.Keys`, both B-592 specs:
- The Attack step opens small: summary "Sword · No infusion", hints "2 Weapon" and "3 Infusion", no Level hint.
- 2 shows the two weapons (not Skills); 2 again puts the Hammer in hand with the line open.
- 3 shows three levels (not Items); T steps the level chip; 3 again closes the line.
- Back closes an open line first. No size or backlash text.
- A spell has no Weapon hint and an "Infusion level" hint; its level chip click works; it has no element line, and its summary is "Lv II".

**Not proven there:** the look against the mockups, real key presses, a pad, an item step, a co-op client's weapon switch.

**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`). Play on the QA save folder only, at `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`; repeat 1 to 3 at `SetWindowSize(Width=1920, Height=1080)`.
