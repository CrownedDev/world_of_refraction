# PIE — B-224: combat numbers baseline

**Branch:** `feature/B-224-combat-numbers` (written unbuilt in a cloud session; the Unreal writer builds, tests and merges).
**To be proven by tests (once built):**
- `WoR.Tuning.Baseline`: pillar curve 0..7, reference Power and requirement by tier, enemy HP by rank, Elite order and flee, requirement floor 0.5 on the weapon part, Defense cap 75%, crit base x1.25, tuning defaults and filled rows, the print.
- `WoR.Tuning.CombatData`, `WoR.Weapon.Power`, `WoR.Weapon.RequirementFactor`, `WoR.Balance.Sim.Runner`, `WoR.Damage`, `WoR.StatSources` still green (they read the constants, so the new numbers flow through them).

What changed for play:
- **Enemies by rank:** a mini-boss has x3 max HP, a boss x6 (on top of trial difficulty and zone tier). Normal fights are unchanged. Elite (x1.5) exists as a rank but no encounter is authored Elite yet.
- **Defense** now caps at 75% with all gear and buffs (was 85%).
- **Crits** do +25% with no crit-damage investment (was +0%); the stat still reaches x1.5, gear x2.5.
- **Weapon requirement:** being far under a weapon's requirement now loses at most half the weapon part (was up to 68%); still nothing until weapons have Power.

**Setup:** pull the branch, build, run the tests. Open the demo fight.
