# PIE — B-564: a co-op guest keeps the gear and skills it earns (Q-561, Crown 2026-11-17)

**Branch:** `feature/B-564-guest-keeps-gear-skills` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). Not seen in PIE yet.

**What it is:** after the host finishes a run with a guest (boss win or extract), the guest's own save for that character also gets:
- **Found gear and evolutions it kept** (D-219, D-221). These are the pieces the run let its member keep as hub gear: owned by its character, with their crystals and rolls. They are **owned, not worn**: the guest puts them on in its own game.
- **Skills it learned** there (D-224), added to its character's learned skills, with no duplicates.
- Its starting kit on the host is not copied (its own save already has its own).
- When the character joined is the one loaded on the guest's side, the guest's pool in memory is reloaded with the pieces, so they are not lost at its next save.

**What only the spec proves (no second client):** the pool key, moving only the guest's kept found gear (never the host's, never the starting kit), skills under its own key, and a second copy adding nothing. **Unproven** until a two-player PIE: the copy reaching the guest and the reload on its side.

**Default chosen (Crown may change):** kept gear arrives owned, not worn. D-219 says what you finish wearing is what you wear in the hub; the guest's own worn record still points at its own pieces, so it is left alone.

Editor steps: none.
