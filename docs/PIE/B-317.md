# PIE — B-317: Store 6, delete the old copies (first part: the pouch copy and the hand-back)

**Branch:** `chore/B-317-store-old-copies` (in dev; the cloud's pre-write `feature/B-317-store-old-copies-delete-cloud` replayed, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-14). A deletion under D-204 (Crown waived the PIE gate of the store jobs).
**Already proven by tests:** the build compiles without the deleted code, and the specs named below stay green.

**What this job deletes:**
- `UCrystalInventoryComponent::HandlePoolItemsChanged` (a private `UFUNCTION()`, a delegate target only) and its bind to `UPoolSubsystem::OnPoolItemsChanged`. A party member's crystal inventory no longer keeps a copy of its pouch in its `Crystals` / `Stones` maps; they are empty while it is bound. The two `UPROPERTY` maps stay: enemies and test actors keep their own counts there. No saved or serialized field is removed.
- `ULoadoutComponent::BattleItemsTaken` (plain C++, not a `UPROPERTY`) and `GetBattleItemsTaken` (not a `UFUNCTION`), with `ResetBattleState`'s hand-back loop. It could no longer be filled: since B-313 a member with a pouch never auto-equips. `ResetBattleState` stays Blueprint-callable with the same effect.

**Not in this job (now B-317c):** the pool's wearers and the per-member gear copies and sync (still read, and saved), and the enemies' own item list off `ItemSlots` (an editor move, check 6), after which `ItemSlots` / `UseItem` / `ApplyAutoEquip` go. The co-op move of the store is B-317b.
