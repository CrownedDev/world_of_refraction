# PIE — B-155: utility crystal tier caps

**Branch:** `feature/B-155-utility-crystal-caps` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including three new cases in `WoR.Loadout.Rules`.
- They check that:
  - each tier band at its cap passes
  - one over a band is reported; a stack counts each crystal; A and B (and C/D, E/F) count together
  - utility stones and crystals attached to gear don't count

**What changed:**
- A character's item slots may carry at most **S 1, A+B 2, C+D 3, E+F 4** utility crystals (D-072, D-077). Going over gives the message "Too many S-tier utility crystals: 2 (max 1)" (and so on per band).
- It is reported, not removed yet: the editor check and the pre-fight error list show it, and the fight still starts with the crystals. Dropping the excess on load is B-159.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev` and open the Output Log.
