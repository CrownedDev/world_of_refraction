# PIE — D-221 stage 1c (B-511, B-512): every trial starts on the class kit

**Branches:** `feature/B-511-class-default-kit`, `feature/B-512-trial-starts-on-kit` (both in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** a trial always starts on your class's default kit, so every run has the same starting point (D-221):
- At the trial's start each member wears their class kit. The kit is reset every time: its tier, its sockets (a crystal in one goes back to your pouch or storage) and its skills.
- Your character's own evolution and innate spells stay; they aren't part of the kit.
- The gear you wore in the hub stays yours, at home. A quit puts it back on; an extract or a boss win leaves you in the kit (D-219).
- Kit pieces can't be sold, dismantled, given or put in the chest.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Pool.ClassDefaultKit` (4): each character's kit is made once as their own; a changed kit replaces its piece; give and chest refused; saved.
- `WoR.Trial.ClassDefaults` (4): the kit is worn whatever the hub wore, home gear stays owned at home; reset each start (tier, the socket's crystal back and not counted as a find, skills); own evolution and innate spells stay; no kit, or a resumed run, keeps the worn gear.

**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. The specs have no live pawns: check 4 is the one that matters (the hub's characters must not write their old gear back over the kit while the trial loads). Co-op guests are host-only (B-230).

## Editor step first (for the editor worker)

Until this asset exists, a trial keeps what is worn and logs `[TrialRun] No class default kits ...` once per member (check 1). Nothing breaks.

Create **`/Game/Data/Loadout/DA_Loadout_ClassDefaults`**, class `ClassDefaultLoadoutData` (the path `DefaultGame.ini` already names). In **Defaults**, three rows. A place is an entry in **Pieces** (0 the primary, 1 a Generic's second weapon, 2 and up the adapters); leave a place's **Piece** empty to skip it. **Skill Picks** empty (the weapon's built-ins), **Off Hand** empty, **Apparel** empty, **Active Index** 0.

- Save the asset, then in PIE run `wor.Trial.PrintClassDefaults`: it lists the three classes and their pieces.

**The debug lines:**
- `wor.Trial.PrintClassDefaults`: the asset's kits, then each character's kit pieces; a piece worn now says `(worn now)`.
- `wor.Loadout.PrintWorn`: what each member wears.
- `wor.Run.PrintKept`: the open run, with the run-start copy (the hub's gear).
- Log lines: `[ClassDefaults] <character>: kit reset ...` and `[ClassDefaults] <character> wears its class kit` at each trial start.
- Shortcuts: `wor.Trial.EndRun win|extract|wipes|quit`, `wor.Trial.GrantFoundWeapon`.
