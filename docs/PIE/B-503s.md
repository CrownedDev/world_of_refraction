# PIE — B-503s (part 1): an element-source pick puts the weapon away (D-218, Q-503s)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503s-wheel-hides-weapon` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** the first part of D-218. On the field wheel, picking an **element source** (the innate, an evolution, a caster's ring: anything that throws the pulse) **puts the weapon away**; picking a **weapon** slice takes it out again. The pick is kept, so a map entered with a source picked starts with the weapon away. The fight is not touched (its own level and pawns). Still to come after Q-503s: a second weapon's slice showing that weapon, a Generic's ring, and the parry following the pick.

**Already proven by tests:** a pulse pick sets the weapon away, a weapon pick takes it out.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE; whether the field pawn's weapon is shown by `UWeaponMeshComponent` (check 1). Other co-op players still see the weapon (the owner and host only).
