# PIE — B-503s: the wheel's pick in hand and in the parry (D-218, Q-503s)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503s-wheel-hides-weapon` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** the first part of D-218. On the field wheel, picking an **element source** (the innate, an evolution, a caster's ring: anything that throws the pulse) **puts the weapon away**; picking a **weapon** slice takes it out again. The pick is kept, so a map entered with a source picked starts with the weapon away. The fight is not touched (its own level and pawns). Still to come after Q-503s: a second weapon's slice showing that weapon, a Generic's ring, and the parry following the pick.

**Already proven by tests:** a pulse pick sets the weapon away, a weapon pick takes it out.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE; whether the field pawn's weapon is shown by `UWeaponMeshComponent` (check 1). Other co-op players still see the weapon (the owner and host only).

## Editor steps

None. Pull dev on the main checkout and build with the editor closed.

## Part 2 (`feature/B-503s-wheel-pick-active-parry`): the weapon in hand, a Generic's adapters, the parry

**Already proven by tests:** a weapon slice's pick makes that weapon ACTIVE in the saved loadout; a Generic's Pistol adapter and evolution throw the pulse, a ring infuses; the parry inputs from a swing pick (plain: spark only; infused: that element), a pulse pick (unarmed, its element, Ice in Ice's colour, Dark Fire as Fire), and the fallback for a member never picked.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. A co-op guest's own pawn may not show the swapped weapon until its next loadout sync (host only, shortcut).

Setup: `wor.Debug.TestGear`, then give the Generic a second weapon (an axe) as the secondary entry in `wor.UI.LoadoutV2`.
