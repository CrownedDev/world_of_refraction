# PIE — B-042v: GAS stage 3, part 5l (turn speed on GAS)

**Branch:** `feature/B-042v-gas-turn-speed` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.GAS.TurnSpeedSources` (5 cases) checks:
- turn speed on GAS equals the speed turn order uses, with one sources effect;
- a turn-speed buff, alone or with a turn-speed stone, comes through on GAS as a whole number, without re-applying anything;
- a debuff past -100% floors turn speed at 1 on both paths;
- in a fight it follows the speed turn order cached for the combatant.

The full suite (720) is green, including the turn order specs.

What changed:
- Turn order and GAS now read one turn-speed getter. Turn order's numbers are unchanged.
- On GAS turn speed is built from the same parts.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, start the demo fight.
