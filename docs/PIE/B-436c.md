# PIE — B-436c: enemy HP on the target only (piece 3a, D-263)

**Branch:** `feature/B-436c-target-hp`.
**What changed (D-263 point 3, `Design/BattleHUDRefinePlan.md` piece 3a):**
- The always-on enemy cards under the turn order are gone; the top right holds only the turn order (and Intel under it, B-587).
- An enemy's card is now the **target marker**: it shows only while the target picker highlights that enemy, centred above its head, and follows the highlight (left / right, hover). Its name, HP bar (no number, Q-391), status build-up bar, status chips, then **"Tier C"** centred at the bottom.
- No enemy card in the command state, after Back, after the action, or under Intel. Clicking the marker still opens Intel on that enemy.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.BattleHUD` "shows an enemy's card only as the marker over the highlighted target" (two enemies: none shown on the main menu; Attack shows the highlighted one only; cycling moves it; Back hides it; the tier line, no HP number, no Enemies column) and `WoR.UI.Intel` (the marker layer hides under Intel). Not proven there: the marker's place over the real arena's heads, at both window sizes.

**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`). Play on the QA save folder only. Run every check at `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`, then repeat 2 and 3 at `SetWindowSize(Width=1920, Height=1080)`. `wor.UI.Print` prints `target marker (follows the picker): <name> (Tier X)` or `none`.
