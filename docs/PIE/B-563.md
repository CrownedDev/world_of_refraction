# PIE — B-563: a co-op guest keeps what it earns (Q-561, Crown 2026-11-17)

**Branch:** `feature/B-563-guest-keeps-earnings` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). Not seen in PIE yet.

**What it is:** a guest in a friend's game keeps what its character earns there, by the solo rules.
- **Currencies:** what its wallet gained there (Prisms, essences, Diamond, Mentor Tokens) is added to its own character's save. Spending there comes off it. Gold stays run-only.
- **Kept levels:** the levels kept from the host's last finished run (a boss win or an extract) replace the character's last kept levels, as in the guest's own game.
- **Leaving mid-run, or the host quitting mid-run,** counts as a quit: only the wallet's change is kept.
- **A new character made to join** is saved to a free slot of the guest's own when the join is sent. With every slot full, Begin says so.
- **Gear and skills:** not yet (B-564).

**What only the spec proves (no second client):** the wallet sum, finding the guest's member in the host's save, the kept levels, the new character's save. **Unproven** until a two-player PIE: the host's save copy arriving at the guest and the guest's slot being written.

Editor steps: none.
