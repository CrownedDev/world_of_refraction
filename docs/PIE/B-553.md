# PIE — B-553: the mouse on the remaining screens (D-233)

**Branch:** `feature/B-553-mouse-remaining-screens` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17).

**What changed:** the screens that didn't take the mouse yet now do, through the same code the keys use:
- the Equipment screen's wheel;
- the Recruit card and the Join request card (hover, click);
- hover on the Character sheet, the Gate (plus a click on a gate tab) and Play together.

Credits, the Rest spot and the battle HUD were checked and needed nothing (`InputMap.md`, screens table).
