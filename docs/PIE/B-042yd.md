# PIE — B-042yd: the other payouts on GAS

**Branch:** `refactor/B-042yd-gas-payouts` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.GAS.OtherPayouts`: energy restore / drain, HP drain, self-damage and percent restores give the same numbers at UseGAS 1 and 0; a HealBlocked percent restore heals nothing on both.

What changed: with `wor.Effects.UseGAS 1`, these payouts go through GAS (Q-042yd: one-shots pay through their own tick spec). With the switch off (the default) nothing changes.
