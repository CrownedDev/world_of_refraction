# PIE — B-503f: the battle transition back to its first timing, and a wall that fills the screen

**Branch:** `fix/B-503f-transition-timing-wall` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:**
1. **Timing** (Crown: "how it was in the beginning was fine, let's just remove the slow aspect for now"). B-500's first timings, with no slow phase and no freeze beat. These are values in the data asset (editor step 2 below); no code change.
2. **The wall fills the screen.** Crown's screenshot showed live world on every side of the shards. The wall was placed as if the camera's field of view were always the horizontal one. A camera that keeps its vertical field of view makes the real view wider, so the wall came out too small on both axes. The wall is now placed from the screen's real projection on both axes, every frame, for any aspect and FOV, the editor's odd viewport sizes included. The frozen picture is taken with the screen's real horizontal angle, so it lines up with the world behind it. The shards still reach 4% past each edge.

**Already proven by tests:** the full suite passes; the spec checks that the wall covers the view whether the FOV is kept on the horizontal or the vertical, and on a non-square view.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE.

## Editor steps (for the editor worker)

1. Close the editor, pull dev on the main checkout, `Scripts/ue/build.sh`, open the editor.
2. Open `Content/Data/Combat/DA_BattleTransition_Default` and set:

| Section | Field | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timing | Crack Seconds | 0.25 | B-500's crack |
| Timing | Break Seconds | 0.2 | B-500's break |
| Timing | Break Ease | 1 | even speed: no slow start (B-500 had a fast start; 1 is the nearest the field allows) |
| Timing | Arrival Settle Seconds | 0 | no freeze beat on the battle map |
| Timing | Blow Away Seconds | 0.2 | B-500's fly-off |
| Timing | Blow Away Ease | 2 | B-500's fly-off curve (speeds up past the camera); no hang |
| Timing | Picture Fade Seconds | 0.1 | unchanged from B-500 |
| Motion | Crack Tilt Degrees | 5 | B-500 |
| Motion | Break Spread | 0.06 | B-500 |
| Motion | Break Toward Camera | 0.15 | B-500 |
| Motion | Break Tumble Degrees | 35 | B-500 |
| Motion | Drift Toward Camera | 0.3 | B-500: the shards keep moving while the battle map loads, instead of freezing |
| Motion | Drift Seconds | 2.5 | B-500 |
| Motion | Drift Tumble Degrees Per Second | 20 | B-500 |
| Motion | Blow Away Toward Camera | 1.6 | B-500 |
| Motion | Blow Away Spread | 0.5 | B-500 |

Leave the rest as it is (Max Hold Seconds, the shard count, the Prism light, the camera and lens). Save the asset and commit it.

The pause between the break and the fly-off is the battle map loading. No value changes it.
