# PIE — B-188: map features

**Branch:** `feature/B-188-map-features` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Trial.MapFeature` (5 cases) checks five things: a chest's roll only picks items tagged with its pool, and the same seed gives the same item; a weapon goes into the inventory and a spell is learned; a chest uses its zone's pool and opens only once per run; lore is recorded once however often it's read; a boss arena with no boss starts no fight.

**What changed:** three things to place on a trial map, each used with the interact key (E) while standing next to it (a hint shows):
- `TrialChest`: opens once per run and gives one item from its zone's loot pool (or its own pool, if set on the chest).
- `TrialLoreObject`: shows a line of placeholder lore; the first read goes into the run's found list.
- `BossArenaTrigger`: pressing E starts the boss fight against the boss of the variation you picked at the door (or the arena's Fallback Boss).

**Setup:**
- Tag one or two weapons or spells with `Source.Zone.Tier1` (their Source Tags).
- On a trial map, place an `EnemyZone` set to tier 1 with Loot Pool `Source.Zone.Tier1`, and put a `TrialChest` inside it.
- Place a `TrialLoreObject` (set its Title and Body, or keep the placeholder).
- Place a `BossArenaTrigger`, and give it a Fallback Boss (any enemy Character Data) unless the trial's variation has a Boss.
- Enter through the trial door. Have the Output Log open.
