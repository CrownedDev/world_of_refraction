**Branch:** `feature/B-042e-gas-damage-execution` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.GAS.DamageExecution` (5 cases) checks:
- GAS's damage maths gives today's damage, crit and blocked amount for attacks and spells, with no element, Fire and Water;
- it reads Defense and element resistance from the GAS stats (Ice also counts against Water), and keeps today's numbers for a fighter whose GAS stats aren't filled in;
- a skill's own crit chance and "ignore resistance" are kept;
- applying the GAS hit takes exactly that damage off the GAS Health.

The full suite (680) is green.
