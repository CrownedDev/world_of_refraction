# PIE — B-391b: damage numbers and the Broken Darkness card (Q-111, Q-391b)

**Branch:** `feature/B-391b-hud-pieces-cloud` (cloud pre-write, UNBUILT, on `feature/B-391-battle-hud-cloud`). Build B-391 first.
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.UI.DamageNumber`: colour per damage type (Physical and typeless white, Ice its own), size and brightness by the element multiplier with clamps, a weak, a resisted and an immune hit give three distinct styles, immune grey, heal green "+N", a crit bigger, Darkness's pale outline, rise and fade. `WoR.UI.BattleHUD`: the absorb line and its overload tints; on a duel two numbers on one target stack and a 0 is dropped. `WoR.Net.Presentation`: the multiplier rides with the hit; a heal counts apart from hits.
**How to open it:** start any fight with `wor.UI.NewHUD 1` (the default). For a Broken Darkness card, bring a BD character (innate Darkness + `bBrokenDarknessInnate`).
**RPC change:** `UCombatPresentationComponent::Multicast_ShowHit` now takes one `FCombatHitShown`. `OnHitShown` keeps its old three parameters and fires for hits only, so a Blueprint bound to it is unchanged (check 10).

**Known:** the number starts at the top of the target, not the exact impact point (the hit event has no location). Immune can't happen in play today (resistance caps at 50%), so check 4 is the only way to see grey. A drain heal with no status manager, or a GAS-only heal that skips `BroadcastHealing`, shows no number. Placeholder looks: one font, sizes and slopes are `TODO: tune` in `DamageNumberView.h` (Q-391b).
