# PIE — B-150: fallen members stay in the party; a rest revives them

**Branch:** `feature/B-150-fallen-members` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Party.Fallen` checks four things: a member dead at a fight's end stays in the party, marked fallen; they are left out of the next fight's roster; a member with no carried state is not fallen; a rest brings the fallen back at full HP without healing the standing.

**What changed:** D-043. A member who dies and is still down when the fight ends stays in your party, marked fallen. They sit out the next fights until revived, as before. A rest spot now revives every fallen member at full HP. Rest spots don't exist yet, so the placeholder party screen has a stand-in **Rest: revive the fallen** button.

**Setup:** a trial with a party of two or three (recruit members first). Output Log open. The party screen opens with the console command `wor.UI.PartyControl`.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In a trial fight, let one non-leader member die, then win the fight | Back in the trial, `wor.Party.Print` shows that member "carried HP 0 ... (FALLEN - sits out until revived)" | [ ] |
| 2 | Open `wor.UI.PartyControl` | The member's row reads "<name> (fallen): played by ..." | [ ] |
| 3 | Start the next fight | The fallen member does not spawn; the others do | [ ] |
| 4 | Back in the trial, press **Rest: revive the fallen** (or run `wor.Party.ReviveFallen`) | Output Log "Revived 1 fallen member(s)"; the row loses "(fallen)"; `wor.Party.Print` shows full HP for them, and a wounded standing member keeps their HP | [ ] |
| 5 | Start another fight | The revived member fights again at full HP | [ ] |
