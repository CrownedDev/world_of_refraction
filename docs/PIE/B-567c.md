# PIE — B-567c: skills limited per piece, and an innate limit (D-225, D-240, Crown 2026-11-17)

**Branch:** `refactor/B-567c-skills-per-piece` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-17). Not seen in the editor or played yet.

**What it is:**
- **Most Skills Per Piece** (tick box) replaces Most Skills: how many skills each piece may hold. Unticked: every slot the piece has by the player's rules (its tier's slots; a weapon's attached crystal adds its own). No more default of 3.
- **Most Innate** (tick box): its innate budget in points (each innate spell costs its tier, F 1 to S 7, less its pillar discount). Unticked: D-240's budget, 3 plus 1 per pillar point of its own levels (Mind + Body + Spirit), at most 24 (Broken Darkness: 3, each point worth 2, a pillar's seventh worth 3, up to 48). At 1 / 7 / 1 that is 12. It keeps the authored innate spells that fit, in order.
- The roll now puts each skill on a piece that holds it (an ability on a weapon of its type, a spell on an adapter whose crystal matches), so the fight wears exactly what the roll printed.
- Print Enemy Kit shows `skills per piece ..., innate ...` and `innate budget now N points`. `wor.Trial.PrintEnemyKits` lists each piece with its own skills.

**Old values don't carry over:** the old Most Skills is dropped on load (no enemy had it set).
