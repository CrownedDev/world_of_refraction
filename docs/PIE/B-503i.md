# PIE — B-503i: the party strip cards and the field attack chip, as the mockup draws them

**Branch:** `fix/B-503i-party-strip-cards` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** Crown's PIE of B-503g / B-503h: the cards were a fixed narrow width ("Character Template" cut off, the name and HP bar running under the next box), the chip sat on the last card, no frames, no icon and the element in red text. Now each card is framed as in `docs/Design/screen_mockups/hub/hud/1_hub_hud_prompt.png` (thin light outline, dark fill, the top-left corner cut) and sizes to its name between a minimum and a maximum width; a longer name ends in "..." inside the card; the HP bar spans the card under the name. The field attack chip is a card of the same style after the strip, with the same gap: the item's icon (or the icon table's weapon-type / element icon, or the weapon type in words when there is no icon), the name, and the element as a coloured dot with its name. On the hub map the cards show names only, no HP bar (D-216); the trial keeps the bar.
D-216's shared battle card (one card class for the field and the fight, show levels) is B-503j, not this job.

**Already proven by tests:** the full suite passes; specs check that four widest cards and the widest chip fit across a 16:9 screen, and the chip's name, icon and element dot for a weapon, a ring and the plain swing.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE; the widths (card 207 to 300, chip up to 360, in 1080p units) and the 9-unit corner cut are first guesses from the mockup.
