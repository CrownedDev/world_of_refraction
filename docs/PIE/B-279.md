# PIE — B-279: character forms, part 1 (the form asset and the meter)

**Branch:** `feature/B-279-character-forms` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Character.Forms`: 3 of 6 pieces fill slower than the full set; another tier gives half credit; the best party member counts (not the sum); 100% unlocks and caps; the meter survives save bytes and a load. Full suite green.

What changed: a new data asset, **CharacterFormData**, names an authored character. Its character asset's inventory is the loadout the player is matched against. Each fight won adds to that form's meter for the pieces the party wore (full set = 25% a fight, for now). At 100% the form unlocks (no skin swap yet: B-279b).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor.
1. Content Browser, right-click, Miscellaneous, Data Asset, pick **CharacterFormData**. Name it `DA_Form_Test`.
2. Set **Character** to a character asset whose inventory holds the weapon (and spells) your party member uses in a test fight. Set **Form Name**.
3. Click **Print Form** in the Details panel.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Click Print Form on `DA_Form_Test` | Output Log: `[Form] <name> ...` with its reference pieces listed | [ ] |
