# PIE — B-176: flee (D-091)

**Branch:** `feature/B-176-flee` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Combat.Flee` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - only a normal fight can be fled, and the Gold share rounds down
  - in a normal fight, fleeing takes the Gold share and ends the fight once, as Fled
  - in a boss fight, the flee is refused, no Gold is taken and the fight goes on
  - a mini-boss fight and an enemy are refused

**What changed (D-091):**
- **Flee** is now on the command menu.
- In a **normal** fight it ends the fight at once:
  - each party member loses 10% of their Gold (**Q-176**: confirm the cost)
  - the results screen says **FLED**
  - you go back to the trial with your HP/EP as they were, and the enemy is still there
- In a **mini-boss or boss** fight, Flee is greyed with "You can't flee this fight."
- Encounters have a new **Rank** setting (Normal by default) on their Encounter component.
- Note: Gold doesn't carry across the battle swap yet (B-090), so the Gold loss only shows on the battle pawn (Output Log line).

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and enter a fight from the trial level. For check 4, set an enemy's Encounter component **Rank** to Boss first.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In a normal fight, on your turn, look at the command menu | **Flee** is listed after Defend | [ ] |
| 2 | Choose **Flee** | The results screen shows **FLED**; the Output Log shows "fled the fight - party lost N Gold" | [ ] |
| 3 | Press Continue | You are back in the trial, the enemy is still on the map, and your party has the HP/EP it had when you fled | [ ] |
| 4 | With the enemy's Rank set to **Boss**, enter the fight | Flee is greyed, its description reads "You can't flee this fight."; clicking it does nothing | [ ] |
| 5 | Get stunned in a normal fight | Flee is not on the menu while stunned (only Attack and Defend are) | [ ] |
