# PIE — B-023: the AI sees Stun and finds cleanse spells

**Branch:** `fix/B-023-ai-live-enums` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds, and every test passes, including the new `WoR.AI.StatusQueries` spec.
- The spec failed on the old code in the two places this job fixes, and passes now. It checks:
  - the AI counts Stun as a dangerous status, but not Wind's Skip Turn (a turn-order delay, D-014) or a plain debuff
  - the AI always values landing Stun
  - the AI finds a spell with a **Cleanse Self** effect. It skips **Cleanse Allies** (that one doesn't clear the caster) and spells without a cleanse

**What changed for the player:**
- Enemies notice when a target is stunned (Lightning) and stop piling status buildup on it.
- Wind's Skip Turn no longer counts as dangerous, so enemies don't waste a turn cleansing it and keep building status on a Wind-capped target.
- An enemy with a Cleanse Self spell and a burn that would kill it now casts that spell. Before, it never found one.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a fight against an enemy. Best case: one enemy that has a Cleanse Self spell, and a party member who can build the Lightning and Fire status bars.
