# PIE — B-550: Dormant Essence, one balance (D-235)

**Branch:** `feature/B-550-dormant-essence` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What changed:** Gear essence and Skill essence are one balance, **Dormant Essence**. Gear upgrades and skill buys both charge it; run rewards and dismantling both pay it. Costs and yields keep their numbers. Elemental and Stat essence are unchanged. An older save keeps its essence: its Gear and Skill balances are added together on load (no wipe).

**Tip:** `wor.Wallet.Add GearEssence 200` gives Dormant Essence for the checks below.

|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | In the hub, upgrade a weapon or adapter | It costs Dormant Essence; the chip drops by the cost | [ ] |
