# PIE — B-616: the vendor screen in three columns, and the Blacksmith's Upgrade tab

**Branch:** `feature/B-616-vendors-v2` (from dev 3418bc390).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/hub/vendors/v2/` (1 Buy, 2 Sell, 3 Upgrade).
**Decision:** Q-616 (the three columns; Upgrade picks a target tier, a jump costs every step; the tile grid and the Combat Master's skill upgrade wait as B-616b and B-616c). Folds in B-609 (Q-609).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.VendorScreen` (16, with the Upgrade spec: the summed cost, the wrap, the refusals, the screen raising a dagger two tiers for both steps' cost) and the full suite, headless.
**Setup:** the QA save folder only (`-saveddirsuffix=QA`), Play in a new window, in the hub. `wor.Debug.MaxResources` tops the wallet up to 9999. Talk to the Blacksmith (or `wor.UI.VendorScreen Blacksmith`).

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the Blacksmith | Tabs Buy, Sell, Upgrade, Dismantle. Three columns: the stock rows on the left (icon, name, tag, tier, count, a +; no price on the row), the lit thing in the middle (name, kind line, its lines), "Details" on the right with the price, over the cart | [ ] |
| 2 | A on a stock line, then X | The line goes in the cart (right column); X buys it, as before | [ ] |
| 3 | RB to Sell | Your gear on the left; the middle names the lit piece; Details says "You get" and what it pays. A asks, the second A sells (as before) | [ ] |
| 4 | RB to Upgrade | Your weapons and adapters (LT / RT for another member). A row reads e.g. "F → E"; Details: the Dormant Essence it costs, then "Tier: F → E", "Dismantles for: …", "Dormant Essence: 9,999 → …" | [ ] |
| 5 | Right twice on a piece, then Left | The target goes up a tier each press (the cost grows by each step's cost), wraps from S back to the next tier; Left goes back | [ ] |
| 6 | A | "Upgrade … from F to D for … Dormant Essence?" with Confirm / Cancel. B cancels | [ ] |
| 7 | A, A | "… is now D." The row now starts from D; the wallet chip shows the essence spent | [ ] |
| 8 | Open the loadout (or the inventory) | The piece shows its new tier | [ ] |
| 9 | Pick a piece at S (or set one: `wor.Debug.SetGearTier <name> S`) | The row is greyed: "Already at S, the highest tier."; A says it and asks nothing | [ ] |
| 10 | In a run, rest at an Anvil (`wor.Trial.SetRestService anvil` at a rest spot) | The Blacksmith's screen with the Upgrade tab; it works the same | [ ] |
