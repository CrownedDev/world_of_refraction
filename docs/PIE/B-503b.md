# PIE — B-503b: the field attack's swing (D-215)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503b-field-swing` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** with a weapon entry in use, the field attack plays the weapon's battle swing without the dash, in place, and the hit lands when the blade does (the montage's Impact notify, 0.40 s for the sword and shield) instead of at the press.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Field.Attack` (8 tests; new: the hit's delay is the montage's earliest Impact notify at the montage's rate, and no delay with no montage; a weapon entry carries its attack's swing, and a second swing is refused until the hit has landed).
**Not proven:** the swing on screen. **Nothing changes until the editor steps below are done**: with no field montage set the attack hits at once with no animation, as before. Nothing was seen running from the writer's worktree.

## Editor steps first (for the editor worker)

1. **Build the montage.** With dev pulled and built on the main checkout, close the editor (the script saves packages) and run:
   `UnrealEditor-Cmd <uproject> -run=pythonscript -script="Scripts/editor/build_light_hit.py" -unattended -nullrhi`
   It makes `AM_SAS_LightHit_Field` in `/Game/Data/Skills/Weapons/SwordAndShield/LightHit/` (the strike clip `Anim_SAS_V2_ComboAttack02_01_IP` alone; notifies VFX0 at 0.30 s, SFX0 at 0.325 s, Impact0 at 0.40 s; no warp window) and sets **Animation > Field Montage** on `DA_SAS_Attack_LightHit`. The log should end with `[LightHitBuild] done`. Commit both assets.
2. **The map character's Anim Blueprint** needs a **Slot 'DefaultSlot'** node before its output pose, or no montage shows on the map. Add it if it is missing.
3. **Open `AM_SAS_LightHit_Field`** and preview it on the map character's mesh: the swing plays in place, with no slide.
4. **Other weapon types:** each weapon type's attack asset needs its own Field Montage (its strike without the approach). Only the sword and shield's Light Hit has one. A weapon whose attack has none hits at once with no animation.

## Checks

**Setup:** play a trial map with the starting sword and shield equipped. `wor.Field.CycleAttack` until the sword is in use; its line should read `(swing AM_SAS_LightHit_Field)`. Attack with Left mouse / RB, or `wor.Field.Attack`.

**Decided by default, Crown may change (Q-503b):**
- The hit lands when the blade does, read from where the character stands then.
- Cycling to another entry during the swing doesn't change the hit on its way.
- The character can still walk during the swing, so the feet may slide. If that looks wrong, say so: holding the character still for the swing, or swinging with the upper body only, is a follow-up.

`wor.Field.Print` shows the entry in use, its montage and, on the host during a swing, how long until the hit lands.
