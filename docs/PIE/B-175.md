# PIE — B-175: one free switch per turn

**Branch:** `feature/B-175-one-switch-per-turn` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Loadout.TurnSwitch` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - a second switch in the same turn is refused with a reason, and a ring switch shares the one free switch
  - the weapon flips only once
  - a switch that changes nothing keeps the switch free
  - in a real fight, the actor's next turn allows a switch again

**What changed (rules page 04, ET-19):** Switch Weapon and Switch Ring are still free (they don't end the turn), but only **one** of them per turn. After switching, both buttons are greyed with "Already switched this turn." Each of your turns gives the switch back, bonus turns included (**Q-175**). A ring that breaks still auto-switches to the next ring without using your switch.

**Setup:** open the editor on `dev`, open the Output Log, and start a test fight from the trial level with a Generic (two weapons) or a character with 2+ rings.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On your turn, press **Switch Weapon** (or **Switch Ring**) | The switch happens; the menu comes back with both switch buttons greyed, reading "Already switched this turn." | [ ] |
| 2 | Click a greyed switch button | Nothing happens | [ ] |
| 3 | Act (attack, defend…) and wait for your next turn | The switch buttons are usable again | [ ] |
| 4 | Check the Output Log after `DebugLogCapabilities` (or opening the menu) | A "Turn switch: free / used" line matching the buttons | [ ] |
| 5 | In the editor, search Blueprints for **Toggle Equipment** | No combat Blueprint calls it directly (that would skip the rule). If one does, note it | [ ] |
