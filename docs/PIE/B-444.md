# PIE — B-444: the "Spirit of {character}" screen

**Branch:** `feature/B-444-spirit-forms-screen` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/hub/skins/1_forms.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.FormsScreen` (3 cases): the title names the character and your own form comes first, worn; a character not met is "???" with no pieces until you own one of their pieces; a moved meter meets them, and the unlock names every piece.
**What changed:** `wor.UI.Forms` opens the new screen in place of the plain-button placeholder (which is now `wor.UI.FormsOld`). Left: your own form, then each character form with its meter (gold when unlocked), "???" for someone you haven't met. Right: the lit form, its percent, what it needs, and their pieces you own or wear ("?" for the rest). A wears the lit form, Y switches Manny / Quinn. Decisions: **Q-444** (reference-backed default).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** the look, the keys and gamepad; that wearing a form changes the body in the hub.
**Left out:** the form preview picture; the pieces as icon tiles (a list of names); a "Spirit of {character}" row in the rest menu (comes with the rest menu).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, play in the hub. To see an unlocked form, fill one with the forms debug commands (`wor.Forms.*`, see `wor.UI.PrintForms`).
