# PIE — B-341: trimmed turn rules (D-133)

**Branch:** `feature/B-341-turn-rules-trim` (in `dev`; built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**Behaviour change on merge:** `GetEmeraldBonusTurnDelay` is now 0 for every tier. The enemy AI's enemy-target Emerald (secure a lethal DoT) is unchanged; its self-target Emerald (wait for EP to afford a stronger action) can no longer fire, since no EP regens before an immediate bonus turn (flagged OBSOLETE in `UAIDecisionManager`). Extra Action's turn now passes the 2:1 cap (Q-341c). **For Crown:** self-targeting an F to A Emerald spends your turn and gives you one back (a net-zero swap); only S gains a turn (Q-341b calls the tier split a tuning matter).
**Note:** the speed timeline is unchanged. Ties now go base speed → your party → slot. Every Emerald's bonus turn lands right after the current turn (S: two). Open the console with `~`; `wor.PrintBelt` logs the turn belt.
