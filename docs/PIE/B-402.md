# PIE — B-402: side-on Defence view

**Branch:** `feature/B-402-side-on-defence-view` (in dev; built on the desktop 2026-11-12 from `feature/B-402-side-on-defence-view-cloud`).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Combat.Camera`: one target gives a low side-on two-shot from the attacker's weapon side with both fighters whole on screen; three targets and the attacker all on screen in a wider shot; a melee-range attacker stays on screen; `SteadyForDefence` drops shake and FOV punch only while defending.
**Setup:** a fight with an enemy that has a multi-hit (combo) skill and one that hits the whole party, camera movement on (`wor.Camera.MovementOff 0`), enemy cinematics on. Keep `wor.Camera.Print` handy. It's a feel check: compare with the reference fight (Expedition 33, Maelle vs Painted Maelle, 5:38–5:52).
