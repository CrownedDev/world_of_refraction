# PIE — B-459b: Play together (Invite)

**Branch:** `feature/B-459b-invite-screen` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/coop/session/1_invite.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.InviteScreen` (3 cases): who plays each seat (you as host, a friend by name, an AI seat, a held one); friends with an invited one marked "invited · waiting"; the session row (Host a session / Stop hosting); with no friends list the screen says how a friend finds the session; an invite before hosting is refused.
**What changed:** the party screen's Invite row and the rest menu's Invite open the new "Play together" screen in place of the plain-button placeholder (`wor.UI.Invite` still opens the old one until this pass). Left: your friends (A invites). Right: the party's seats and who plays each, then Host a session / Stop hosting (A). X moves between the two lists. Decisions: **Q-459b** (reference-backed default).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** a real friends list and invite (Steam / EOS; on LAN there is no list); hosting and stopping with a friend in the game; the look.
**Left out:** online / in another game / offline status and the dots by each friend (the friends list gives names only); the portraits by each seat; seating a friend (B-459c, the join request card).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, play in the hub. For friends and invites, a build with an online subsystem that has a friends list; on LAN, two machines and `wor.Session.Find` / `wor.Session.Join`.
