# PIE — B-435b: same-element stacking for spells

**Branch:** `feature/B-435b-spell-stacking-cloud` (cloud, **unbuilt**: build first; from `origin/dev`).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Infusion.SameElementStack` (an innate Fire spell with a Fire gem stacks at any level and the gem adds no EP; a ring Fire spell with a Fire innate pays the innate's EP for ×1.25 buildup, a Water innate doesn't join; a Reality caster stacks on Innate or the ring; the L2 HP doubles with a same-element evolution). Not provable headless: the battle UI's numbers, the hit's element for a Broken Darkness pick, the AI.
**Setup:** pull the branch, build, start a fight with a Fire Caster holding a Fire (Garnet) ring with a Fire spell, and a Fire crystal in the weapon if the class allows.
