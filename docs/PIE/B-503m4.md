# PIE — B-503m4: the wheel on RB, one slice per loadout entry (D-217, D-216, Q-503m4)

**Branch:** `feature/B-503m4-wheel-on-rb` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-15).
**What it is:** step 1 of Crown's four (apply the UI), after the v2 mockup (`docs/Design/screen_mockups/hub/hud/v2/3_trial_wheel_open.png`). In a trial, **tap RB** (left mouse) to attack; **hold RB** (hold R on keyboard) half a second and a wheel opens **above the strip, bottom left**. One **slice per loadout entry** (each weapon, the adapter or rings, a Refractor's innate, the evolution), each with a diamond in its element's colour. The **left stick** (the mouse on PC) picks a slice; **D-pad left / right** (A / D or the mouse wheel) changes its element: a weapon's plain / crystal, Water / **Ice**, Reality's (R) Fire, Broken Darkness's Dark Fire. The middle shows "◄ Fire ►" and below it what the strip will read ("Fire Sword"). **Letting go** confirms. LB does nothing on the map now. The played character's card is always the **first card**; the trial card has **no build-up bar**. A placeholder look in C++ (no `.uasset`).

**This pass is look only** (Crown's step 2). Say what to change; the code and data are aligned afterwards (B-503o to B-503r).

**Already proven by tests:** the slices and their options per class (a weapon user's plain + crystal per weapon, a ring infusing the weapon in hand, a Refractor's crystal and innate, Water's Ice, Reality's and Broken Darkness's options); D-pad stepping wraps inside a slice; the mouse pointer stays inside the wheel; the played card first; no build-up bar on the trial card.
**Not proven:** nothing seen in PIE. Whether the left stick and A / D really stop the character while the wheel is up depends on how the pawn's move keys are mapped (check 4); whether mouse up is up on the wheel (check 5).
