# PIE — B-432: buying for a member no longer needs room in your own armoury

**Branch:** `fix/B-432-buy-for-capacity` (in dev, built 2026-11-14; written by the cloud, built and tested on the desktop).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Economy.BuyFor`: a payer whose weapons are full buys a weapon for a receiver with room; the payer is still full afterwards and is still refused when buying for itself; a receiver with no room is still refused before paying.
**What changed:** when you buy a weapon or ring for another party member, only the receiver's room is checked. The piece only passes through the buyer.
**Setup:** a merchant selling a weapon or ring; a party of two; fill the buyer's weapons (or rings) to the limit.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | With the buyer's weapons full, pick the **other member** with the shop's buy-for button and buy a weapon | The purchase goes through; the other member has the weapon; the buyer's count is unchanged | [ ] |
| 2 | Same, but buy **for the buyer** | Refused as before (no room), nothing spent | [ ] |
| 3 | Fill the **receiver's** weapons too, then buy for them | Refused before paying, nothing spent | [ ] |
| 4 | Repeat 1 with a ring | Same as 1 | [ ] |
