# PIE — B-579: Health Potion and Energy Potion

**Branch:** `feature/B-579-potions`.
**What changed (D-253, Q-579):**
- Two new items. A Health Potion gives a living target 25% of its max HP; a Healing Stone used first makes it heal more. An Energy Potion gives 25% of its max EP, with no status build-up.
- One strength each: no tier, one stack, no element, no socket. Both are fight items.
- A dead target is refused and the potion is not spent.
- The AI's low-HP heal uses the Health Potion.
- A potion costs 12 Prisms (half a tier F crystal's Prisms, no essence) and can't be sold, dismantled or merged.
