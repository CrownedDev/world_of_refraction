# PIE — B-579: Health Potion and Energy Potion

**Branch:** `feature/B-579-potions`.
**What changed (D-253, Q-579):**
- Two new items. A Health Potion gives a living target 25% of its max HP; a Healing Stone used first makes it heal more. An Energy Potion gives 25% of its max EP, with no status build-up.
- One strength each: no tier, one stack, no element, no socket. Both are fight items.
- A dead target is refused and the potion is not spent.
- The AI's low-HP heal uses the Health Potion.
- A potion costs 12 Prisms (half a tier F crystal's Prisms, no essence) and can't be sold, dismantled or merged.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Items.Potions` (8 tests) and the full suite, headless. Not proven there: the fight menu, the tile and text on screen, a merchant's shelf, the vendor's sell and dismantle refusal.
**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`, "How an agent plays"). Play on the QA save folder only.
**Setup:** in the hub, `wor.Debug.GiveTestKit potions` (5 of each), and `wor.Economy.GiveCrystal HealingStone F 2`. Then `wor.Inventory.PrintSlots`.
**To know:** no merchant stocks potions until its asset gets the two rows (Q-579); checks 10 and 11 need those rows, added in the editor: skip them and say so if they are not there.
