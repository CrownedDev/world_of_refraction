# PIE — B-441: the inventory screen

**Branch:** `feature/B-441-inventory-screen` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.InventoryScreen`: each tab lists its own things and marks the worn piece; a tile's verbs are Give and Store, with Take only on the Chest tab; a verb that can't run is greyed with its reason (a worn primary; the chest during a run); the pouch draws its slots; "Give to" says who has room and refuses a full pouch; Take is greyed when your pouch has no room, and a whole stack moves only what fits; the screen gives, stores a whole stack and steps back one level at a time.
**What changed (D-181, D-177, D-195):** the game menu's **Inventory** page opens the real screen: character tabs, **Gear / Pouch / Chest** tabs, a grid of tiles, the focused thing's details on the right with its verb menu (**Give, Store**, and **Take** on the Chest tab only).
**Decided by default (Q-441, you may change it):** during a run the home chest can't be used at all: Store and Take are greyed with "The chest stays at home until the run ends", for pouch stacks too (the mockup's "Home chest: in the hub only").
**Not built yet (B-461):** the gear filter row, Sort, evolution and apparel tiles, icons (a letter stands in: W weapon, A adapter, C crystal, S stone), the place name top right.
**Kept until this passes:** the old plain-button inventory (`wor.UI.Inventory`).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, PIE in the hub with a party of two or more that owns a spare weapon and some crystals.
