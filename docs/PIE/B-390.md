# PIE — B-390: a curse's tier and clear price

**Branch:** `feature/B-390-curse-tiers-clear-price` (desktop, built, specs green; in `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Memory.Curse`: an F-tier Pillar curse's clear costs the F step of the item essence price ladder (10 today), an S-tier costs more, Elemental can be chosen; short of the essence the curse stays, nothing is spent and a message says why; with enough it clears and spends only that essence; a cleared curse can still be regained.
**Setup (editor):** the **Curse Data** asset `TestCurse` from PIE B-378b. Its new **Clear** fields: Tier `F-Tier`, Clear Essence `Pillar` (the defaults). Press **Print Curse**: the line ends `clear costs 10 Pillar Essence`. Hold the curse (M-Curse's fragment, PIE B-378).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the hub, `wor.UI.ClearCurse` | "Curse 1 of 1: <title> (F-Tier, clearing costs 10 Pillar Essence)" | [ ] |
