# PIE — B-214c: field skills part 3 (glide, high / double jump, air dash, wall-run)

**Branch:** `feature/B-214c-ground-air-moves-cloud` (cloud, **unbuilt**: build first; it carries B-214b's commits).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Field.Moves` (each move's refusals, EP, glide gravity and fall cap, the double jump and air dash once per airtime, Lightning's blinks, the wall-run hold and its once-per-airtime rule). Not provable headless: the feel, the landing event, the wall trace against real geometry, co-op.
**Setup:** pull the branch, build, play a trial map with ledges and walls. Keys come in B-214g, so use the console (`~`). Unlock each type first, e.g. `wor.Field.Unlock Glide Wind` (the leader needs a source of that element: a Caster or a crystal).
