# PIE — B-521: pieces start empty, and the axes (D-224, asset overhaul stage 3)

**Branch:** `feature/B-521-pieces-start-empty` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is:** stage 3 of `Design/AssetOverhaulPlan.md` (§3 step 2). A weapon or adapter that is **new to you** arrives with an **empty socket**, whatever crystal its asset still has baked in:
- bought from a merchant (and shown empty in the shop);
- found in a chest or dropped;
- given, a class kit piece, or a piece restored from a save (its socket comes from the save, as before).
- **The merchant's price** no longer adds the baked crystal: a piece costs its own tier.

**What keeps its baked crystal until stage 6 (Q-521):** the old authored layout only: a character's or enemy's inventory asset (`Weapons`, `Rings`) and its authored loadouts. So enemies keep their spells, and today's played characters keep their crystals, until stages 4 and 5 replace those assets.

**The axes:** no code. "Hand Axe + Bearded Axe" is the joined piece name and "Dual Axes" the held type, both already built (B-513b, B-477).

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Loadout.PiecesStartEmpty` (a new weapon and ring mint empty whatever the asset has; the authored layout keeps the Garnet; a merchant's ring costs its tier base only). Full suite 2253 passed and 1 failed (this spec's price case, which needed a test world); fixed, the spec 3 of 3; validation 16 known, 0 new.

**Not proven:** the merchant, a chest drop and the axes in PIE.

**Known limit:** an old authored loadout whose piece reference no longer matches an owned piece still falls back to the asset, baked crystal and all (rare: a stale reference). Stage 6 removes that fallback.

**Prebuilt finds** (a found ring that comes with its crystal or evolution, Crown Q7) are **not in this stage**: they reuse the starting gear's `Crystal` field, so they come with stage 4.

## Editor step (before checks 3-4): the axes

The editor worker makes, per `Design/AssetOverhaulPlan.md` §2 and `Conventions/ContentNaming.md`:
- `DA_Weapon_Axe_Hand` ("Hand Axe", Axe, SingleOrDual handling, F tier, no crystal, no skills, its own mesh, an axe attack and stance);
- `DA_Weapon_Axe_Bearded` ("Bearded Axe", the same shape, its own mesh);
- both added as spares on `DA_Inventory_Generic`, as the stage 1 and 2 pieces were.
