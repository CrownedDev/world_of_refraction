# PIE — B-310: run wear follows the item's GUID

**Branch:** `fix/B-310-run-wear-by-guid` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Party.RunWearByGuid`: with two identical adapters on a Resonator, a cast wears the active one; each copy's carried wear goes back to the same item even when the two swap places; a crystal slot finds its copy by GUID, and an unknown GUID finds nothing.
- The full suite is green.

What changed: wear carried between fights, and crystal break / destroy / repair, follow the owned item instead of its asset, so two identical adapters (or weapons) no longer share one wear record. With two identical copies, wear lands on the one in use (Q-310).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. A Resonator with two copies of the same adapter, each with a crystal, in a trial run.
