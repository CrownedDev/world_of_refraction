# PIE — B-628: the party screen in three columns (seats, the active loadout, Details)

**Branch:** `feature/B-628-party-screen` (on top of `feature/B-629-skills-tab`).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/party/v2/1_party.png`.
**Decision:** Q-628 (no Invite row; the middle is the active loadout only; the keys).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.PartyScreen` (the active loadout's pieces and skills, Dismiss's refusals, the start place) and the full suite, headless.
**Setup:** the QA save folder only (`-saveddirsuffix=QA`), Play in a new window, in the hub, with at least two members in the party (recruit one in the hub). Debug: `wor.UI.PrintParty` logs each member with its loadout.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the game menu (Tab), then Party (or `wor.UI.Party`) | Three columns. Left: "Party", "N of 3", "recruit in the hub or a trial", then a card per member (name, class, who plays it, "Seat N" at the right). No Invite row | [ ] |
| 2 | Look at the middle column | The lit member's name and class, its loadout's title with "✓ in use", then a plate per piece: name, type and tier, and its slotted skills ("No skills slotted" when none); a Caster also has Innate spells | [ ] |
| 3 | Look at the right column | Details, the name, "Played by" (with ‹ › on a member that isn't the leader), "Starts" ("Back Row, top" or "Auto"), the 3 x 3 grid with the lit member's cell lit, enemies ► at the right, three buttons and the note | [ ] |
| 4 | Down / Up (or move the mouse over a card) | The lit card moves; the middle column, Details and the grid follow | [ ] |
| 5 | On the second member, Right (or click the Played by value) | Played by turns You ↔ AI; the card's line follows. On the leader: "The leader can't be given to the AI." in orange | [ ] |
| 6 | A (or click Move start place) | The member's cell steps through the grid; Starts names the new place. A member already there swaps | [ ] |
| 7 | Y (or click Open loadout) | The loadout screen opens on that member's Equipment page. Change nothing, B: back on the party screen, same member lit | [ ] |
| 8 | On the leader, look at the X button | Greyed, "The leader can't be dismissed." under it; X shows the same in orange | [ ] |
| 9 | On another member, X once | The button turns lit and reads "X again: dismiss <name>"; Down (another card) puts it back to "Dismiss" | [ ] |
| 10 | X, then X again on that member | The member leaves: its card is gone, "N of 3" drops, the card in its place (or the last one) is lit | [ ] |
