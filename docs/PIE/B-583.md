# PIE — B-583: camera rules (D-255)

**Branch:** `feature/B-583-camera-rules`.
**What changed (D-255, its defaults a to f):**
- Hub and trial: the look goes at most 10° up and 55° down (it was the engine's 89.9 both ways, so looking up put the camera in the floor). Yaw is free. Set by `AWoRHubController` on its camera manager (`WoRCameraRules`).
- From contact with an enemy team (walking in, or a field hit) until the fight starts: the player who made contact can't move, and the look turns at most 30° either side and 10° up or down from where it pointed (still inside 10 up / 55 down). Allies can still join.
- If the fight doesn't start (the encounter re-arms), move and look are given back as they were.
- The fight's own camera is unchanged; distance, field of view, lag and turn speed are unchanged.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Field.CameraRules`, headless, through the engine's own view clamp (`APlayerCameraManager::ProcessViewRotation`): 10 up / 55 down; 30 yaw / 10 pitch around contact, across north (0/360); a release keeps the field wheel's own move hold; a field hit holds and the re-arm gives both back. Not proven there: a real mouse or stick, the feel, a real trial's contact, co-op.
**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`, "How an agent plays"). Play on the QA save folder only. `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`.
**To know:** the gamepad stick goes through the same clamp as the mouse (the camera manager limits the control rotation whatever turned it); checked in code, not by a pad. Not checked below the knee: on flat ground 10 up keeps the camera about 60 cm off the floor.
