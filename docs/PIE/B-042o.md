# PIE — B-042o: GAS stage 3, part 5e (the status bar on GAS)

**Branch:** `feature/B-042o-gas-status-bar` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Status.Buildup` now runs every case with `wor.Effects.UseGAS 0` and `1`, and both give the same results:
- the bar fills by the hit, less the target's resistance;
- an element-matched resistance buff lowers only its element;
- drains, decay and the full reset after quiet turns;
- the cap fires the element's status and empties the bar;
- all-status, stun and fire immunity block their hits;
- a burn that builds status fills the bar at its tick;
- bars are forgotten between fights.

A new case checks that with the switch on the bar's number lives in GAS's Buildup attribute (hit, decay, reset). The full suite (758) is green.

What changed:
- Nothing with the switch off (the default).
- With `wor.Effects.UseGAS 1`, the status bar's number is kept on GAS, and each hit is added by GAS's buildup calculation (the same maths). The bar's colour (last element) and its decay count stay as before.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, start the demo fight. Open the console (`~`).
