# PIE — B-456d: the vendor screen, piece 4 (the Spiritualist's Repair, Curses and Merge)

**Branch:** `feature/B-456d-spiritualist-services` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.VendorScreen`: Repair lists each worn piece with its price, a broken one says so, an empty wallet greys the rows and "repair all"; A asks, the second A repairs that piece and pays its price; X asks, Back cancels, the second X repairs the rest. Curses lists the held curse with its price; A asks, the second A clears it for that price. Merge lists the crystal kind at the highest tier it can make, with what it uses, makes and gives back; Left / Right changes the tier (past E it wraps to S, and S is greyed with the reason); the second A merges (one E made, two F used, the price paid). `WoR.Equipment.GearRepair`, `WoR.Memory.Curse`, `WoR.Economy.CrystalMerge`: the services' own rules, unchanged.
**What changed (D-193; mockups `hub/vendors/3_spiritualist_repair.png`, `4_spiritualist_curses.png`, `6_merge_crystals.png`):** every tab of the vendor screen now works. The Spiritualist's **Repair**, **Curses** and **Merge** tabs do what the console and the placeholder panels did. A repair can now be **one piece** or everything (before: everything only).
**Decided by default (Q-456d, Crown may change any):** only worn pieces are listed on Repair; Curses lists the player's curses (curses are held by the player, not by one character); Merge is one list with the tier picked on Left / Right (the mockup has two columns), starting at the highest tier you can make; in co-op only the host can pay (a guest's rows are grey).
**Left out:** icons; a durability bar (the row says "35%" or "broken"); the mockup's unworn rows with "—".

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, PIE in the hub. Give yourself money: `wor.Wallet.Add Prisms 5000` (hub Gold), `wor.Wallet.Add PillarEssence 500`.
