# PIE — D-245: what a new character picks is in its inventory

**Branch:** `feature/D-245-picks-owned` (built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-18). **Built unseen:** not played yet.

**What it is:** a new character knows its class's starting skills from the first moment in the hub. Before, they only appeared when a trial was entered and were gone after the run.
- The picked weapons (Generic) or the two empty adapters and picked crystals (Resonator) were already the character's own and worn from the start; that is unchanged. Checks 1 and 2 confirm it.
- The starting skills are known, not put in a slot (D-224).
- Only a character made after this build gets them. An older character keeps what it has. A co-op guest and a hub recruit don't get them.
- The starting skill is now an owned skill, so a lost run treats it like any owned skill (D-224).

**Defaults chosen (Crown may change, Q-245):** gear is worn at once, skills are known but not slotted; everything is the character's own, not shared; a run still starts on the class kit made as its own copies.

**Editor steps:** none.
