# PIE — B-185: first strike

**Branch:** `feature/B-185-first-strike` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Combat.FirstStrike` (4 cases) checks four things: the contact judgement (from behind the team = party, seen with your back turned = enemy, face to face = nobody); an enemy side with first strike takes its whole first round; the opening ends when the rest of that side falls; the party with first strike goes first even against a faster enemy, and the turn strip shows it.

**What changed:** how you meet an enemy team on the map now matters (a placeholder until the field attack exists). Walk into a team from behind (outside the 120° cone in front of where it faces or walks) and your party acts first: each member takes its first turn before any enemy moves. If a team that sees you walks into you while your back is turned, the enemies act first instead. Meeting face to face changes nothing.

**Setup:** a trial level with an idle `FieldEnemyTeam` (or an enemy pawn with an encounter) whose facing you know (its arrow in the editor). Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walk up to the team from behind and touch it | Output Log: "[EncounterComponent] ...: first strike EFirstStrike::Party", then "[BattleGameMode] First strike: EFirstStrike::Party" | [ ] |
| 2 | Look at the turn-order strip when the fight starts | Your party members are first, before every enemy, even a faster one | [ ] |
| 3 | Run `wor.Trial.Print` in the fight | `first strike=EFirstStrike::Party`. The turn-order debug print (TurnManager `DebugPrintTurnOrder`) marks the enemies `[WAITS: first strike]` until your side has acted | [ ] |
| 4 | Return to the trial; stand in front of a team facing away from it, and let it reach you (a patrolling team walking into your back works well) | "first strike EFirstStrike::Enemy"; in the fight the enemies take their first turns before you | [ ] |
| 5 | Touch a team face to face | "first strike EFirstStrike::None"; the normal speed order | [ ] |
