# PIE — B-295: Emerald bonus turn is a real extra turn

**Branch:** `fix/B-295-emerald-real-extra-turn` (merged into `dev`).
**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.Turns.EmeraldExtraTurn`: with equal speed the order goes Hero, Hero (bonus), Foe, Hero, Foe; the bonus costs no later turn (D-052).
- The full suite is green.

What changed (plays differently): before, an Emerald bonus turn only moved a turn earlier (the holder lost a later one). Now it's an extra turn on top. The 2:1 cap on it isn't built yet (B-297), so a fast holder can briefly get more than 2 turns per enemy turn.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. A character with an Emerald crystal that grants bonus turns.
