# PIE — B-067: small fixes

**Branch:** `fix/B-067-small-fixes` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Combat.SmallFixes` (2 cases) checks two things:
- A Double Hit's damage total equals all the HP the target lost.
- Turns keep flowing after a combatant's actor is destroyed.

**What changed:**
- The turn order skips a fighter whose actor is gone instead of crashing.
- A Double Hit's second hit counts in the damage total and gets the kill if it kills. Lifesteal now drains on the second hit too.
- A skill with only a ritual or return animation (no main one) no longer freezes the turn until the 8-second failsafe; neither does a caster removed between animation legs.
- A caster that dies during its own skill doesn't walk its corpse back or turn to face anyone.
- The combat orchestrator no longer reads a missing GameInstance at startup.

**Setup:** a normal fight; Output Log open.
