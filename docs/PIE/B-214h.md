# PIE — B-214h: field skills part 8 (evolutions)

**Branch:** `feature/B-214h-evolution-movement-cloud` (cloud, **unbuilt**: build first; it carries B-214b to B-214g).
**Already proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Field.Evolution` (the multiplier, the EP discount at least 1, combined flight limits; a seated evolution: faster sprint, cheaper field skills, unlimited flight; a broken one: nothing; the +15% / -25% defaults). Not provable headless: the sprint feel.
**Setup:** pull the branch, build, play the hub with the leader. Have an evolution crystal to seat on the character's own slot (the loadout's primary evolution).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No evolution seated: sprint; `wor.Field.Print` | `evolution none`; the normal sprint | [ ] |
| 2 | Seat an evolution on the character's own slot, sprint again | Clearly a bit faster (+15%); `wor.Field.Print` shows `evolution <name> (sprint +15%, field EP -25%)` | [ ] |
| 3 | With Enhanced Sprint unlocked and picked, turn it on with the evolution seated | Faster still (the element's multiplier times the evolution's) | [ ] |
| 4 | A 4-EP-or-more move (e.g. `wor.Field.Unlock Teleport Void`, a Void leader) with the evolution | It costs less EP than without (3 → 2) | [ ] |
| 5 | In the editor, tick the evolution asset's **Field Flight Limits → Unlimited EP**; fly with it seated | Flight costs no EP; unseat it and flight drains again | [ ] |
| 6 | Wear the evolution to broken (0 durability) | The bonus is gone: normal sprint, full EP costs | [ ] |
