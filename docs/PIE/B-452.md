# PIE — B-452: the settings screen

**Branch:** `feature/B-452-settings-screen` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockups:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/settings/1_gameplay_control_model.png`, `2_controls.png`, `3_accessibility.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.Settings` (6 cases): six named pages; Gameplay and Accessibility list every approved row; Turn View steps through its styles and back; the End Turn button toggles and the setting follows; a row that isn't built says so and doesn't change; the Controls page lists the built keys by section; the screen opens on Gameplay, wraps its pages and marks a change to save.
**What changed:** the game menu's Settings page opens a settings screen. Two settings work: **Turn View** and the **End Turn button**. Every other approved row is listed, greyed, and says "This setting isn't built yet." (they come one at a time: B-453; the battle control model is B-436l).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** how it looks, the keys and gamepad, and that a changed setting is still there after you restart.
**Left out:** the two control-model cards in the side panel; the device and section tabs on Controls (it is one list with both devices side by side); "Y Reset page"; Camera, Display and Audio have no rows yet.
**A correction to the mockup:** "AI finishes my turn when I idle" is not the code's existing "when your fighters fall, let the AI finish" option (that one is on the Party screen), so it is listed as not built.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor. The editor step of `docs/PIE/B-439.md` still applies. Esc ends PIE in the editor: use the gamepad, or `wor.UI.Pause` and Backspace.
