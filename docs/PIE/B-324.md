# PIE — B-324: gear in the shared chest

**Branch:** `feature/B-324-shared-chest-gear` (written unbuilt in a cloud session, **stacked on `feature/B-323-give-between-characters`**, which sits on `feature/B-322-per-character-pouch`; merge in that order).
**To be proven by tests (once built):** `WoR.Pool.Chest` (A puts a spare dagger in, B takes it and owns it; worn gear and the primary can't be put away; a later draw and a save leave chest gear in the chest). `WoR.Pool.SharedGear`, `WoR.Pool.Give`, `WoR.Pool.ItemSlots` still green.

What changed for play:
- The shared chest holds **unworn weapons and adapters** as well as items. Any member can take a piece and then owns it (`wor.UI.Storage`).
- A piece in any of your loadouts can't be put away: take it off first.
- Taking a piece you already own a copy of wakes the duplicate choice (`wor.UI.Duplicates`).
- Evolutions can't go in the chest yet (Q-323).

**Setup:** pull the branch, build, run the tests. Start PIE in the hub with a party of two or more; member A owns a spare weapon not in any loadout.
