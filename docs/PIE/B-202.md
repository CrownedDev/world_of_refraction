# PIE — B-202: one Guard animation; a Perfect Guard flashes and sounds

**Branch:** `fix/B-202-one-guard-anim-perfect-flash` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:** the editor builds and every test passes. `WoR.Defense.Guard` checks the rule: only a successful Perfect Guard (or a perfect legacy Parry press) gets the flash and sound. A normal Guard, a Dodge and a missed press get none.

**What changed:** Crown changed Q-182. Every Guard plays the block animation, normal or Perfect, with no switch to the parry animation. A Perfect Guard adds a flash and a sound at the defender when the hit lands. Each character's Cosmetics asset has two new fields, **Perfect Guard Flash VFX** and **Perfect Guard Sound**. Until the art exists they hold engine stand-ins: Niagara's RadialBurst template and the editor's compile-success chime. These stand-ins work in the editor only.

**Setup:** a fight against an enemy with a melee attack. Your character has a Cosmetics asset with a block montage. Output Log open.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guard an attack with normal timing | The block animation; no flash, no chime | [ ] |
| 2 | Guard an attack with Perfect timing | The same block animation (no parry animation); a burst flash and a chime at your character; Output Log "[DefenseSystem] Perfect Guard feedback for ..." | [ ] |
| 3 | Dodge an attack, Perfect or not | The dodge animation; no flash | [ ] |
| 4 | Open your character's Cosmetics asset | Perfect Guard Flash VFX and Perfect Guard Sound are filled with the stand-ins; swap in the real ones when ready | [ ] |
