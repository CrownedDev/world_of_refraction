# PIE — B-440d: the loadout screen, piece 4 (the Skills page)

**Branch:** `feature/B-440d-loadout-skills-page` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/loadout/3_skills_refractor.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.LoadoutSkills` (4 cases): a Refractor's innate spells by school, six slots a school, the points less the discount; the choices are the school's learned spells not already in, with the points after, and over the points they are refused; an innate spell goes in and comes out; a weapon's slots show its built-in until replaced, and X brings it back.
**What changed:** the loadout screen has a working **Skills** page (LB / RB, or Q / E). Left / Right picks the list: a Refractor's schools first ("Destruction 3"), then each weapon and adapter. A on a slot lists the skills the member knows that fit; A puts the lit one in. X takes it out (one press: nothing is lost). Decisions: **Q-440d** (reference-backed default).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** the look, the keys and gamepad; that a fight uses the new skills.
**Left out:** the lists are a line of text, not tab plates; R3 Details; clicking a page or list tab (keys only); a Broken Darkness Refractor's element pools.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor. A Refractor who knows a few spells (Sera), and a Generic with a weapon and a learned ability.
