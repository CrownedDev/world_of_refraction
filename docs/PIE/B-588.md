# PIE — B-588: Position (D-263 points 1 and 2)

**Branch:** `feature/B-588-position`.
**What changed (D-263, PA-32, Q-588):**
- The main menu's **Step Forward** and **Step Back** corner hints are gone; one **Position** hint (key **4** / **D-pad Up**) replaces them.
- Position opens a small list titled **Position**: **Front Row**, **Middle Row**, **Back Row**, then Back. The row you stand in is greyed "You are here."; the other corner hints (Defend, Flee, End Turn…) hide while it is open.
- Pick a row by a click, **1 / 2 / 3** or **A / X / Y**. You go straight there (Front to Back in one move), free; the turn goes on. Once per turn: afterwards Position is greyed "Already moved this turn.". Back (Backspace / B) leaves without moving.
- Push and pull skills still move one row.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.RowMove` "the Position move" (Front to Back in one move, once per turn, refused after the turn's step, for the row you're in, off-turn and stunned) and `WoR.UI.CommandMenu.Keys` (4 opens it, one Position hint and no Step, the picker's keys and marked row, only Back as a hint, 3 moves to the back, Back leaves without moving). Not proven there: the look, the pad, the mouse, the character's walk to the new row.

**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`). Play on the QA save folder only. Run every check at `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`, then repeat 2 and 3 at `SetWindowSize(Width=1920, Height=1080)`. `wor.UI.Print` prints the menu's rows (`hint` marks a corner hint).
