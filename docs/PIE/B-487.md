# PIE — B-487: the inventory and vendor screens use the icon table

**Branch:** `feature/B-487-inventory-vendor-icons` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.IconTable` (the texture for what an icon stands for; an item's effect as an icon kind), `WoR.UI.InventoryScreen` (a weapon tile asks for its weapon-type icon; a crystal tile for its element diamond, a tier badge and its effect's icon).
**Setup:** open the hub with a character who carries a weapon, a few crystals and a stone. Part A works now; part B needs the icons imported and the table filled (B-486). `wor.UI.Icons.Print` says which icons are set.

**A. Before the icons are imported (no icon table)**

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the inventory (the game menu's Inventory page) | Exactly as before: letters on the tiles (W, A, C, S, Ev, Ap), the tier in brackets after the name, no icons, no new gaps | [ ] |
| 2 | Open a vendor, the Buy and Sell tabs | Exactly as before: the tier letter in its column, the effect sentence as the last detail line | [ ] |

**B. After B-486 (the table is filled)**

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Inventory, Pouch tab | Each crystal tile shows its element's diamond in place of the letter C, with a tier badge in the top right corner; the count stays bottom right | [ ] |
| 4 | Inventory, Gear tab | Each weapon tile shows its weapon-type icon in place of W; a worn piece keeps its E | [ ] |
| 5 | Focus a crystal | In the panel: the diamond before its name, a tier badge after it (no "(C)" in brackets), and its effect's icon beside the effect sentence | [ ] |
| 6 | Hover the icons with the mouse | A tooltip with the name: the crystal's name, "Tier C", the weapon type, the effect's kind | [ ] |
| 7 | A water crystal | The Water diamond (an Ice crystal would show the same: Ice has none of its own) | [ ] |
| 8 | Vendor, Buy tab | A weapon line starts with its weapon-type icon; a crystal line with its element diamond and a tier badge in the tier column; an evolution with its element diamond and tier badge | [ ] |
| 9 | Focus a crystal on Buy | The panel's effect sentence sits on its own line beside its effect icon | [ ] |
| 10 | Vendor, Sell and Dismantle tabs | The same icons on the things you own | [ ] |
| 11 | Clear one icon in the table (say the Fire diamond) and reopen | Fire crystals read as text again (the letter, the tier in words); everything else keeps its icon | [ ] |

**Notes / what went wrong:**
- No icon: adapters and apparel (the table has none for them), skills on the vendor's Buy tab, the Repair, Curses, Item slots and Merge rows.
- A stone shows the stone icon only if the table's Stone Socket is set; until then it keeps its letter S and still gets its tier badge.
- A crystal's effect icon is chosen from what the crystal does (a Garnet burns: damage over time; a Sapphire: last stand); a use with no icon kind (a repair) has none.
