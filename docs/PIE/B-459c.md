# PIE — B-459c: the join request card

**Branch:** `feature/B-459c-join-request-card` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/coop/session/2_join_request.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.JoinRequestCard` (2 cases): the card names who asks and with which character, lists the AI seats, and says why a seat can't be given now (a fight, a closed seat away from a rest spot) and how many more wait; the run signals each request and answer; giving a seat seats the joiner; refusing hides the card.
**What changed:** when a friend asks to join, the host sees a small card in the top-right corner: "{friend} wants to join", "with {character} · {class}", the party's AI seats, and "Hold View (Tab) to answer". Hold View (Tab on keyboard) for half a second: the card takes the keys; Up / Down light a seat, A gives it, X refuses, B lets go. It replaces the plain-button placeholder (`wor.UI.JoinSeat` still opens the old one until this pass). Decisions: **Q-459c** (reference-backed default).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** two players in PIE; the hold on a real gamepad; that a tap of View / Tab still does what it did; the look.
**Left out:** the element icon by each seat; mouse clicks on the card (keys only).

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build. PIE with 2 players, Net Mode "Play As Listen Server". The host plays in the hub with an AI member in the party (game menu > Party). On the client's console: `wor.Party.RequestJoin <path of a CharacterData asset>`.
