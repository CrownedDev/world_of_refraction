# PIE — B-458: the party screen

**Branch:** `feature/B-458-party-screen` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Mockup:** `docs/Design/screen_mockups/menus/party/1_party.png`.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.PartyScreen` (3 cases): each member's row and who plays it (an AI reads "AI", no level text, D-200); the leader's seat stays yours and another member's seat toggles; X steps through the 3 x 3 start cells.
**What changed:** the game menu's Party page opens the new party screen in place of the plain-button placeholder (`wor.UI.PartyControl` still opens the old one until this pass). Left: one row per member (name, class, start row, field skill, "Played by You (leader)" / "You" / "AI"), then "Invite". Right: the starting grid (Back / Middle / Front, enemies to the right) and the lit member's guard and seat note. A changes the lit member's seat (you / AI) or opens Invite, X moves the lit member on the grid. Decisions: **Q-458** (reference-backed default).
**Not checked by me (needs your eyes):** the look, the keys and gamepad; that a fight starts members in the cells the grid shows.
**Left out:** "When your fighters fall" (retired by B-346: an AI seat plays on); Y "Swap order" (the party has no reorder yet); a friend's name in "Played by" and the real Invite screen (B-459); the tier chip by the name.

**Setup:** pull `dev`, build, open the editor, play in the hub with a party of two or three (`wor.Party.RecruitForRun` or the Gate).
