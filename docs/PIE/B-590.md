# PIE — B-590: turn order as small hexagon portraits, with two settings (D-264)

**Branch:** `feature/B-590-turn-hexes`.
**What changed (D-264, mockups `I_hex_row_small.png` (row) and `H_hex_column_small.png` (column)):**
- The top right's "Turn order" title and text list are gone. In their place is a strip of hexagons with no panel and no names:
  - the current turn first, about 56 px at 720p (84 at 1080p), with a violet edge;
  - the next turns about 40 px (60 at 1080p), with a cyan edge for the party and rose for enemies.
- Each hexagon shows the character's portrait (`CharacterData` → Portrait). A character with no portrait gets a plain dark hexagon with the same edge.
- Settings → Gameplay has two new rows:
  - **Turn order layout:** Row (default), or Column down the right side with the current turn on top.
  - **Turns shown:** 1 to 6 (default 6), counting the current turn. It stops at 1 and at 6.
  - Both are saved with the other settings. The fight reads them at each new turn.

**Already proven by tests:**
- `WoR.UI.BattleHUD`:
  - the hexagons' sizes, edge colours, the empty-slot skip and the Turns shown cut;
  - the hexagon's corners;
  - on a real duel, the hexagons lead with the acting character, and a HUD built after the settings change has 1 hexagon in a column.
- `WoR.UI.Settings`:
  - the two rows, their defaults, the toggle, the stops at 1 and 6;
  - an old save's 7 reads as 6;
  - an in-memory save and load.

**Not proven there:** the look at both window sizes in both layouts, the portraits themselves, and a change made in the pause menu during a fight.

**Written for an agent with `WoRAgentPlayToolset`** (`Conventions/HandsOffPlan.md`). Play on the QA save folder only.
- Run checks 1-6 at `SetWindowSize(Width=1280, Height=720)`, then repeat 1, 2, 4 and 5 at `SetWindowSize(Width=1920, Height=1080)`.
- Compare the size, colours and placement with the two mockups in `Saved_QA573/` (the faces in them are screenshot crops, so ignore the pictures).
- `wor.UI.Print` prints `turn order: row|column, N hexagons`, then one line per hexagon: `hex: <name> | current|next | party|enemy | <size> | portrait: <texture>|none (placeholder)`.

**To know (decided defaults, Q-590, Crown may change):**
- The placeholder is a plain dark hexagon.
- Bonus turns are no longer marked. The old list's green "(bonus)" has no place in a picture, and the text stays in `wor.UI.Print`.
- The current turn's edge is violet even when it's an enemy's turn.
- The gaps are 4 px at 720p, and the edges are 2 px (current) and 1.3 px (next).
- A portrait is stretched over the hexagon's square box, so art that isn't square will look squashed (check 2).
- A settings change shows from the next turn on.
