# PIE — B-179: trial vendors roll their stock per run

**Branch:** `feature/B-179-trial-vendor-stock` (in `dev`).
**Already proven:**
- The editor builds and every test passes, including the new `WoR.Merchant.StockRoll` spec.
- The spec checks that:
  - a home shop shows its full list
  - a trial vendor rolls the same stock from the same seed
  - it shows 8 different lines, in the order they are authored
  - another seed rolls other lines
  - a vendor with fewer than 8 lines shows them all

**What changed:** in a trial, a merchant now shows 8 lines picked at random from its list, rolled when you enter the trial and the same for the whole run. In the hub, merchants are unchanged. **Q-179**: what the vendor rolls from and whether its stock should run out.

**Setup:**
- Open the editor on `dev`.
- You need a merchant in a trial level. If the trial level has none, drag a merchant actor (`AMerchantInteractable`, e.g. the hub's Spiritualist) into it and set its **Merchant** to a merchant asset with more than 8 lines.
- Open the Output Log. Note that the shop still charges Prisms in a trial (Gold shops are B-073).

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the hub, open any merchant | Its full list, as before | [ ] |
| 2 | Enter the trial | Output Log: "[TrialRun] Entering trial ... (run seed N; replay with: wor.Trial.Seed N)" | [ ] |
