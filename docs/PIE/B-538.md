# PIE — B-538: the mouse on the title and the new-game screen (D-233)

**Branch:** `feature/B-538-mouse-front-screens` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is (Crown):**
1. The mouse cursor shows on the title and the new-game screen, in the menu level and over the hub (`wor.UI.Title`).
2. Title: moving the mouse over a row lights it; a click picks it, as Enter / A does. New Game's hold-to-confirm panel takes a held click.
3. New game: hovering a Body card, a Class card, an element or Begin lights it; a click picks it; a click on Begin begins, as A on Begin does.
4. Neither screen has a list long enough to scroll, so the wheel has nothing to do here.
5. Every other screen and what the mouse does there: `docs/Design/InputMap.md`, "Screens and how they are reached", the Mouse column.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.NewGame`: a click picks a body, a class or an element and takes the focus; a click off the cards changes nothing; a click on Begin is A on Begin.
