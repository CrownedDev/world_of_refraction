# PIE — B-484: loadout v2, Add gear window: the box follows the highlighted row

**Branch:** `feature/B-484-add-gear-box-follows-row` (in dev, built 2026-11-14).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.LoadoutV2` (a new case: one piece's own details for a weapon, an adapter and a crystal; a weapon alone reads the same as its one-weapon entry's box; nothing for nothing).
**Setup:** open the hub with a character who owns three or more weapons, and a crystal or stone in the pouch; console `wor.UI.LoadoutV2`.

| 2 | Move Up / Down (keyboard or gamepad) | The box changes to each highlighted weapon | [ ] |
| 3 | Move the mouse over other lines without clicking | The box shows the weapon under the mouse; move off the list and it goes back to the focused one | [ ] |
| 4 | Look at the EQUIPPED line | "EQUIPPED" sits at the right end of the row in its own space; the weapon type ("Axe") and the rest of the line are whole, nothing drawn over anything | [ ] |

| 6 | Open "Augment socket 1" on an entry that is already in | Each crystal's own box: its name and tier, element, what it is, its cells; a picked line shows "PICKED" at the right end | [ ] |
| 7 | X on a weapon line | Compare still shows under the list; X in the box's simple view shows fewer lines | [ ] |
| 8 | Weapons tab, two weapon entries | "ACTIVE" at the right end of the one in hand, as before | [ ] |
| 9 | Apparel tab, A on a piece | The box follows the highlighted or hovered piece there too | [ ] |
