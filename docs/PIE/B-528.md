# PIE — B-528: the quick strip and the field attack work in the hub (D-218)

**Branch:** `feature/B-528-hub-quick-strip` (in dev, built and tested on the desktop 2026-11-16).

**What it is:** D-218 says "it works in the hub too". The hub HUD now builds the quick strip, so holding R / RB opens it in the hub the same way it does in a trial. Before this, the hub opened nothing. The keys, the tap attack and the pick were already shared. The pick writes the piece in hand on the worn record, so the Equipment screen and the next fight agree. The strip above the party card stays trial only: the hub card is the name only (D-216). This is the old B-503y patch, rebuilt on the worn record.

**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.UI.HubHUD` (with the test world as the hub: hub cards, no card strip, and the quick strip is built and opens). Full suite in the session note.

| # | Do this | You should see | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the hub, with a weapon and an adapter worn, hold RB (or R on the keyboard) | The quick strip opens through the screen's middle, the world dimmed, the piece in hand tagged IN HAND. Same as in a trial | [ ] |
| 2 | Move along the pieces (left / right, or A / D and the mouse wheel), change a weapon's source (up / down, or W / S), then press A or click a tile | The strip closes. Nothing appears above the party card (the hub card is the name only) | [ ] |
| 3 | Tap RB (or left click) | The picked attack plays (a swing for a weapon, a pulse for a ring) | [ ] |
| 4 | Pick a different weapon on the strip, then open the Equipment screen | That weapon is the one in hand (ACTIVE) | [ ] |
| 5 | Close the Equipment screen and hold RB again | IN HAND is on the weapon you picked (a trial starts on the class kit, so check this in the hub) | [ ] |
| 6 | In the hub, open the strip and close it with B / Backspace | Nothing changes: the piece in hand stays | [ ] |
