# PIE — B-361b: teach a skill for Mentor Tokens (D-154)

**Branch:** `feature/B-361b-mentor-tokens` (in `dev`; built on the desktop 2026-11-04).
**What changed:** a new balance, **Mentor Tokens**, on each player's own wallet. It is earned only by playing together: every player gets 1 when a boss falls with two or more players in the game, and 1 more when a co-op run ends after its boss was beaten (Q-361b); solo play earns none. A player can **teach** a skill their character has learned to another player's character on the Give screen ("Teach it"): the learner gets its own copy, the teacher keeps theirs, and the teacher's player pays by the skill's tier (1 at F, one more per tier, so S costs 7). Save version 17: older saves are wiped with the version warning (D-138).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Currency.MentorTokens` (paid only when two play together; teach price by tier; a teach gives the learner a copy and spends the teacher's player's tokens; too few tokens or one player's own characters is refused with nothing spent; the tokens survive a save), plus the full suite.
**Setup:** PIE with **2 players, Listen Server**, as `docs/PIE/B-360.md` (the joiner has a seat with its own character). Both Output Logs open.
