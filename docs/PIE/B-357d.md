# PIE — B-357d: a gear evolution's passives act on an L2 action from its item

**Branch:** `feature/B-357d-gear-evolution-l2-passives` (built and specs green on the desktop 2026-11-09; in `dev`).
**Play change (D-147, Q-357b A, Q-357d):** an evolution on a weapon, an adapter or an apparel piece now gives its lasting passives (effects whose every payload is permanent) to an action charged to **L2** that its item powers, for that action only. The weapon in hand powers its abilities and anything infused from its socket; the adapter in use, anything infused from its socket; apparel, every action. Nothing at L0 / L1, nothing at fight start. Trigger-gated rows of a gear evolution stay off.
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Loadout.EvolutionPlacement` (11 cases): which gear powers an action; L1 gets nothing, L2 gets the powering items' evolutions, a broken one gives nothing; an L2 ability from an evolved weapon wears the passive during the action and loses it after; an evolution also on the character slot isn't added twice; an apparel evolution acts on any L2 action.
**Setup:** a Generic with an evolution (one with a lasting passive, e.g. a defence or damage buff) in its weapon's element socket. For check 4, an apparel piece with an evolution in its socket.
