# PIE — B-424: the light hit's air-burst dash

**Branch:** `feature/B-424-light-hit-airburst` (the worktree editor runs it; merged into `dev` after Crown's PIE and a final rebuild).
**Already proven by tests:** `WoR.Combat.ApproachWarp` (burst maths, striking spot, the camera rush timed to the burst and the cut past 3,500 cm/s), full suite green.
**What changed:** the starting sword and shield (`DA_Equipment_Generic_SAS`) now attacks with **Light Hit** (`DA_SAS_Attack_LightHit`): a forward dash whose hop is stretched into a quick burst, the `ComboAttack02_01` strike, then a hop back to the grid spot. The camera rush now waits for the hop and moves with it; when the burst is too fast to follow it holds and cuts instead.
**Setup:** use the editor already open from the B-424 worktree (`.claude/worktrees/unreal-writer-58223d`), open the Output Log, start a fight with the Generic character (any trial fight with at least two enemy rows). To go back to the old attack, set the weapon's Weapon Attack to `DA_SAS_Attack_F`.
