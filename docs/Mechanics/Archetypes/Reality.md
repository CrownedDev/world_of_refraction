# Reality

**Status:** Live. Player-facing reference.

## What it is

**Reality is not a class — it's an element-archetype:** a **Refractor whose innate element is
Reality**. It plays like any other Refractor (innate spellcaster, energy-based), but its element
is the odd one out — instead of a single colour of magic, Reality is the element of *everything
and nothing*.

Defensively, a Reality Refractor is **broadly resistant** — it shrugs off a little of every
element rather than hard-countering one. Its only soft spot is **Void**.

## Signature — the any-element caster

Where a Fire Refractor is locked to Fire and a Wind Refractor to Wind, **a Reality source can
cast spells of *any* element** — the usual "this spell's element must match your source" rule is
bypassed entirely. Reality is the universal key.

Reality spells (and [Generic spells](../GenericSpells.md) cast from a Reality source) are named
**"Reality [Name]"** — a Generic "Ball" from Reality is a **"Reality Ball"**.

## The anti-Broken-Darkness role

This is Reality's headline: **Reality is the hard counter to [Broken Darkness](BrokenDarkness.md).**

A Broken Darkness fighter grows by **absorbing** the elements of attacks they defend — every
element feeds them. **Reality is the one element they cannot absorb. It unmakes them instead.**

Two ways it lands:

- A **Reality crystal (Iolite)** used on a Broken Darkness character, or
- a Broken Darkness character **parrying or blocking a Reality attack**

…will **drain their absorption energy** *and* **reset their active pool back to base Darkness**.
They lose the element they'd stolen and snap back to black — cleansed and drained. They stay
Broken Darkness (Reality doesn't turn them back into a normal Refractor), but a dangerous built-up
pool is undone in one stroke.

So Reality is the disruptor against a snowballing Broken Darkness: you don't out-trade their
stolen element, you **erase it**.
