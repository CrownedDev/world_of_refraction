# Generic

**Status:** Live. Player-facing reference.

## What it is

The **Generic** is the **physical fighter** — the martial class that lives on weapons, not on
innate magic. **Dual-wields** two weapons, hits hard, and is the **most durable** archetype, in
exchange for being the **least flexible**.

## How it fights

- **Weapons first.** Two weapon slots make Generic the weapon specialist. **Weapon abilities** (the
  special moves bound to a weapon) come from the weapon, so any class holding a weapon gets them (D-027).
- **Loadout:** a **Primary** slot (a weapon, a ring, or an evolution) plus a **Secondary** slot
  (a second weapon). Two weapons is the signature dual-wield setup.

## Casting — borrowed from gear

A Generic has **no innate magic of their own** — they're not born with an element or a spell
school. **But they can still cast** — by borrowing magic from a **ring** or an **evolution** they
wear, which lends its element and its spells. A **weapon crystal never gives spells**: it makes the
weapon's abilities elemental (D-027, D-007).

- A Generic with a **Fire ring** casts **Fire** spells.
- A [Generic spell](../Magic/GenericSpells.md) (a basic "Ball"/"Bolt" template) slotted into that ring
  becomes that element — a Generic "Ball" on a Fire crystal is a **"Fire Ball"**.

So think of a Generic as a **weapon-first fighter who borrows magic from their gear**, not an
innate caster. Swap the crystal, swap the magic.

## Resource

Regular **EP** that regenerates over time (no special energy rules).

## Signature — the durable wall

Generic is the **tankiest archetype against physical attacks** (weapons, slashes, impacts) — and
in exchange, the **softest to magic**. That's the trade for raw durability: hard to cut down with
steel, easier to burn down with spells.
