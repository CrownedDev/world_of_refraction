# Refractor

**Status:** Live. Player-facing reference.

*(The Refractor is the class referred to as "Caster" in the code.)*

## What it is

The **Refractor** is the **innate elemental spellcaster** — one weapon and an **innate element
locked in at creation**. Where a Generic borrows magic from gear and a Resonator swaps it via
rings, a Refractor **is** their element, start to finish.

## The eight elements play the same

A Refractor can be any of **eight elements** — **Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Light, Darkness,
Lightning, Void** — and they are **mechanically uniform**: a **Fire Refractor plays like a Wind
Refractor**, just in a different colour. Picking an element only changes three things:

1. **What you resist** — each element shrugs off some attacks and takes others harder.
2. **The status your hits build toward** — every element fills the target's status bar toward a
   different effect:

   | Element | Builds toward |
   |---|---|
   | Fire | Damage-over-time (burn) |
   | Water | Heal-block |
   | Earth | Defense-down |
   | Wind | Skip-turn |
   | Lightning | Stun |
   | Light | Crit-down |
   | Darkness | Silence |
   | Void | Random debuff |
   | Reality | Burst damage |

3. **Colour** — the look of your spells.

Two elements are special enough to get their own pages: **[Reality](./Reality.md)** (the
any-element Refractor) and a **Darkness Refractor who breaks** under strain becomes
**[Broken Darkness](./BrokenDarkness.md)**.

## Spell pool

A Refractor's spells are bound by **two limits at once**: a **per-school spell count** *and* a
**point budget** — stronger (higher-tier) spells cost more of that budget, so you trade breadth
for power. **Mastery** (investing in your world stats) makes spells cheaper as you specialise,
widening your arsenal. The exact counts, budget, and discounts are in
[Spell Pools — Count & Budget](../Magic/SpellPoolBudget.md).

## Weather

Refractors **lead the weather.** A team's strongest Refractor sets the battlefield's weather to
**their element**, and its strength scales with that Refractor's **health** — a healthy Fire
leader bakes the field, a fading one lets it slip. (A Generic or Resonator leader has no innate
element, so they don't set meaningful weather.)
